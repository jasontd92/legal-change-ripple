LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT ("Lease") is entered into effective as of the 1st day of October, 2022 ("Effective Date"), by and between STEMMONS CROSSING RETAIL, LP, a Texas limited partnership ("Landlord"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Tenant"), whose address is 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, Texas 77041.

RECITALS

A. Landlord owns that certain real property commonly known as 2410 Stemmons Freeway, Suite 140, Dallas, Texas 75207, located within the Stemmons Crossing shopping center (the "Property").

B. Landlord desires to lease to Tenant, and Tenant desires to lease from Landlord, a portion of the Property consisting of approximately 7,400 rentable square feet, comprising approximately 2,100 square feet of retail showroom space and approximately 5,300 square feet of warehouse and operational space (the "Premises"), upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

C. The parties intend that this Lease shall be a triple net lease ("NNN Lease"), whereby Tenant shall be responsible for payment of its proportionate share of operating expenses, taxes, and insurance in addition to Base Rent.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1. Defined Terms. As used in this Lease, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:

A. "Lease" means this entire lease agreement, including all exhibits and amendments thereto.

B. "Premises" means the space described in Recital B, including all fixtures and equipment now or hereafter permanently affixed to the Premises, together with Tenant's non-exclusive right to use common areas of the Property as set forth herein.

C. "Lease Year" means each successive twelve (12) month period during the Term, commencing on the Effective Date.

D. "Base Rent" means the annual rent specified in Article 4, payable in equal monthly installments in advance.

E. "Operating Expenses" means all reasonable costs and expenses incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, repairing, and managing the Property, including but not limited to real estate taxes, insurance premiums, utilities for common areas, maintenance and landscaping services, parking lot repairs and striping, and common area lighting.

F. "Tenant's Proportionate Share" means seven and ninety-four one-hundredths percent (7.94%), representing the ratio of the Premises (7,400 square feet) to the total rentable area of the shopping center (approximately 93,200 square feet).

G. "Commencement Date" means October 1, 2022.

H. "Expiration Date" means February 1, 2028, unless sooner terminated pursuant to the provisions hereof.

ARTICLE 2: LEASE GRANT – PREMISES

2.1. Lease of Premises. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises, together with non-exclusive rights to use the Common Areas, upon the terms and conditions of this Lease.

2.2. Description of Premises. The Premises are located at 2410 Stemmons Freeway, Suite 140, Dallas, Texas 75207, in the Stemmons Crossing shopping center, and consist of approximately 7,400 rentable square feet, which includes approximately 2,100 square feet of ground floor retail showroom space with full storefront visibility from Stemmons Freeway, and approximately 5,300 square feet of warehouse, storage, and operational space for inventory and equipment. The Premises include two (2) designated loading dock spaces with overhead doors, one (1) grade-level employee entrance, and one (1) front customer entrance. The Premises are also equipped with existing plumbing and HVAC infrastructure suitable for Tenant's business operations.

2.3. Condition of Premises. Tenant acknowledges that it has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their present "as-is" condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Landlord shall deliver the Premises in broom-clean condition with all mechanical systems in good working order as of the Commencement Date. Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance, repairs, and alterations to the Premises during the Term.

2.4. Building Fixtures and Tenant's Property.

A. All fixtures, equipment, and other articles of personal property owned by Landlord and used in the operation of the Premises, whether or not attached or affixed to the Premises, together with all alterations and improvements which by the terms of this Lease become the property of Landlord, shall be and remain a part of the Premises and shall constitute the property of Landlord (collectively, "Building Fixtures").

B. Except as otherwise expressly provided by the terms of this Lease, all of Tenant's trade fixtures, equipment, machinery, tools, apparatus, and other personal property, whether or not affixed to the Premises, shall be and remain the personal property of Tenant and may be removed by Tenant upon the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease (collectively, "Tenant's Property").

ARTICLE 3: TERM

3.1. Initial Term. The Term of this Lease shall be for a period of sixty-four (64) months, commencing on October 1, 2022 (the "Commencement Date") and ending at 11:59 p.m. on February 1, 2028 (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated pursuant to the provisions of this Lease.

3.2. Renewal Option. Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew this Lease for an additional period of sixty (60) months ("Renewal Term") upon the same terms and conditions, except that Base Rent shall be adjusted to the then-current Fair Market Value rental rate for similar properties in the Dallas area as mutually agreed by Landlord and Tenant, or if the parties cannot agree, as determined by independent appraisal. To exercise this renewal option, Tenant must deliver written notice to Landlord not less than one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the Expiration Date. If Tenant fails to timely exercise this option, it shall be deemed waived.

3.3. Surrender of Premises. Upon expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, Tenant shall peacefully surrender the Premises to Landlord in good condition and repair, reasonable wear and tear excepted, and shall remove all of Tenant's Property. All alterations, additions, and improvements made by Tenant shall become the property of Landlord unless Landlord directs Tenant to remove them.

ARTICLE 4: BASE RENT AND ADDITIONAL CHARGES

4.1. Base Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord annual Base Rent in the amount of One Hundred Thirty-Six Thousand Nine Hundred Dollars ($136,900.00) per year, representing Eighteen Dollars and Fifty Cents ($18.50) per rentable square foot per year for the 7,400 square feet of the Premises. Base Rent shall be payable in equal monthly installments of Eleven Thousand Four Hundred Eight Dollars and Thirty-Three Cents ($11,408.33), in advance, on the first day of each calendar month during the Term, without demand, deduction, or offset.

4.2. Payment of Rent. All rent and other charges due under this Lease shall be payable to Landlord at such address as Landlord may from time to time designate in writing, or by electronic transfer to such account as Landlord may specify. Time is of the essence with respect to payment of rent. Rent not received within five (5) days after the due date shall be considered late and shall incur a late charge as set forth in Section 4.5.

4.3. Operating Expenses. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its Proportionate Share (7.94%) of all Operating Expenses incurred by Landlord in maintaining and operating the Property. Operating Expenses shall include, without limitation: (i) real property taxes and assessments; (ii) comprehensive general liability insurance and property insurance maintained by Landlord; (iii) costs of maintaining, repairing, and replacing the common parking areas, landscaping, and exterior lighting; (iv) costs of utilities serving common areas; (v) snow and ice removal; (vi) common area maintenance supplies and labor; and (vii) management fees not to exceed three percent (3%) of gross rental revenue. Operating Expenses shall not include capital improvements with a useful life of more than five (5) years, depreciation, or debt service on any mortgage or note.

4.4. Taxes and Insurance. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all property taxes attributable to Tenant's Proportionate Share of the Property and for Tenant's insurance obligations as set forth in Article 9. Landlord shall provide Tenant with annual statements of actual Operating Expenses and property taxes within sixty (60) days following the end of each Lease Year, together with supporting documentation. Tenant's payment of its Proportionate Share shall be due within thirty (30) days of such statement.

4.5. Late Charge and Default Interest. If any rent or other charges are not received by Landlord within five (5) business days of the due date, Tenant shall pay Landlord a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. In addition, any amounts remaining unpaid thirty (30) days after due date shall accrue interest at the rate of twelve percent (12%) per annum or the maximum rate permitted by Texas law, whichever is less, from the due date until paid.

4.6. No Abatement of Rent. Except as expressly provided herein, rent shall not be abated, diminished, or reduced by reason of any casualty, damage, condemnation, or for any other cause whatsoever, including any claims by Tenant.

ARTICLE 5: USE OF PREMISES

5.1. Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the conduct of a retail and wholesale plumbing and HVAC equipment business, including sales, service, installation, parts storage, and related office and administrative functions ("Permitted Use"). Tenant shall not use or occupy the Premises for any other purpose without the prior written consent of Landlord.

5.2. Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, statutes, ordinances, rules, regulations, and requirements relating to the Premises, including but not limited to building codes, zoning ordinances, environmental laws, and safety regulations. Tenant shall obtain all required licenses, permits, and certifications necessary to conduct its business.

5.3. Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall not use, generate, store, or permit the presence of any hazardous, toxic, or radioactive materials in or about the Premises except in commercially reasonable quantities necessary for normal business operations. Tenant shall comply with all environmental laws and regulations. Tenant shall maintain all such materials in properly labeled containers and shall not dispose of any such materials except through licensed waste disposal contractors. Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from any environmental contamination caused by Tenant's use of the Premises.

5.4. Conduct of Business. Tenant shall conduct its business in a professional manner consistent with the nature of the shopping center. Tenant shall not engage in any illegal activities, nor shall Tenant permit any nuisance, annoyance, or disturbance to other tenants or the public. Tenant shall not allow loud music, excessive noise, or objectionable odors to emanate from the Premises.

5.5. Signage. Tenant shall be permitted to maintain one (1) monument sign and one (1) storefront fascia sign, the design, size, materials, and location of which shall be subject to Landlord's prior written approval and shall comply with all applicable zoning ordinances and sign codes. All signage shall be professionally maintained and illuminated. Tenant shall remove all signage upon expiration or termination of this Lease.

ARTICLE 6: RADIUS RESTRICTION

6.1. Non-Competition. Tenant acknowledges that it will conduct retail and wholesale operations serving customers from a wide geographic area and that Landlord has a legitimate interest in maintaining the value and viability of the shopping center. Accordingly:

Radius Restriction. During the Term, Tenant shall not open or operate any other retail showroom for the display and sale of plumbing fixtures or HVAC equipment within three (3) miles of the Shopping Center, measured from the nearest boundary of the Shopping Center.

ARTICLE 7: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1. Tenant's Maintenance Obligations. Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, maintain the Premises in good condition and repair throughout the Term, including but not limited to:

A. Cleaning of the showroom, warehouse, and all interior spaces on a daily basis;

B. Maintenance and repair of all interior walls, floors, and ceilings;

C. Maintenance and repair of all interior plumbing fixtures, restrooms, and HVAC systems;

D. Cleaning and sealing of the parking and loading areas allocated to Tenant;

E. Maintenance of all doors, windows, locks, and hardware;

F. Maintenance of any equipment or machinery located within the Premises that belongs to Tenant; and

G. Pest control and extermination services as needed.

7.2. Landlord's Maintenance Obligations. Landlord shall be responsible for:

A. Structural integrity of the building, including roof, foundation, and exterior walls;

B. Parking lot surfaces and common area landscaping;

C. Common area lighting and utilities;

D. Maintenance of the shopping center common areas; and

E. Building-wide systems serving multiple tenants.

7.3. Emergency Repairs. If either party fails to perform required repairs and such failure creates an emergency condition (including but not limited to fire, flood, or safety hazard), the other party may make such emergency repairs and charge the responsible party for the costs incurred. The responsible party shall reimburse the other party within fifteen (15) days of presentation of invoice.

ARTICLE 8: COMMON AREAS

8.1. Definition. "Common Areas" means all areas of the shopping center not demised as exclusive space to individual tenants, including parking areas, loading and unloading areas, landscaped areas, common corridors, and exterior building facades.

8.2. Use and Maintenance. Tenant shall have the non-exclusive right to use the Common Areas for ordinary business purposes in common with other tenants and the public. Tenant shall not obstruct or interfere with the use of Common Areas by others. Tenant shall comply with all reasonable rules and regulations established by Landlord regarding use of the Common Areas.

8.3. Common Area Costs. Tenant shall pay its Proportionate Share (7.94%) of the annual costs of maintaining and operating the Common Areas, including but not limited to parking lot maintenance, landscaping, lighting, snow and ice removal, signage, and security. Landlord shall provide an annual accounting of such costs within sixty (60) days following the end of each Lease Year.

8.4. Parking. Tenant shall have the right to use four (4) designated parking spaces in the Common Areas parking lot for employee and customer parking. Tenant shall not use Common Area spaces for storage of merchandise, equipment, or vehicles. Tenant shall not permit vehicles to park in any space for more than twenty-four (24) consecutive hours without Landlord's consent.

ARTICLE 9: INSURANCE

9.1. Tenant's Insurance Obligations. Tenant shall obtain and maintain, at its sole cost and expense, the following insurance coverage during the entire Term:

A. Commercial General Liability insurance with a minimum limit of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per occurrence and Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) aggregate, covering bodily injury, property damage, and products liability;

B. Property insurance on all of Tenant's Property and Tenant's improvements to the Premises, with a minimum limit of coverage equal to one hundred percent (100%) of replacement value;

C. Workers' Compensation insurance as required by Texas law;

D. Employers Liability insurance with limits of not less than One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00) per accident;

E. Automobile Liability insurance with combined single limit coverage of at least Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00) per accident, covering all owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles used in connection with Tenant's business.

9.2. Insurance Requirements. All insurance policies required under this Article shall:

A. Be issued by insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best & Company;

B. Be primary and non-contributory as to any insurance maintained by Landlord;

C. Include waiver of subrogation in favor of Landlord;

D. Provide that coverage shall not be cancelled, materially modified, or allowed to lapse without thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Landlord;

E. Name Landlord and its lenders as additional insureds with respect to liability coverages.

9.3. Certificates of Insurance. Tenant shall provide Landlord with certificates of insurance evidencing all required coverage prior to the Commencement Date and annually thereafter, and upon each renewal of policies. Such certificates shall indicate that the policies are in full force and effect and shall confirm compliance with all requirements of this Article.

9.4. Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall maintain comprehensive general liability insurance covering the building structure and Landlord's Property. Such insurance shall be for Landlord's sole benefit and shall not affect Tenant's obligations hereunder.

ARTICLE 10: UTILITIES AND SERVICES

10.1. Responsibility for Utilities. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services provided to the Premises, including but not limited to:

A. Electricity and gas for the Premises;

B. Water and sewer services;

C. Trash collection and disposal;

D. Telephone and internet services;

E. Any specialized utilities or services required for Tenant's business operations.

10.2. Utility Arrangements. Tenant shall enter into direct contracts with utility providers for all utilities serving the Premises. Tenant's name shall appear on all utility accounts. Landlord shall cooperate with Tenant in arranging service connections.

10.3. Common Area Utilities. Landlord shall be responsible for utilities serving common areas, and Tenant's Proportionate Share of such costs shall be included in Operating Expenses.

ARTICLE 11: ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS

11.1. Tenant-Made Alterations. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Minor repairs and maintenance shall not require Landlord's consent, but alterations affecting the building structure, electrical systems, plumbing, or HVAC systems shall require Landlord's written approval.

11.2. Conditions for Alterations. Any alterations approved by Landlord shall be subject to the following conditions:

A. Tenant shall provide detailed plans and specifications prepared by a licensed architect or engineer;

B. All work shall be performed by licensed, insured contractors approved in writing by Landlord;

C. Tenant shall obtain all required building permits prior to commencing work;

D. All work shall comply with applicable building codes and zoning ordinances;

E. Tenant shall protect Landlord and other tenants from disruption and shall maintain clean, safe job sites;

F. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims arising from such work.

11.3. Ownership of Alterations. All alterations and improvements made to the Premises shall become the property of Landlord upon installation and shall remain in place upon expiration or termination of this Lease, unless Landlord directs Tenant to remove them and restore the Premises to their original condition.

ARTICLE 12: ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1. Restriction on Transfer. Tenant shall not assign, sublease, or otherwise transfer this Lease or any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion. Any assignment or transfer in violation of this Article shall be void.

12.2. Transfer of Tenant's Interest. If Tenant desires to assign this Lease or sublet the Premises, Tenant shall notify Landlord in writing and shall provide Landlord with financial information regarding the proposed assignee or subtenant, along with the proposed terms of assignment or sublease.

12.3. Landlord's Recapture Right. If Tenant requests Landlord's consent to an assignment or sublease, Landlord shall have the option to terminate this Lease and reclaim the Premises for its own use or for re-leasing to third parties. Landlord shall exercise this option, if at all, within fifteen (15) days of receipt of Tenant's request.

ARTICLE 13: DEFAULT

13.1. Tenant's Default. Tenant shall be in default under this Lease if any one or more of the following events occurs and remains uncured:

A. An order, judgment, or decree shall be entered by any court adjudicating Tenant bankrupt or insolvent, or approving a petition seeking reorganization of Tenant or appointing a receiver, trustee, or liquidator of Tenant or of all or a substantial part of its assets, if such order, judgment, or decree shall continue unstayed and in effect for any period of sixty (60) days;

B. Tenant shall file an answer admitting the material allegations of a petition filed against Tenant in any bankruptcy, reorganization, or insolvency proceeding or under any laws relating to the relief of debtors, readjustment of indebtedness, reorganization, arrangements, composition, or extension;

C. Tenant shall make any assignment for the benefit of creditors or shall apply for or consent to the appointment of a receiver, trustee, or liquidator of Tenant or any of the assets of Tenant;

D. Tenant shall file a voluntary petition in bankruptcy or shall admit in writing its inability to pay its debts as they come due, or shall file a petition or answer seeking reorganization or arrangement with creditors or take advantage of any insolvency law;

E. Tenant fails to pay Base Rent, operating expenses, property taxes, insurance charges, or any other monetary obligation under this Lease within five (5) days after receipt of written notice from Landlord that such payment is due and unpaid;

F. Tenant abandons the Premises or ceases to conduct its business operations from the Premises for a continuous period of thirty (30) days or more;

G. Tenant violates any non-monetary term, provision, or condition of this Lease and fails to cure such violation within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice from Landlord specifying the default, provided that if the nature of the default is such that it cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant shall not be in default if Tenant commences the cure within such thirty-day period and thereafter diligently prosecutes the same to completion within a reasonable time not to exceed ninety (90) days;

H. Tenant engages in any illegal activity or uses the Premises in violation of law;

I. Tenant fails to maintain required insurance coverage or fails to provide evidence of such coverage as required under Article 9;

J. Tenant fails to maintain the Premises in proper condition as required under Article 7.

13.2. Cure Period. Except as otherwise expressly provided, Tenant shall have fifteen (15) days following receipt of written notice of any monetary default to cure such default. For non-monetary defaults, Tenant shall have thirty (30) days following receipt of written notice, except that if the default cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant shall have an additional sixty (60) days provided Tenant commences the cure within the initial thirty-day period and diligently pursues such cure to completion.

ARTICLE 14: REMEDIES

14.1. Remedies for Default. Upon the occurrence of any Event of Default not cured within the applicable cure period, Landlord shall have the following remedies in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity:

A. Landlord may terminate this Lease by giving Tenant written notice of termination;

B. Landlord may reenter and repossess the Premises without legal process;

C. Landlord may remove all of Tenant's Property and store such property at Tenant's expense;

D. Landlord may perform any obligation of Tenant and charge the reasonable cost thereof to Tenant as additional rent;

E. Landlord may pursue any other remedy available under this Lease or under Texas law.

14.2. Damages for Holdover. If Tenant remains in possession of the Premises after expiration or termination of this Lease without Landlord's consent, Tenant shall pay Landlord daily rent at the rate of 150% of the Base Rent then in effect, plus all damages, costs, and attorney's fees incurred by Landlord in removing Tenant.

14.3. Acceleration of Rent. Upon any material default by Tenant, Landlord may declare all rent for the remainder of the Term immediately due and payable, without notice or demand.

14.4. Waiver of Right to Cure. If Tenant fails to pay rent or other charges when due and such failure continues for ten (10) days after Landlord provides notice, Tenant shall forfeit the right to cure such default if a second such failure occurs within a twelve (12) month period.

ARTICLE 15: INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

15.1. Tenant's Indemnification. Tenant shall indemnify, defend with counsel approved by Landlord, and hold harmless Landlord, Landlord's lenders, and Landlord's agents from and against all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, and expenses (including attorney's fees) arising out of or resulting from:

A. Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises;

B. Any breach or default by Tenant under this Lease;

C. Any injury to persons or damage to property occurring in or about the Premises caused by or arising from Tenant's operations;

D. Any environmental contamination caused by Tenant;

E. Any liens or encumbrances resulting from Tenant's failure to pay contractors or suppliers;

F. Any negligent or wrongful act or omission by Tenant.

15.2. Landlord's Limitation of Liability. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury to persons or damage to property in the Premises caused by Tenant, other tenants, or the public, except to the extent caused by Landlord's negligence or willful misconduct. Tenant's sole remedy for any claim against Landlord shall be recovery of actual damages, and Tenant waives any claims for consequential, punitive, or special damages.

ARTICLE 16: MISCELLANEOUS

16.1. Entire Agreement. This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant relating to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations. No modification or amendment of this Lease shall be valid unless in writing and signed by both Landlord and Tenant.

16.2. Severability. If any provision of this Lease is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be severed, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

16.3. Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Venue for any legal action shall be in Dallas County, Texas.

16.4. Authority. Each party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Lease and that the person executing this Lease on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.

16.5. Notices. All notices required or permitted under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by email with read receipt, or sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth below:

16.7. Quiet Enjoyment. Landlord covenants that Tenant, upon payment of all rent and performance of all obligations hereunder, shall have quiet enjoyment of the Premises during the Term without interference from Landlord or third parties claiming through Landlord.

16.8. Successors and Assigns. This Lease shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the successors and permitted assigns of both Landlord and Tenant.

16.9. Waiver. No waiver of any breach or default under this Lease shall be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach or default. Landlord's acceptance of partial rent shall not be deemed a waiver of default.

16.10. Attorneys' Fees. In any action or proceeding arising from this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing party.

16.11. Estoppel Certificate. Upon request by Landlord or a potential buyer or lender, Tenant shall execute and deliver to Landlord an estoppel certificate in a form reasonably approved by Landlord, certifying that this Lease is in full force and effect, the amount of rent paid, the amount of any defaults, and such other information as Landlord may reasonably request.

16.12. Environmental Compliance. Tenant shall comply with all environmental laws and regulations applicable to the Premises and shall promptly notify Landlord of any environmental issues discovered in or about the Premises.
