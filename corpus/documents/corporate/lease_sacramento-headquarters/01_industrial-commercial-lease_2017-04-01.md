LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT ("Lease") is entered into as of April 1, 2017 ("Effective Date"), by and between NATOMAS COMMERCE CENTER, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation d/b/a Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air ("Tenant").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of certain real property commonly known as 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Sacramento, California, more particularly described in Exhibit "A" attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference ("Property"); and

WHEREAS, Landlord desires to lease to Tenant, and Tenant desires to lease from Landlord, certain space within the Property, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

ARTICLE I – PREMISES

Section 1.1. Description of Premises. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, that certain space consisting of approximately 38,400 rentable square feet ("RSF"), commonly known as Building 4, located at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Sacramento, California 95834, which space consists of office space, warehouse space, and vehicle fleet yard area, as more particularly shown on the floor plan attached hereto as Exhibit "B" (collectively, the "Premises"). The Premises are part of a multi-tenant industrial and commercial complex managed by Landlord.

Section 1.2. Condition of Premises. Tenant acknowledges that Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts the same in "AS-IS" condition as of the Commencement Date, with the exception of those items to be completed by Landlord as set forth in Exhibit "C" (Landlord's Delivery Obligations). Landlord shall complete all items listed in Exhibit "C" prior to the Commencement Date, including but not limited to: (i) ensuring all building systems, including HVAC, heating, plumbing, electrical and fire detection systems are in proper working order; (ii) providing a clean, sanitary facility with all bathrooms sanitized; (iii) certifying that all code deficiencies have been remedied and obtaining a Certificate of Occupancy from the City of Sacramento; (iv) performing all repairs to exterior windows and doors necessary to create a watertight envelope; (v) ensuring all parking lot and exterior lighting is operational; and (vi) removing any hazardous materials that do not comply with Environmental Protection Agency guidelines.

Section 1.3. Commencement Date. The date upon which Tenant takes possession of the Premises and this Lease becomes effective for all purposes shall be April 1, 2017 (the "Commencement Date"), or if Landlord has not completed its delivery obligations by such date, the Commencement Date shall be extended until ten business days after Landlord's substantial completion of such obligations.

Section 1.4. Landlord's Representations. Landlord represents and warrants that Landlord has good and marketable title to the Property, that the Property is free and clear of all liens and encumbrances except as disclosed to Tenant in writing, and that Landlord has the full power and authority to enter into this Lease.

ARTICLE II – TERM

Section 2.1. Initial Term. The initial term of this Lease shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall expire at 11:59 p.m. on March 31, 2027 (the "Lease Term" or "Initial Term"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Lease. Tenant shall vacate the Premises and surrender possession to Landlord on or before the expiration of the Lease Term in the condition required by this Lease.

Section 2.2. Renewal and Extension. This Lease shall not be renewed or extended except as expressly provided in Article XII hereof. At the expiration of the Initial Term, unless Tenant has exercised the renewal option set forth in Article XII, this Lease shall terminate and Tenant shall surrender possession of the Premises to Landlord.

ARTICLE III – RENT

Section 3.1. Base Rent. Tenant agrees to pay to Landlord, monthly in advance without offset or deduction, base rent for the Lease Term ("Base Rent") in accordance with the following schedule, commencing on the Commencement Date and continuing on the first day of each calendar month thereafter during the Lease Term:

Year 1 (April 1, 2017 – March 31, 2018): $37,632.00 per month
Year 2 (April 1, 2018 – March 31, 2019): $38,761.00 per month
Year 3 (April 1, 2019 – March 31, 2020): $39,923.00 per month
Year 4 (April 1, 2020 – March 31, 2021): $41,121.00 per month
Year 5 (April 1, 2021 – March 31, 2022): $42,355.00 per month
Year 6 (April 1, 2022 – March 31, 2023): $43,626.00 per month
Year 7 (April 1, 2023 – March 31, 2024): $44,935.00 per month
Year 8 (April 1, 2024 – March 31, 2025): $46,283.00 per month
Year 9 (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2026): $47,672.00 per month
Year 10 (April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027): $49,102.00 per month

Base Rent is calculated at the rate of $0.98 per RSF per month, increasing by three percent (3%) annually on each anniversary of the Commencement Date. All Base Rent shall be payable in immediately available funds to such account as Landlord may designate in writing. Base Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a per diem basis.

Section 3.2. Additional Rent and Expenses. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay all additional rent and operating expenses as described in Article IV hereof, including but not limited to Tenant's proportionate share of property taxes, insurance, common area maintenance, repairs, and utilities (the "Additional Rent"). All Additional Rent shall be due and payable concurrently with Base Rent unless otherwise specified herein. The term "Rent," when used in this Lease, shall mean and include Base Rent and all Additional Rent.

Section 3.3. Application of Rent. Landlord shall have the right to apply any payments received from Tenant to any amounts due under this Lease in such order as Landlord determines in its sole discretion, including late fees, costs, or damages.

Section 3.4. Late Payment. If any installment of Rent is not received by Landlord by the fifth day of the month in which it is due, Tenant shall pay a late fee of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. In addition, if Rent remains unpaid for fifteen days after the due date, interest shall accrue on the unpaid balance at the maximum rate permitted by California law, not to exceed ten percent (10%) per annum.

ARTICLE IV – OPERATING EXPENSES

Section 4.1. Tenant's Proportionate Share. The Lease is on a triple net (NNN) basis. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of all operating expenses and costs incurred by Landlord in connection with the operation and maintenance of the Property, including but not limited to real property taxes, property insurance, common area maintenance, repairs, utilities for common areas, security services, landscaping, parking lot maintenance, and snow removal (collectively, "Operating Expenses").

Section 4.2. Tenant's Proportionate Share Defined. Tenant's proportionate share of Operating Expenses shall be thirty-two percent (32%), representing Tenant's occupancy of 38,400 RSF out of the total 120,000 RSF in the Property, or such percentage as may be adjusted if the total rentable square footage of the Property is modified.

Section 4.3. Estimation and Payment of Operating Expenses. Landlord shall provide Tenant with a written estimate of Operating Expenses for each calendar year. Tenant shall pay Tenant's proportionate share of such estimated Operating Expenses monthly in equal installments as part of the Additional Rent. Landlord shall provide Tenant with annual accounting of actual Operating Expenses and reconciliation by April 30 following the end of each calendar year. If actual Operating Expenses exceed Tenant's monthly payments, Tenant shall pay the difference within thirty days of receipt of the reconciliation statement. If Tenant's monthly payments exceed actual Operating Expenses, Tenant shall receive a credit against the next month's rent or Landlord shall refund the overpayment at Landlord's election.

Section 4.4. Exclusions from Operating Expenses. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Operating Expenses shall not include: (i) depreciation or amortization; (ii) income taxes or excess profit taxes of Landlord; (iii) interest on debt; (iv) leasing commissions or other acquisition costs; (v) capital improvements or replacements; (vi) repairs or maintenance for which Landlord receives reimbursement from insurance or from third parties; (vii) costs associated with evictions or collections from other tenants; (viii) costs incurred due to Landlord's violation of law or negligence; or (ix) costs related to the removal, remediation or cleanup of any hazardous substances that existed prior to the Commencement Date or were brought into the Property by Landlord or other tenants.

Section 4.5. Utilities. Tenant shall be responsible for and shall pay directly to the utility providers all charges for electricity, gas, water, sewer, telephone, and internet service that are separately metered or billed to the Premises. For utilities that are not separately metered, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share as part of Operating Expenses.

ARTICLE V – USE

Section 5.1. Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the purpose of operating offices, warehousing facilities, and a vehicle dispatch center for a plumbing and heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) contracting business, including the storage and maintenance of vehicles and equipment, and for such incidental uses as are customary and reasonable in connection with such business ("Permitted Use").

Section 5.2. Prohibited Uses. Tenant shall not use or permit the use of the Premises for any purpose other than the Permitted Use without the prior written consent of Landlord. Tenant shall not engage in any use that: (i) violates any law, statute, regulation or ordinance; (ii) creates a nuisance; (iii) involves the manufacture, storage or use of hazardous materials except as necessary for the operation of the Permitted Use and in compliance with all applicable laws; (iv) disturbs, annoys or interferes with the quiet enjoyment of other tenants in the Property; (v) creates smoke, fumes, odors or excessive noise; (vi) involves illegal activity; or (vii) is of a nature that would increase insurance premiums or create insurance liability.

Section 5.3. Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall at all times comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, statutes, ordinances, rules and regulations. Tenant shall obtain and maintain all licenses and permits necessary for the operation of the Permitted Use and shall provide copies to Landlord upon request.

Section 5.4. Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall not bring into the Premises, generate, use, store or permit the presence of any hazardous materials except in small quantities necessary for normal maintenance and operation of the Permitted Use, and only in compliance with all applicable environmental laws. Tenant shall comply with all regulations promulgated by the Environmental Protection Agency, the California Environmental Quality Act, and the State Water Resources Control Board. If Tenant's operations result in any environmental contamination, Tenant shall be responsible for all investigation, remediation and cleanup costs.

Section 5.5. Business Operations. Tenant shall operate the Premises in a professional, safe and orderly manner. The fleet yard shall be maintained in a neat and presentable condition and shall not contain any inoperable, abandoned or junk vehicles. Tenant shall not conduct any retail business or allow walk-in customers. All signage related to the Permitted Use shall comply with the requirements of this Lease and applicable zoning ordinances.

ARTICLE VI – MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

Section 6.1. Tenant's Obligations. Tenant shall, throughout the Lease Term, keep and maintain the Premises in good repair, condition and appearance, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Tenant's maintenance obligations shall include but not limited to: (i) interior painting and wall repair; (ii) interior carpeting and flooring maintenance and repair; (iii) all HVAC filters and routine maintenance; (iv) plumbing fixtures and drains; (v) maintenance of all interior walls, ceilings, and doors; (vi) cleaning and maintenance of the office and warehouse areas; and (vii) landscaping and grounds maintenance within the Premises boundary.

Section 6.2. Fleet Yard Maintenance. Tenant shall maintain the fleet yard portion of the Premises in a neat, clean and organized condition. All vehicles and equipment shall be parked in designated areas only. Tenant shall maintain all paved surfaces in good condition and shall repair any damage caused by Tenant's vehicles or operations. Tenant shall sweep and clean the fleet yard area weekly and shall promptly remove any debris, fluids, or materials that may accumulate.

Section 6.3. Landlord's Obligations. Landlord shall be responsible for: (i) structural repairs to the building; (ii) roof repair and maintenance; (iii) exterior wall repairs; (iv) foundation repairs; (v) common area maintenance and cleaning; (vi) parking lot repairs (except for damage caused by Tenant); (vii) exterior lighting; and (viii) the main electrical panel and plumbing trunk lines. Landlord shall maintain comprehensive property insurance and shall repair damage caused by fire, theft, earthquake or other insurable perils, except where such damage results from Tenant's negligence or willful misconduct.

Section 6.4. Emergency Repairs. If any emergency condition arises that endangers life or property, Tenant may perform emergency repairs without prior authorization from Landlord, but Tenant shall notify Landlord of such repairs within twenty-four hours. Tenant shall provide Landlord with copies of invoices and shall pay the cost, which shall be reimbursable as a credit against the next month's rent if Landlord approves the emergency repair.

Section 6.5. Alterations and Improvements. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural alterations. Tenant shall provide Landlord with detailed plans and specifications for any proposed alterations and shall require that any contractor maintain comprehensive general liability insurance. All alterations shall be made in compliance with applicable building codes and shall be performed by licensed contractors. Upon expiration of the Lease, Tenant shall remove all fixtures installed by Tenant and repair any damage caused by such removal, except for improvements that Landlord has approved as permanent additions.

Section 6.6. Warranty of Work. Tenant warrants that all maintenance and repairs performed by Tenant shall be completed in a professional and workmanlike manner in compliance with all applicable codes. Tenant shall provide Landlord with copies of all maintenance logs and repair invoices upon request.

ARTICLE VII – INSURANCE

Section 7.1. Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall obtain and maintain, at Tenant's sole cost and expense, the following insurance coverage throughout the Lease Term: (i) comprehensive general liability insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, covering bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury; (ii) property insurance on all of Tenant's personal property, fixtures, equipment and improvements within the Premises with replacement cost coverage; (iii) workers' compensation insurance as required by law in California; and (iv) commercial auto insurance on all vehicles owned or operated by Tenant, with minimum limits of $250,000 per occurrence for bodily injury and property damage.

Section 7.2. Insurance Requirements. All insurance policies shall: (i) be issued by insurers licensed to do business in California and rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best; (ii) name Landlord as an additional insured on the liability and property damage policies; (iii) contain a waiver of subrogation in favor of Landlord; (iv) provide that coverage shall not be canceled, materially altered or reduced without at least thirty days' prior written notice to Landlord; and (v) be primary insurance, not excess to any insurance carried by Landlord.

Section 7.3. Certificates of Insurance. Tenant shall provide Landlord with certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage within ten days of the Commencement Date and annually thereafter no later than thirty days before expiration of each policy. If Tenant fails to provide proof of required insurance, Landlord may, at Landlord's election, obtain such insurance at Tenant's expense.

Section 7.4. Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall maintain property insurance covering the building structure and roof, insuring the building against loss or damage by fire, extended coverage, and such other perils as Landlord deems appropriate. The cost of Landlord's property insurance shall be included in Operating Expenses. Landlord shall also maintain comprehensive general liability insurance on Landlord's operations.

Section 7.5. Waiver of Subrogation. Landlord and Tenant mutually waive all claims against each other for loss or damage to property insured or required to be insured under this Article, even if such loss would otherwise be recoverable from the other party. This waiver applies to all losses, including those arising from negligence, except for intentional misconduct or gross negligence.

Section 7.6. Indemnification. Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord, Landlord's agents, employees and contractors from and against all claims, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from: (i) Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises; (ii) any breach of this Lease by Tenant; (iii) any injury to persons or damage to property occurring in the Premises or arising from Tenant's operations; (iv) any environmental contamination caused by Tenant; or (v) Tenant's negligence or willful misconduct. This indemnity shall not apply to claims arising from Landlord's sole negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE VIII – ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Section 8.1. Prohibition on Assignment. Tenant shall not assign, transfer, sublet, pledge or encumber this Lease or the Premises, nor permit any other person or entity to occupy or use the Premises, without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any attempted assignment, subletting or transfer without consent shall be void and shall constitute a material default of this Lease. This restriction shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the heirs, executors, administrators, successors and assigns of Landlord and Tenant.

Section 8.2. Conditions for Consent. Landlord's consent to any assignment or subletting shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed, but may be conditioned upon: (i) the proposed assignee or subtenant providing Landlord with financial statements, references and background information satisfactory to Landlord; (ii) the proposed assignee demonstrating the financial capability to perform all obligations under this Lease; (iii) the continued use of the Premises for the Permitted Use; and (iv) the proposed assignee entering into a written assumption of the Lease or sublease agreement in form acceptable to Landlord.

Section 8.3. Profit Sharing. If Tenant assigns this Lease or subleases all or any portion of the Premises, Tenant shall pay to Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any profit received by Tenant as a result of such assignment or subletting, such profit to be calculated as the difference between the rent Tenant receives and the rent due under this Lease, plus all costs and expenses incurred by Tenant in connection with such assignment or subletting.

Section 8.4. Original Tenant Remains Liable. Notwithstanding any consent to assignment, Tenant shall remain liable for the performance of all obligations under this Lease. Landlord's acceptance of rent from an assignee shall not constitute a waiver of the original Tenant's liability.

Section 8.5. Corporate Transfers. Notwithstanding the foregoing restrictions, if Tenant is a corporation and stock in Tenant is traded on a public exchange, a transfer of such stock shall not constitute an assignment requiring Landlord's consent, provided that such transfer does not result in a change of control of more than fifty percent (50%) of Tenant's outstanding shares.

ARTICLE IX – DEFAULT

Section 9.1. Events of Default. Tenant shall be in material default under this Lease if Tenant: (i) fails to pay any Base Rent or Additional Rent when due and such failure continues for five days after written notice from Landlord; (ii) fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease and such failure continues for fifteen days after written notice from Landlord, or if the default cannot reasonably be cured within fifteen days, Tenant fails to commence such cure within fifteen days and diligently pursue it to completion within a reasonable time not to exceed forty-five days; (iii) abandons the Premises; (iv) becomes insolvent, bankrupt, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed; (v) uses the Premises for any purpose other than the Permitted Use; or (vi) violates any law or regulation that materially affects the Premises or Landlord's ability to collect rent or enforce this Lease.

Section 9.2. Notice of Default. Upon the occurrence of any event of default, Landlord shall provide Tenant with written notice specifying the nature of the default and the actions required to cure the default. The notice period for payment defaults shall be five days and for other defaults shall be fifteen days (or such longer period as required by law if the default cannot reasonably be cured within fifteen days), unless a shorter notice period is otherwise specified in this Lease.

Section 9.3. Landlord's Right to Cure. If Tenant fails to cure any default within the applicable notice period, Landlord shall have the right (but not the obligation) to cure such default at Tenant's expense. All costs and expenses incurred by Landlord in curing a default, including reasonable attorneys' fees, shall be due and payable by Tenant within ten days of invoice and shall bear interest at ten percent (10%) per annum.

Section 9.4. Right to Reentry. Upon the occurrence of a material default that is not cured within the applicable notice and cure period, Landlord shall have the right to reenter and repossess the Premises and all property therein, and to remove Tenant and all occupants, without further notice or demand. Landlord shall have the right to change all locks and to remove any fixtures, equipment or property belonging to Tenant. Tenant shall be liable for all costs of reentry and repossession.

Section 9.5. Eviction. In the event of a material default not cured within the notice and cure periods provided herein, Landlord shall have the right to pursue eviction proceedings against Tenant under the California Code of Civil Procedure. Tenant shall pay all costs and attorneys' fees incurred by Landlord in connection with any eviction proceeding.

Section 9.6. Landlord's Remedies. Upon default by Tenant, Landlord shall be entitled to pursue all remedies available under California law, including but not limited to: (i) termination of this Lease and reentry and repossession of the Premises; (ii) acceleration of all rent due for the remainder of the Lease Term; (iii) recovery of all rent due and all damages resulting from the default; (iv) recovery of attorneys' fees and costs; and (v) recovery of any costs incurred by Landlord in mitigating damages, including costs of re-leasing the Premises. Landlord's pursuit of one remedy shall not preclude pursuit of any other remedy.

Section 9.7. Damages Calculation. Upon termination of the Lease due to Tenant's default, Landlord's damages shall include all rent due through the end of the Lease Term, less the fair market rental value of the Premises for the remaining Lease Term. Tenant shall remain liable for all rent due through the end of the Lease Term or until the Premises are re-leased, whichever is sooner, less any rent received from a new tenant.

Section 9.8. Right to Terminate. Upon a material default that remains uncured for thirty days (or fifteen days for payment defaults), Landlord shall have the right to terminate this Lease by giving Tenant written notice of termination. Upon such termination, Tenant shall immediately surrender all possessions and vacate the Premises.

ARTICLE X – MAINTENANCE OF INSURANCE AND DEFAULT

Section 10.1. Cooperation. Tenant shall cooperate with Landlord and Landlord's insurance carriers in the event of any loss, damage or claim. Tenant shall provide all requested information and shall reasonably assist in the investigation and resolution of any claims.

Section 10.2. Survival. The obligations of Tenant under this Article shall survive the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease with respect to any claims arising during the Lease Term.

ARTICLE XI – ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

Section 11.1. Signage. Tenant shall not install or maintain any signage on the exterior of the Premises or on the roof without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any signage approved by Landlord shall comply with all applicable zoning ordinances and homeowner association restrictions. Upon termination of the Lease, Tenant shall remove all signage and repair any damage caused by such removal.

Section 11.2. Parking. Tenant shall have the non-exclusive right to use forty-five parking spaces in the lot adjacent to the Premises, at no additional cost beyond Operating Expenses. Tenant shall not obstruct parking areas or permit vehicles to be parked in violation of parking lot rules. Tenant's employees and customers shall comply with all posted parking regulations.

Section 11.3. Entry by Landlord. Landlord shall have the right to enter the Premises at reasonable times upon forty-eight hours' written notice (except in emergencies) to inspect the Premises, perform repairs or maintenance, show the Premises to prospective tenants or lenders, or post notices. Landlord's entry shall not unreasonably interfere with Tenant's business operations.

Section 11.4. Estoppel Certificates. Tenant shall execute and return estoppel certificates to Landlord or Landlord's lender within ten days of request, in form reasonably approved by Landlord, certifying the status of this Lease, the amount of rent paid, and absence of defaults.

Section 11.5. Compliance with Other Tenants. Tenant shall comply with the reasonable rules and regulations established by Landlord from time to time for the operation of the building and common areas, which rules shall be furnished to Tenant in writing and shall not materially increase Tenant's obligations or decrease Tenant's rights hereunder.

Section 11.6. Notices. All notices required under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by certified mail return receipt requested, or sent by nationally recognized overnight courier service. Notices to Landlord shall be addressed to Natomas Commerce Center, LLC, 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 100, Sacramento, CA 95834, Attention: Property Manager. Notices to Tenant shall be addressed to Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, CA 95833, Attention: Facility Manager. Either party may change its address by written notice to the other party.

Section 11.7. Subordination. This Lease shall be subordinate to all mortgages, deeds of trust, and other liens against the Property, and to the extensions and renewals thereof. Tenant shall execute subordination agreements in form reasonably requested by Landlord or any lender.

Section 11.8. Binding Effect. This Lease shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties hereto and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

ARTICLE XII – OPTION TO EXTEND

Section 12.1. Grant of Option. Provided that Tenant is not in material default under this Lease at the time of exercise or at the commencement of the renewal term, Tenant shall have the right to renew and extend the Lease Term for one additional period of five years (the "Renewal Term") commencing on April 1, 2027 and expiring on March 31, 2032.

Section 12.2. Exercise of Option. Tenant shall exercise the renewal option, if at all, by delivering written notice to Landlord not less than one hundred eighty days (180 days) and not more than two hundred ten days (210 days) prior to the expiration of the Initial Term. Time is of the essence with respect to Tenant's exercise of this option. If Tenant fails to timely exercise this option, the option shall expire and be of no further force or effect.

Section 12.3. Terms of Renewal. The Renewal Term shall be upon all the same terms, covenants and conditions as are contained in this Lease, except that: (i) Base Rent during the Renewal Term shall be determined by mutual written agreement of Landlord and Tenant. If the parties cannot agree on the renewal rent within sixty days after Tenant's exercise of the option, Base Rent for the Renewal Term shall be established by an independent appraiser selected by the American Arbitration Association, with the cost to be split equally between Landlord and Tenant. The appraiser's determination shall be binding on both parties; (ii) Tenant shall have no further option to renew beyond the Renewal Term unless expressly granted in a written amendment to this Lease; and (iii) all other terms and conditions of this Lease shall remain in effect.

Section 12.4. Condition of Premises. At the commencement of the Renewal Term, Tenant shall accept the Premises in their "as is" condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted, except that Landlord shall be responsible for all structural repairs and repairs to the roof and exterior of the building.

ARTICLE XIII – GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 13.1. Entire Agreement. This Lease, together with the exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, understandings and agreements, whether written or oral.

Section 13.2. Amendment. This Lease may not be amended, modified or supplemented except by written instrument signed by both Landlord and Tenant.

Section 13.3. Waiver. No waiver of any provision or condition of this Lease or any breach thereof shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom such waiver is sought to be enforced. Failure of Landlord to enforce any provision of this Lease shall not constitute a waiver of such provision or of Landlord's right to enforce the same.

Section 13.4. Severability. If any provision of this Lease is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.

Section 13.5. Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Venue for any action arising from this Lease shall be exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Sacramento County, California.

Section 13.6. Authority. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this Lease and that the person executing this Lease on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.

Section 13.7. Time of Essence. Time is of the essence in this Lease with respect to all dates and time periods specified herein.

Section 13.8. Surrender of Premises. Upon the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, Tenant shall immediately surrender the Premises in clean condition, with all debris and personal property removed, all walls and flooring cleaned or repaired, all equipment and fixtures installed by Tenant removed (unless otherwise approved by Landlord), and with all damage caused by Tenant's removal repaired. Tenant shall leave all keys and provide confirmation of the establishment of forwarding addresses.

Section 13.9. Interpretation. Headings and captions are for convenience only and do not affect the meaning of the provisions. The use of the word "including" means "including without limitation." Any reference to days means calendar days unless otherwise specified as business days.

Section 13.10. Quiet Enjoyment. Landlord covenants that if Tenant pays all rent and performs all obligations under this Lease, Tenant shall have quiet enjoyment of the Premises during the Lease Term, subject to the terms and conditions of this Lease and Landlord's reserved rights.

Section 13.11. Brokers. Tenant represents that it has not dealt with any real estate broker in connection with this Lease and that no commissions are due to any broker. If any broker makes a claim for a commission based on dealings with Tenant, Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from all claims.

Section 13.12. Attorneys' Fees. In the event of any action or proceeding arising from or relating to this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs from the non-prevailing party.
