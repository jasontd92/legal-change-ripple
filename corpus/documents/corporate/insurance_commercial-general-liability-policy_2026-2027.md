4. COVERAGES PROVIDED

This Commercial General Liability Insurance Policy provides coverage for bodily injury, property damage, personal and advertising injury, medical payments, and contractual liability arising out of the business operations of the named insured, subject to the limits, terms, conditions, and exclusions set forth herein.

5. LIMITS OF LIABILITY

The following Limits of Liability apply under this policy and shall not be exceeded regardless of the number of claims, insureds, or claimants:

5.1 Each Occurrence Limit: Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00)

5.2 General Aggregate Limit: Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000.00), including all bodily injury, property damage, and personal and advertising injury occurring during the policy period.

5.3 Products-Completed Operations Aggregate Limit: Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000.00), covering all such claims arising from products sold or distributed or work completed by the named insured during the policy period.

5.4 Per-Project Aggregate Limit: Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00), which applies to operations at individual project locations. This per-project aggregate shall be included within and not in addition to the general aggregate limit stated above.

All limits stated above are the maximum amounts that will be paid by Granite Shield Casualty Company for all claims arising during the policy period, regardless of whether such claims are reported during the policy period or thereafter.

6. PREMIUM AND DEDUCTIBLE

6.1 Annual Premium: Four Hundred Twelve Thousand Eight Hundred Sixty Dollars ($412,860.00), payable in full at the inception of this policy or as otherwise agreed in writing by the insurer.

6.2 Deductible: Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000.00) per occurrence. All deductibles are the sole responsibility of the named insured. The named insured shall retain all losses up to the full amount of the deductible, and the insurer shall not be liable for any portion of losses falling within the deductible amount.

7. DEFINITIONS

7.1 "Insured" means any person or organization qualifying as an insured under the terms of this policy, including the named insured and any additional insureds where coverage is provided.

7.2 "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in bodily injury, property damage, or personal and advertising injury.

7.3 "Business" means the business described in the application for insurance, specifically the contracting business involving the installation, repair, maintenance, and replacement of HVAC and plumbing systems.

7.4 "Project" means any single location or address where the named insured provides services during the policy period.

8. COVERAGE PROVISIONS

8.1 General Liability Coverage

Granite Shield Casualty Company shall pay on behalf of the insured all sums which the insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal and advertising injury to which this insurance applies, subject to the Limits of Liability and all terms and conditions of this policy.

8.2 Contractual Liability Coverage

This policy extends coverage to include liability assumed by the named insured under written contracts or agreements. Specifically, the following types of liability are covered:

a) Liability assumed under construction contracts requiring the named insured to provide general liability coverage;

b) Liability assumed under service agreements requiring the named insured to maintain insurance;

c) Liability arising from contractual provisions requiring the named insured to defend, indemnify, or hold harmless any third party;

d) Liability assumed under rental or lease agreements for equipment or facilities used by the named insured.

Contractual liability coverage does not apply to liability that would not be covered under this policy if such liability had not been assumed under contract.

8.3 Additional Insured Coverage

Where the named insured enters into a written contract that requires the named insured to provide additional insured coverage, such coverage shall be provided automatically without the need for a separate endorsement, subject to the limitations and conditions set forth in Section 10 below.

8.4 Defense Costs

The insurer shall provide a defense for the insured in any claim or lawsuit covered by this policy. Defense costs shall be paid in addition to any amounts paid for damages, medical payments, or other expenses covered under this policy, except that defense costs shall be included within the Limits of Liability with respect to any amount in excess of the available limits.

8.5 Medical Payments

This policy does not provide separate medical payments coverage. All covered medical expenses shall be subject to the same Limits of Liability as other covered damages.

ADDITIONAL INSURED

10. ADDITIONAL INSURED - OWNERS, LESSEES OR CONTRACTORS

10.1 Automatic Coverage

Additional Insured - Owners, Lessees or Contractors - Scheduled Person or Organization and Where Required by Written Contract. Any person or organization with whom the Named Insured has agreed in a written contract, executed prior to loss, to name as an additional insured is an insured, but only with respect to liability for bodily injury or property damage caused in whole or in part by the Named Insured's acts or omissions in the performance of ongoing operations or your work, and only to the extent required by that contract. Coverage is primary and noncontributory where the written contract so requires.

10.2 Scope of Additional Insured Status

Where an additional insured is covered under this policy, the following provisions shall apply:

a) The additional insured shall be afforded the same coverage and protection under this policy as is provided to the named insured, subject to all terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions;

b) Coverage shall apply only to liability arising out of the business operations of the named insured, not the operations of the additional insured;

c) The additional insured shall not be required to pay any deductible, but the named insured remains responsible for all deductible payments;

d) All Limits of Liability shall apply equally to the named insured and any additional insured;

e) No coverage is provided for the sole negligence or sole willful misconduct of the additional insured.

10.3 Automatic Application to Written Contracts

This additional insured coverage applies automatically to all written contracts, including but not limited to purchase orders, service agreements, lease agreements, and construction contracts, that require the named insured to maintain insurance or to provide additional insured coverage, without the need for:

a) Prior approval from Granite Shield Casualty Company;

b) Separate policy endorsements or amendments;

c) Issuance of certificates of insurance or formal notices;

d) Modification of this policy or payment of additional premium.

10.4 Contractual Requirements

For additional insured coverage to apply, the written contract must:

a) Require the named insured to carry general liability insurance;

b) Specifically identify the additional insured as requiring additional insured status;

c) Describe the nature and scope of the contract services;

d) Establish that the liability arises out of the named insured's performance under the contract;

e) Be in effect prior to or contemporaneous with the loss or occurrence giving rise to a claim.

10.5 Limitations on Additional Insured Coverage

Notwithstanding the provisions above, additional insured coverage shall not apply to:

a) Bodily injury, property damage, or personal and advertising injury arising solely out of the independent acts, omissions, sole negligence, or willful misconduct of the additional insured;

b) Liability assumed under a contract if such liability would not be covered under this policy if assumed by the named insured;

c) Operations conducted by the additional insured independent of and not related to the contract with the named insured;

d) Claims arising from the failure of the additional insured to maintain its own insurance coverage;

e) Any claims reported after the expiration of this policy or after termination of the underlying contract;

f) Liability arising from the violation of any statute, regulation, or ordinance by the additional insured;

g) Contractual liability that would otherwise be excluded under the terms of this policy.

10.6 Relationship to General Aggregate Limit

All claims paid on behalf of additional insureds shall be applied against and shall reduce the General Aggregate Limit of Liability, and once this limit is exhausted, no further coverage shall be available for any insured, including both named insureds and additional insureds.

10.7 Notice and Cooperation

Each additional insured shall be subject to all conditions of this policy, including but not limited to the duties and obligations set forth below:

a) Prompt notice of any claim, suit, or occurrence that may result in a claim;

b) Cooperation in the investigation and defense of any claim;

c) Adherence to all policy conditions and exclusions;

d) Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations;

e) Maintenance of all records and documentation related to the contract and services.

10.8 Effective Date

Additional insured status under this policy becomes effective on the later of: (a) the inception date of this policy; or (b) the date of execution of the written contract requiring such additional insured coverage.

11.7 Priority of Claims

11.8 Notice of Claims

Such notice shall include identification of all parties against whom subrogation rights would have been available prior to the waiver.

POLLUTION EXCLUSION

12. POLLUTION EXCLUSION - WITH HOSTILE FIRE EXCEPTION

12.1 Pollution Exclusion - General

This policy excludes coverage for liability arising out of the actual, alleged, threatened, or suspected release, discharge, emission, escape, dispersal, seepage, migration, percolation, leakage, spillage, or exposure to any solid, liquid, gaseous, or thermal irritant or contaminant, including but not limited to smoke, vapors, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemical compounds, petroleum products, hazardous waste, pollutants, or any other substance or material that may pose a threat to human health or the environment.

12.2 Application to All Forms of Pollution

The pollution exclusion applies to:

a) Sudden and accidental discharges of pollutants;

b) Gradual, continuous, or repeated releases of pollutants over time;

c) Exposure to pollutants at concentrations that do not immediately cause harm but may cause bodily injury or property damage;

d) Any combination of pollutants or contaminants;

e) Pollutants migrating through soil, groundwater, air, or other media.

12.3 Hostile Fire Exception

Notwithstanding the pollution exclusion set forth above, coverage is provided for liability arising out of a hostile fire, which is defined as a fire that escapes from where it was intended to be and causes bodily injury, property damage, or loss of use of property. Hostile fire coverage includes:

a) Damage caused by heat from the fire;

b) Damage caused by smoke from the fire;

c) Damage caused by soot, ash, or debris from the fire;

d) Damage caused by flames or radiation from the fire;

e) Damage resulting from efforts to extinguish the fire, including water damage and structural damage.

For purposes of this exception, a fire shall be deemed hostile if it results from the fault or negligence of any person, including the insured, or results from accident or chance, but shall not be considered hostile if it results from the sole willful misconduct of the named insured.

12.4 Cleanup and Remediation Costs

The pollution exclusion applies to all costs and expenses associated with cleanup, removal, remediation, treatment, restoration, or mitigation of property contaminated or threatened by pollutants, including but not limited to:

a) Environmental site assessments and investigations;

b) Removal or disposal of contaminated soil, groundwater, or materials;

c) Installation of treatment systems or containment structures;

d) Monitoring of environmental conditions;

e) Natural resource damage assessments;

f) Restoration of damaged ecosystems or vegetation.

12.5 Regulatory Compliance

The pollution exclusion applies regardless of whether the release or exposure to pollutants is mandated, authorized, or allowed by any statute, regulation, ordinance, or governmental order. No coverage is provided for liability arising from compliance with environmental laws or regulations.

12.6 Liability of Third Parties

The pollution exclusion applies to liability assumed by the named insured under contract or by operation of law for the pollution-related liability of other parties or entities, including property owners, adjacent landowners, and other contractors.

12.7 Survival of Exclusion

This pollution exclusion shall remain in effect for the entire policy period and shall not be waived, modified, or eliminated except by express written endorsement signed by an authorized representative of Granite Shield Casualty Company.

DESIGNATED OPERATIONS EXCLUSION

13. DESIGNATED OPERATIONS EXCLUSION - RESIDENTIAL NEW CONSTRUCTION

13.1 Exclusion of Residential New Construction Operations

This policy excludes coverage for liability arising out of or connected with operations involving the construction, renovation, or development of new residential buildings, dwellings, or housing units, except where the named insured is providing services exclusively as a mechanical contractor performing HVAC or plumbing work incidental to such construction.

13.2 Definition of Residential New Construction

For purposes of this exclusion, residential new construction means the construction of new buildings or structures designed, intended, or used primarily as dwelling units for human occupancy, including but not limited to:

a) Single-family detached homes;

b) Townhouses, row houses, and attached residential units;

c) Multi-family residential buildings (apartments, condominiums, etc.);

d) Manufactured or modular housing;

e) Accessory dwelling units;

f) Mixed-use buildings with residential components.

13.3 Included Operations

This exclusion does not apply to the following operations, which remain covered subject to all other terms and conditions of this policy:

a) Installation, replacement, repair, maintenance, or commissioning of HVAC systems, including heating systems, cooling systems, ventilation systems, and air handling equipment;

b) Installation, replacement, repair, maintenance, or commissioning of plumbing systems, including water supply lines, waste disposal systems, drain systems, and plumbing fixtures;

c) Installation of ductwork, piping, insulation, sealing materials, and other components integral to HVAC or plumbing systems;

d) Testing, balancing, adjusting, and startup of mechanical systems;

e) Providing general contracting or project management services for the installation of HVAC or plumbing systems;

f) Services provided to owners or general contractors for the installation of mechanical systems.

13.4 Specifically Excluded Operations

Coverage is excluded for liability arising from or connected with:

a) General construction management, site management, or construction supervision services;

b) Framing, roofing, siding, window installation, or other structural and exterior work;

c) Foundation work, excavation, grading, site preparation, or landscaping;

d) Electrical work, except for electrical connections and controls integral to HVAC or plumbing systems;

e) Drywall installation, painting, flooring, or other finishing work;

f) Any work for which the named insured is the general contractor, construction manager, or primary contracting entity;

g) Operations where the named insured is responsible for overall project completion or project scheduling;

h) Subcontracting services beyond the scope of HVAC and plumbing work.

13.5 Geographic Limitation

This designated operations exclusion applies only to operations conducted within the states of Washington, Illinois, and Texas. Operations in all other states and jurisdictions remain covered under this policy, subject to all other terms and conditions.

13.6 Existing Buildings Excluded from Exclusion

Notwithstanding the exclusion set forth above, coverage is provided for HVAC and plumbing operations involving existing residential buildings that are not undergoing new construction. This includes repair, maintenance, replacement, and upgrade work on mechanical systems in residential properties that were previously constructed and occupied.

13.7 Interaction with Other Exclusions

This exclusion shall be applied in conjunction with all other exclusions, limitations, and conditions of this policy. If a claim involves both excluded and non-excluded operations, coverage shall be provided only for that portion of the claim arising from the non-excluded operations.

13.8 No Waiver

This exclusion may not be waived, modified, or eliminated except by express written endorsement signed by an authorized representative of Granite Shield Casualty Company and only upon payment of any additional premium required.

POLICY CONDITIONS

14. GENERAL CONDITIONS AND PROVISIONS

14.1 Duties of the Insured in the Event of Loss or Claim

The insured shall:

a) Notify the insurer in writing of any occurrence, accident, injury, or incident that may result in a claim or lawsuit under this policy as soon as practicable, but in no event later than thirty (30) days after the insured becomes aware of such occurrence;

b) Provide the insurer with all information requested regarding the occurrence, including names and addresses of witnesses, involved parties, and claimants;

c) Preserve all evidence, photographs, documents, and records related to the loss or occurrence;

d) Cooperate fully with the insurer in the investigation, defense, and settlement of any claim or lawsuit;

e) Provide access to the insurer and its representatives for inspection of the loss site and examination of physical evidence;

f) Not admit liability, make settlement offers, or attempt to settle any claim without the written consent of the insurer;

g) Comply with all requests for information and documentation from the insurer;

h) Maintain accurate records of all payments, expenses, and communications related to the claim.

Granite Shield Casualty Company reserves the right to inspect and audit the named insured's operations, facilities, equipment, practices, and records at any time during the policy period and at reasonable intervals thereafter.
