INSURING AGREEMENT

1. COVERAGE

1.1 This policy is issued on an "excess" basis over the underlying insurance described in Schedule B (Schedule of Underlying Insurance) attached hereto and made a part of this policy.

1.2 The Company shall pay all sums which the Named Insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of:

(a) Bodily Injury or Property Damage to which this insurance applies, sustained by any person or organization;

(b) Personal and Advertising Injury committed in the course of the Named Insured's business operations;

occurring during the policy period and caused by an Occurrence, provided such sums exceed the limits of the underlying insurance policies described in Schedule B.

1.3 This policy follows the form and conditions of the underlying insurance to the extent those forms and conditions are consistent with the terms of this policy. In the event of any conflict between the terms of this policy and the terms of an underlying policy, the terms most favorable to the Named Insured shall apply.

1.4 With respect to claims-made coverages in the underlying policies, this policy shall apply to claims first reported to the Company (or any preceding insurer providing umbrella coverage for the Named Insured) during the policy period, provided the claim arises from an occurrence that happened on or after the inception date of the underlying claims-made coverage.

2. DEFENSE

2.1 The Company shall have no duty to defend the Named Insured under this policy. The Named Insured shall maintain defense through the underlying insurance carriers. The Company's obligation to pay applies only to the extent that defense costs are paid under and in accordance with the underlying insurance policies and only to the extent that such costs, together with indemnity payments, exceed the limits of underlying insurance.

2.2 If any underlying insurance carrier denies coverage or defense, or if the limits of underlying insurance are exhausted, the Company reserves the right to retain counsel of its choosing to defend the Named Insured, and the costs of such defense shall be part of the limit of liability.

3. FOLLOW-FORM ENDORSEMENTS

3.1 This policy shall automatically follow all endorsements, modifications, and amendments made to the underlying insurance policies during the policy period, provided such endorsements, modifications, or amendments do not broaden the coverage provided by this policy or reduce the Named Insured's retention requirements.

3.2 The Named Insured shall provide the Company with copies of all endorsements to underlying policies within thirty (30) days of issuance.

DEFINITIONS

1. "Bodily Injury" means bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability, shock, or death sustained by any person.

2. "Business" means the business described in the Declarations, including all related operations conducted by the Named Insured.

3. "Damages" means all damages, judgments, settlements, awards, attorney's fees, court costs, and defense costs as awarded by a court of competent jurisdiction or agreed to in a settlement.

4. "Insured" means the Named Insured and any entity qualifying as an insured under the underlying insurance policies, provided such entity is listed in Schedule A.

5. "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in Bodily Injury or Property Damage neither expected nor intended by the Insured.

6. "Personal and Advertising Injury" means injury arising out of oral or written publication of material that slanders or libels a person or organization; publication of a person's or organization's privacy; misappropriation of advertising ideas or style of doing business; infringement of copyright, slogan, or tradename; or false arrest, detention, or imprisonment.

7. "Policy Period" means the period from July 1, 2026 through July 1, 2027, including any extended reporting period endorsement.

8. "Products-Completed Operations" means all products manufactured, sold, handled, or distributed by the Named Insured, including any service thereof, and work that has been completed or abandoned.

9. "Property Damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including all resulting loss of use of that property.

10. "Retained Limit" means the amount of loss for which the Named Insured is responsible before this policy applies, as specified in the Declarations.

EXCLUSIONS

1. This policy does not apply to any Occurrence or claim:

1.1 Arising out of or caused by intentional acts or omissions by the Insured, or any criminal conduct by the Insured.

1.2 For which there is no underlying insurance in force at the time of the Occurrence, except as provided in Section 2.2 of the Insuring Agreement.

1.3 Arising out of bodily injury or property damage arising in whole or in part from:

(a) Asbestos or asbestos products, materials containing asbestos, or asbestos-containing waste;

(b) Lead or lead-containing products or materials;

(c) Mold, fungus, or any substance or condition that can cause or promote the growth of mold or fungus;

(d) Pollution conditions or environmental contamination; provided, however, that this exclusion shall not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising from the sudden and accidental discharge of heating oil or propane in quantities greater than five (5) gallons during the course of the Insured's business operations.

1.4 For employers liability insurance, any employee benefit obligations, workers compensation, unemployment compensation, disability benefits, or any employee statutory benefit.

1.5 For commercial auto coverage, any bodily injury or property damage arising from the operation of aircraft, watercraft, or vehicles not licensed for highway use.

1.6 Arising out of professional services, including architectural, engineering, legal, accounting, or medical services.

1.7 Arising out of war, invasion, hostilities, terrorist act, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, civil war, or any act or condition incident to any of the foregoing; including any loss of use or diminution in value resulting therefrom.

1.8 Arising out of contractual liability, except as provided by the underlying insurance policies.

1.9 Arising out of claims arising in the European Union or European Economic Area where data protection or privacy regulations impose obligations on the Insured.

1.10 Arising out of securities violations, antifraud statutes, or violations of the Securities Exchange Act.

1.11 For which coverage is barred by statute or public policy.

1.12 Arising out of the Insured's violation of any federal, state, or local environmental statute, regulation, or ordinance, except for violations arising from ordinary operations conducted in substantial compliance with applicable law.

1.13 Arising out of any bodily injury or property damage arising from exposure to nuclear material or reaction.

1.14 Arising out of loss or damage to the Insured's own work, the Insured's own products, or the Insured's own property, except as specifically covered by the underlying insurance.

GENERAL CONDITIONS

1. NOTICE OF OCCURRENCE

1.1 The Named Insured shall notify the Company of any Occurrence that may result in a claim under this policy within thirty (30) days of learning of such Occurrence. Notice should be sent to the Company at the address listed in the Declarations or as otherwise directed by the Company.

1.2 The Named Insured shall provide all details available regarding the Occurrence, including date, time, location, persons involved, witnesses, preliminary description of bodily injury or property damage, and any third-party contact information.

2. CANCELLATION AND NON-RENEWAL

2.1 The Named Insured may cancel this policy at any time by providing written notice to the Company and the broker of record.

2.2 The Company may cancel this policy only for non-payment of premium or material misrepresentation, upon thirty (30) days written notice sent by certified mail to the Named Insured and broker of record.

2.3 The Company may elect not to renew this policy upon thirty (30) days written notice.

2.4 If premium is not paid by the due date, the Company may cancel this policy upon ten (10) days notice.

2.5 Extended Reporting Period: If the Company cancels or does not renew this policy, the Named Insured shall have the right to purchase an extended reporting period endorsement providing thirty (30) days of additional coverage for claims-made coverages at a premium of fifteen percent (15%) of the annual premium.

3. ASSIGNMENT

3.1 This policy shall not be assigned, transferred, or disposed of without the written consent of the Company.

3.2 The Company consents to assignment of this policy to a successor entity in the case of merger, consolidation, or sale of substantially all assets of the Named Insured, provided the Company receives written notice within thirty (30) days of such transaction.

4. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

4.1 This policy, including all endorsements, schedules, and the application of the Named Insured, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No agent has authority to modify or waive any provision of this policy.

5. SEVERABILITY

5.1 Should any provision of this policy be found invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect the remaining provisions, which shall remain in full force and effect.

6. CONCEALMENT AND MISREPRESENTATION

6.1 This policy shall be void if the Named Insured has materially misrepresented any facts material to the acceptance of the risk or determination of premium.

7. PREMIUM AUDIT

7.1 The Company reserves the right to audit the Named Insured's records to verify premium basis during the policy period and for one year thereafter.

8. OTHER INSURANCE

8.1 This policy applies excess to any other insurance or self-insurance maintained by the Named Insured, including captive insurance arrangements.

9. SUBROGATION

9.1 In the event of payment under this policy, the Company shall be subrogated to all rights of recovery of the Named Insured against any third party responsible for such loss, to the extent of payment made.

10. DUTIES AFTER LOSS

10.1 The Named Insured shall:

(a) Preserve all evidence and provide cooperation to the Company and its counsel;

(b) Authorize medical examinations as reasonably requested;

(c) Provide written statement of facts regarding the claim;

(d) Assist in settlement and defense activities.
