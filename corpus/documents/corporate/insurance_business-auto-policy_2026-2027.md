DECLARATIONS

INSURER: Granite Shield Casualty Company, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business located at 4521 Commerce Drive, Charlotte, North Carolina 28205.

NAMED INSURED: Meridian Home Services Holdings, LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Georgia, with its principal place of business located at 2847 Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, Suite 400, Atlanta, Georgia 30341, and its subsidiaries including but not limited to Meridian Plumbing Solutions, Inc., Meridian HVAC Systems, LLC, and Meridian Service Operations, Inc., as identified in Schedule A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

POLICY PERIOD: This policy shall become effective at 12:01 a.m. on July 1, 2026, and shall expire at 12:01 a.m. on July 1, 2027, unless sooner cancelled or terminated in accordance with the provisions hereof.

COVERAGE PROVIDED:

Commercial Auto Liability: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit per occurrence, applying to bodily injury and property damage arising from the operation of covered autos.

Hired Auto Liability: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit per occurrence, applying to bodily injury and property damage arising from the operation of hired autos.

Non-Owned Auto Liability: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit per occurrence, applying to bodily injury and property damage arising from the operation of non-owned autos.

Physical Damage Coverage: Comprehensive and Collision coverage on all scheduled vehicles with a deductible of $2,500 per occurrence for each covered loss.

PREMIUM AND PAYMENT TERMS:

Annual Premium: $47,850.00

The annual premium shall be payable in full on or before the effective date of this policy, or alternatively, may be paid in twelve (12) equal monthly installments of $3,987.50, each installment due and payable on the first day of each calendar month, commencing on July 1, 2026. Failure to timely remit payment of any monthly installment shall result in the automatic cancellation of this policy effective upon the date such payment was due, unless the Insured has requested in writing and Insurer has approved a grace period extension. Premiums are earned upon a pro-rata daily basis. All premiums shall be remitted to: Granite Shield Casualty Company, Premium Accounting Department, 4521 Commerce Drive, Charlotte, North Carolina 28205.

SCHEDULED VEHICLES:

This policy covers one hundred ninety-two (192) service vans and light duty trucks as listed in the Schedule of Covered Autos attached hereto and made a part of this policy. All scheduled vehicles are subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions and limitations contained herein.

LOSS PAYEE:

Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Tennessee, with its principal place of business located at 1204 Industrial Park Drive, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, is named as Loss Payee on account of its interest as the lessor of the vehicles covered hereunder, pursuant to that certain Master Equipment Lease Agreement dated May 15, 2026, between Named Insured and Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC (the "Master Lease"). Loss payable clauses shall read as follows: "This insurance shall not be invalidated should loss occur whether or not recovery can be had against responsible third parties. As to loss payable provisions, loss shall be payable to Named Insured and Lessor jointly, and as required by the Master Lease, all loss proceeds shall be remitted to Lessor as agent for Named Insured."

ADDITIONAL INSURED - LESSOR:

Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC is endorsed hereon as an Additional Insured with respect to liability arising out of the operation of the scheduled vehicles under this policy. The coverage afforded to Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC as Additional Insured shall be primary and non-contributory with respect to any other insurance maintained by Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC. Named Insured shall maintain this policy of insurance and shall not permit cancellation, material modification, or non-renewal of this policy without providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC at the address set forth above.

DEFINITIONS

"Covered Auto" means any auto listed in the Schedule of Covered Autos and any substitute auto of similar type while temporarily replacing a scheduled auto.

"Hired Auto" means any automobile, other than those owned by Named Insured or any employee, that is leased, hired, rented, or borrowed by Named Insured for use in the business described herein. This includes all vehicles under the Master Lease with Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC.

"Non-Owned Auto" means any automobile, other than those owned by or hired to Named Insured, that is used by Named Insured's employees in connection with the business described herein with the consent of the owner thereof.

"Business" means the operation of a plumbing and heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) contracting business, including the performance of emergency repair services, routine maintenance, and installation services on residential and commercial properties.

"Lessor" means Summit Fleet Leasing, LLC, as lessor of the scheduled vehicles under the Master Lease.

CONDITIONS

1. INSURABLE INTEREST AND NOTICE OF LOSS: Named Insured shall immediately provide written notice to Insurer of any accident, claim, or loss that may result in coverage under this policy. Notice shall be provided within thirty (30) days of the occurrence, or as soon thereafter as practicable. All notices of loss shall be directed to: Granite Shield Casualty Company, Claims Department, 4521 Commerce Drive, Charlotte, North Carolina 28205, or by telephone to 1-800-555-0147.

2. MAINTENANCE OF VEHICLES: Named Insured shall maintain all scheduled vehicles in good mechanical condition and shall comply with all applicable vehicle inspection and maintenance requirements prescribed by the lessor, manufacturer, and state and local authorities. Failure to maintain vehicles in accordance with manufacturer specifications may result in denial of coverage for claims arising out of mechanical failure.

3. OPERATOR QUALIFICATIONS: All persons operating covered autos shall possess a valid driver's license appropriate for the class of vehicle being operated, shall comply with all traffic laws and regulations, and shall not have a disqualifying driving record. Named Insured shall maintain records of all drivers and shall ensure that no person with a suspended or revoked license operates any covered auto.

4. USE AND OPERATION: Covered autos shall be used solely for business purposes related to the plumbing and HVAC contracting operations described herein and shall not be used for commercial transportation, racing, speed contests, or any unlawful purpose. Named Insured shall not permit use of covered autos for ride-sharing services or transportation of hazardous materials without prior written approval from Insurer.

5. VEHICLE ADDITIONS OR DELETIONS: Named Insured shall notify Insurer within ten (10) business days of any addition to or deletion from the schedule of covered autos. The addition of any scheduled vehicle shall be effective upon receipt of written notice and payment of applicable premium adjustment. Deletion of a vehicle shall be effective upon the date specified by Named Insured.

6. CANCELLATION AND NON-RENEWAL: Insurer may cancel this policy by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to Named Insured and Lessor. Named Insured may cancel this policy by providing written notice to Insurer at least ten (10) days prior to the intended cancellation date. Insurer shall not cancel this policy for non-payment of premium without first providing fifteen (15) days' written notice of intention to cancel. Non-renewal of this policy shall be provided by Insurer to Named Insured and Lessor at least thirty (30) days prior to expiration.

7. OTHER INSURANCE: This policy shall be primary and non-contributory with respect to any other insurance maintained by Named Insured or Lessor.

8. ASSIGNMENT: Neither this policy nor any rights hereunder may be assigned by Named Insured without the prior written consent of Insurer.

EXCLUSIONS

This policy does not cover loss or damage arising out of:

(a) Operation of any auto while being transported on or by another auto;

(b) Rendering or failure to render any professional service by Named Insured or its employees;

(c) Violation of any traffic law or regulation, including but not limited to speeding, reckless driving, or driving under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances;

(d) Mechanical breakdown, wear and tear, or failure of auto parts, unless such failure results in an accident covered under this policy;

(e) Loss or damage due to war, civil unrest, or acts of terrorism;

(f) Loss or damage resulting from the use of the auto in any racing contest, speed contest, or test;

(g) Loss or damage to the auto while rented to or used by any person other than Named Insured without the prior written consent of Insurer.
