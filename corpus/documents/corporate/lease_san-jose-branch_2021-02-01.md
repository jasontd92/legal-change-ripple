LEASE

This Lease ("Lease") is entered into as of February 1, 2021 ("Effective Date"), by and between COYOTE CREEK BUSINESS PARK OWNER LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation duly qualified to do business in California and doing business as Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air ("Tenant").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord owns certain real property located at 1890 Lundy Avenue, San Jose, California 95131 (the "Property"); and

WHEREAS, Landlord is willing to lease to Tenant, and Tenant is willing to lease from Landlord, a portion of the Property consisting of approximately 11,800 rentable square feet ("Rentable Square Feet" or "RSF") on a single floor (the "Premises"), upon the terms and conditions contained in this Lease.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS

1.1 Defined Terms. As used in this Lease, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:

(a) "Base Rent" means the annual rental sum set forth in Section 4.1 of this Lease, payable in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month.

(b) "Lease Year" means each twelve-month period during the Term, with the first Lease Year commencing on the Effective Date and ending on January 31, 2022, and each subsequent Lease Year commencing on February 1 and ending on January 31 of the following calendar year.

(c) "Operating Expenses" means all costs and expenses incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, and managing the Property, including but not limited to property taxes, insurance, utilities for common areas, landscaping, maintenance of parking areas, and management fees, excluding, however, any costs of capital improvements that extend the useful life of the Property beyond its original useful life.

(d) "Alterations" means any modifications, additions, or improvements to the Premises by Tenant, whether or not they materially alter the Premises.

(e) "Hazardous Materials" means any substance, material, or waste that is now or hereafter regulated, classified, or otherwise identified as hazardous, toxic, radioactive, or otherwise regulated under any federal, state, or local law, statute, ordinance, regulation, or rule, including without limitation petroleum and petroleum products, asbestos, polychlorinated biphenyls, radon, and lead-based paint.

(f) "Tenant's Agents" means Tenant's employees, agents, contractors, and representatives.

2. PREMISES

2.1 Lease Grant. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises consisting of approximately 11,800 Rentable Square Feet located at 1890 Lundy Avenue, San Jose, California 95131, in the building commonly known as Coyote Creek Business Center. The Premises comprise a single contiguous industrial space on the ground floor of the building, including access to a loading dock and approximately twenty (20) dedicated parking spaces.

2.2 Condition of Premises. Tenant acknowledges that it has inspected the Premises and accepts the Premises in their current "as-is" condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Landlord makes no representations or warranties regarding the condition, fitness, or suitability of the Premises for Tenant's intended use, except as expressly set forth in this Lease.

2.3 Measurement of Rentable Square Feet. The Rentable Square Feet of the Premises has been determined by Landlord using industry-standard measurement methodology and shall be binding on Tenant for all purposes of this Lease, including the calculation of all rent and charges. The actual square footage is approximately 11,800 RSF; however, if a subsequent measurement by a mutually retained surveyor employed by both parties in an arbitration setting determines the Rentable Square Feet to differ by more than two percent (2%) from the stated amount, the parties shall equitably adjust Base Rent based on the corrected measurement for the remainder of the Term.

2.4 Parking. Tenant shall have the right to use the parking spaces designated by Landlord in areas common to the Property for vehicles of Tenant and its employees. Tenant shall ensure that all parking is conducted in compliance with posted parking regulations and that all vehicles display valid parking permits if required by Landlord.

3. TERM

3.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Lease ("Initial Term") shall be five (5) years commencing on February 1, 2021 ("Commencement Date") and ending at 11:59 p.m. on January 31, 2026 ("Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Lease.

3.2 Renewal Option. Tenant shall have the right to renew this Lease for one (1) additional term of three (3) years ("Renewal Term") under the same terms and conditions as are contained herein, except that the Base Rent during the Renewal Term shall be adjusted to fair market value for comparable industrial properties in the San Jose area as of the date of renewal, to be determined by agreement of the parties or, if the parties cannot agree, by appraisal. Time is of the essence with respect to Tenant's exercise of this renewal option, and Tenant must provide written notice of its election to renew to Landlord not later than one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the end of the Initial Term.

3.3 Exercise of Renewal Option. Tenant exercised the renewal option contained in Section 3.2 by written notice dated October 30, 2025, and such notice was received by Landlord on October 30, 2025. Accordingly, the Renewal Term shall commence on February 1, 2026 and shall end on January 31, 2029, unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Lease.

3.4 Holding Over. If Tenant shall continue to occupy the Premises after the expiration of the Term without execution of a new lease, Tenant shall become a tenant at sufferance, at a monthly rental rate equal to one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the average Base Rent for the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the expiration of the Term, and otherwise upon the terms contained in this Lease insofar as applicable. The commencement of holdover tenancy shall not constitute a renewal or extension of the Term.

3.5 Surrender of Premises. Upon expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises to Landlord in the condition required by Section 8.1 hereof, with all alterations, improvements, and additions made by Tenant during the Term either restored to their original condition or left in place as Landlord may direct, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

4. RENT AND CHARGES

4.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord as Base Rent for the Premises an annual amount equal to the Rentable Square Feet multiplied by Two Dollars and Five Cents ($2.05) per Rentable Square Foot per month, which equals Twenty-Four Thousand One Hundred Ninety Dollars ($24,190.00) per month, or Two Hundred Ninety Thousand Two Hundred Eighty Dollars ($290,280.00) per annum. Such Base Rent shall be payable in equal monthly installments of Twenty-Four Thousand One Hundred Ninety Dollars ($24,190.00), in advance, without demand, on the first day of each month during the Term, commencing on the Commencement Date and continuing through the last day of the Term.

4.2 Rent Payment Method. Base Rent and all other charges for which Tenant is liable under this Lease shall be paid to Landlord at such address as Landlord may specify in writing, or by such other method as Landlord may designate from time to time. All rental payments shall be made by check, wire transfer, or other method approved by Landlord, payable to the order of Coyote Creek Business Park Owner LLC.

4.3 Late Payment Charges. If any rental payment or other charge due from Tenant under this Lease is not received by Landlord within five (5) business days after the date due, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount, plus interest on such overdue amount at the rate of eight percent (8%) per annum or the maximum rate permitted by California law, whichever is lower, from the date the payment was due until paid. The acceptance of any late payment shall not constitute a waiver of Tenant's default or of Landlord's right to pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

4.4 Operating Expense Charges. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of Operating Expenses incurred by Landlord in operating and maintaining the Property. Tenant's proportionate share shall be calculated as a fraction, the numerator of which is the Rentable Square Feet of the Premises (11,800 RSF) and the denominator of which is the total Rentable Square Feet of all tenant-occupied space in the building (approximately 47,200 RSF), resulting in a proportionate share of approximately twenty-four point ninety-nine percent (24.99%).

4.4.1 Operating Expense Estimate. Landlord shall provide Tenant with a written estimate of Operating Expenses for each Lease Year on or before December 31 of the preceding Lease Year. Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of the estimated Operating Expenses in equal monthly installments, together with Base Rent. If actual Operating Expenses exceed the estimate, Tenant shall pay the difference within thirty (30) days of invoice. If estimated Operating Expenses exceed actual Operating Expenses, Landlord shall credit the excess against future rent due from Tenant.

4.4.2 Operating Expense Exclusions. Operating Expenses shall not include: (i) any costs incurred in making structural repairs or capital improvements that extend the useful life of the building beyond its original intended useful life; (ii) any costs incurred in repairing, replacing, or restoring damaged portions of the building caused by Landlord's negligence or by fire, flood, or other casualty; (iii) any costs incurred in connection with litigation between Landlord and Tenant or between Landlord and other tenants; (iv) any costs incurred in removing, abating, or remediating Hazardous Materials unless such removal or abatement is required by law as a result of Tenant's use of the Premises; (v) any costs for space that is vacant or unrented; or (vi) any lease commissions or marketing costs associated with leasing space in the building.

4.5 Operating Expense Reconciliation. Within one hundred twenty (120) days following the close of each Lease Year, Landlord shall provide Tenant with a statement reconciling all Operating Expense charges paid during such Lease Year with the actual Operating Expenses incurred. If Tenant's share of actual Operating Expenses exceeds the amounts paid during the Lease Year, Tenant shall pay the difference within thirty (30) days of receipt of such statement. If the amounts paid exceed actual Operating Expenses, Landlord shall credit the excess against Tenant's next installments of Operating Expense charges.

5. UTILITIES

5.1 Utilities. Tenant shall pay all charges for water, sewer, gas, electricity, and all other utilities consumed in or furnished to the Premises. Tenant shall arrange for all utilities to be separately metered to the Premises if separate metering is available and reasonably feasible, and Tenant shall pay all utility charges directly to the respective utility companies.

Utilities. Tenant shall pay for all electricity, natural gas and water supplied to the Premises at the rates set forth in the applicable utility provider's filed tariff, as amended from time to time by the California Public Utilities Commission.

5.2 Trash Collection. Tenant shall arrange for and pay all costs of trash and refuse collection and disposal for materials generated by Tenant's operations in the Premises. All trash collection shall be conducted in compliance with applicable waste management regulations and city ordinances.

6. USE OF PREMISES

6.1 Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the operation of a commercial plumbing, heating, and air conditioning contracting business, including office, warehouse, storage, and equipment maintenance, and for no other purpose whatsoever without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or conditioned.

6.2 General Requirements. Tenant's use of the Premises shall at all times be conducted in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, statutes, ordinances, and regulations. Tenant shall not commit or permit any waste, nuisance, or other activity that would impair the value of the Premises or the use and enjoyment thereof by other tenants in the building.

6.3 Prohibited Uses. Without limiting the foregoing, Tenant shall not:

(a) Use the Premises for any manufacturing process except as incidental to Tenant's contracting business;

(b) Create, manufacture, store, or use any Hazardous Materials on the Premises except for normal quantities of common cleaning supplies, refrigerants, oils, solvents, and fuels necessary and customary for Tenant's plumbing and HVAC business, which shall be stored and handled in accordance with all applicable laws and in approved, properly labeled containers;

(c) Operate any machinery or equipment that produces excessive noise, vibration, or odor that would substantially interfere with the use and enjoyment of neighboring spaces;

(d) Cause or permit any condition on the Premises that would substantially interfere with the use and enjoyment of neighboring tenant spaces;

(e) Use the loading dock except for purposes related to Tenant's plumbing and HVAC contracting business;

(f) Permit more than twelve (12) full-time employees to work in the Premises at any one time without Landlord's prior written consent; or

(g) Allow the parking areas or loading dock to be used for storage of materials or equipment.

6.4 Hazardous Materials Compliance. Tenant shall be responsible for the lawful and safe handling, storage, use, transportation, and disposal of all Hazardous Materials brought onto the Premises by Tenant or its agents. Tenant shall comply strictly with all applicable environmental laws, regulations, and industry standards in the handling of Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall not knowingly allow the release, discharge, or disposal of any Hazardous Materials onto the Premises, groundwater, or surrounding property in violation of law. Upon Landlord's request, Tenant shall provide documentation of proper disposal of all Hazardous Materials.

6.5 Signage. Tenant shall have the right to maintain one (1) two-sided monument sign or building sign identifying its business, subject to Landlord's prior written approval of the design, size, materials, lighting, and location of such sign, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. Tenant shall be responsible for all costs of fabrication, installation, maintenance, insurance, and removal of any signage. Tenant shall remove all signage and repair any damage upon expiration or earlier termination of this Lease.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 Tenant's Maintenance Obligations. Tenant shall, at Tenant's sole cost and expense, keep and maintain the Premises and all equipment, fixtures, and systems therein (including HVAC systems, plumbing, electrical systems, lighting, and flooring) in clean, safe, good working order and condition. Tenant shall make all necessary repairs and replacements required to maintain the Premises in such condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty loss excepted. Tenant shall maintain the Premises in a neat, orderly manner, free from any accumulation of debris, hazardous conditions, or items not necessary to Tenant's operations.

7.2 Landlord's Maintenance. Landlord shall be responsible for maintenance and repair of the following building systems: (a) the foundation and structural elements of the building; (b) the roof membrane and roof structure; (c) the exterior walls; (d) the parking areas and common areas; (e) the Common Area HVAC system serving the common areas of the building; and (f) all building systems serving multiple tenant spaces. Landlord shall maintain casualty and liability insurance on the building structure as set forth in Section 8 hereof.

7.3 Repairs by Tenant. If Tenant fails to perform any maintenance or repair obligation required by this Lease within ten (10) business days after written notice from Landlord (or within three (3) business days if the failure creates an emergency or hazardous condition), Landlord may perform such maintenance or repair on behalf of Tenant and charge all costs, including reasonable administrative overhead of ten percent (10%), to Tenant as additional rent due within thirty (30) days of invoice.

7.4 Alterations by Tenant. Landlord acknowledges that Tenant will need to make certain Alterations to the Premises to accommodate Tenant's plumbing and HVAC operations, which Alterations may include installation of specialized equipment, reconfiguration of ductwork and venting systems, electrical upgrades to support Tenant's equipment, securing of racking to floor slabs, and installation of water lines and drainage systems. Tenant shall submit plans and specifications for any proposed Alterations to Landlord for approval prior to commencing work. Landlord's consent to Alterations shall not be unreasonably withheld, provided that Tenant shall comply with all procedures set forth in Section 7.5 hereof.

7.5 Alteration Procedures. Any Alteration by Tenant hereunder shall be done in a good and workmanlike manner in compliance with any applicable governmental laws, statutes, ordinances, and regulations, and all building codes. Before commencing any Alterations: (a) detailed plans and specifications therefor, prepared by a licensed architect or engineer, shall be submitted to and approved by Landlord, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed; (b) Tenant shall furnish to Landlord a certified cost estimate of the proposed work, prepared and certified by the architect or engineer who prepared the plans and specifications; (c) copies of all contracts and subcontracts for the proposed work shall be submitted to and approved by Landlord; (d) Tenant shall provide Landlord with a performance and payment bond from a surety company acceptable to Landlord in an amount equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the estimated cost for any Alteration exceeding Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000.00); (e) Tenant shall provide evidence of workers' compensation insurance and comprehensive general liability insurance as required by Section 8 hereof; and (f) Tenant shall obtain all necessary permits and licenses from governmental authorities before commencing work.

7.6 Permanent Fixtures. Any Alterations or improvements that constitute permanent additions to or modifications of the Premises shall become the property of Landlord and shall remain in the Premises upon expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, unless Landlord directs Tenant to remove such Alterations. Tenant may request that certain Alterations be removable at the end of the Term, and Landlord may, in its sole discretion, consent to removal, in which case Tenant shall remove such Alterations and repair all damage caused by such removal.

8. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall maintain, throughout the Term of this Lease, at Tenant's sole cost and expense, the following insurance coverage with insurers rated A-:VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) Commercial General Liability Insurance covering bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury, with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) per occurrence and Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) annual aggregate;

(b) Property Insurance on all Tenant's personal property, equipment, fixtures, and Alterations made by Tenant in the Premises, with limits sufficient to cover full replacement value;

(c) Workers' Compensation Insurance as required by California law, with statutory limits for bodily injury by disease, each accident, and annual aggregate, and with employer's liability limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per occurrence;

(d) Automobile Liability Insurance covering all vehicles owned, leased, or operated by Tenant, with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) combined single limit per occurrence;

(e) Landlord's Waiver of Subrogation in Tenant's General Liability and Property policies, providing that such insurance shall be primary and non-contributory and shall waive any right of subrogation against Landlord.

8.2 Insurance Certificates. Tenant shall provide Landlord with certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage within ten (10) days after the Effective Date and thereafter within thirty (30) days before expiration of each policy. All policies shall contain endorsements providing that Landlord shall be named as an additional insured with respect to liability policies and as a loss payee with respect to property policies. Tenant shall notify Landlord within five (5) business days of any cancellation, material modification, or expiration of any required insurance policy.

8.3 Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall maintain insurance on the building structure, including roof, foundation, and exterior walls, and general liability insurance covering Landlord's operations, maintenance, and management of common areas.

8.4 Indemnification by Tenant. Tenant shall indemnify, defend (with counsel selected by Tenant and reasonably acceptable to Landlord), and hold harmless Landlord, Landlord's agents, employees, and representatives from and against any and all claims, demands, causes of action, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises; (b) any breach or default by Tenant under this Lease; (c) any injury to persons or damage to property occurring in or about the Premises caused by Tenant or Tenant's Agents; (d) any violation of law by Tenant; or (e) Tenant's handling or use of Hazardous Materials. This indemnity shall not apply to the extent that claims arise solely from Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8.5 Landlord's Indemnification. Landlord shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Tenant from and against any claims arising out of Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct in maintaining the building structure or common areas, or any bodily injury or property damage to third parties caused solely by Landlord's acts or omissions.

9. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

9.1 Events of Default. Tenant shall be in default of this Lease if any of the following events occur:

(a) Failure to Pay Rent: If Tenant fails to pay Base Rent, Operating Expense charges, or any other monetary charges due under this Lease within five (5) business days after written notice from Landlord, or if Tenant fails to pay such charges by the due date on two (2) separate occasions in any twelve (12) month period;

(b) Failure to Perform: If Tenant fails to comply with any non-monetary term, condition, or covenant of this Lease and does not cure such failure within fifteen (15) days after written notice from Landlord specifying the default (or within thirty (30) days if the default requires structural repairs or alterations that cannot reasonably be completed within fifteen (15) days, provided that Tenant shall have commenced such cure within such fifteen (15) day period and thereafter diligently prosecutes such cure to completion);

(c) Abandonment: If Tenant abandons the Premises for thirty (30) consecutive days without paying rent or without notifying Landlord of Tenant's intention to maintain the Lease;

(d) Insolvency: If Tenant becomes insolvent, bankrupt, or subject to receivership, or if there is a levy or attachment of Tenant's property;

(e) Assignment in Violation of Lease: If Tenant assigns this Lease or sublets the Premises in violation of Section 10 hereof; or

(f) Repetitive Defaults: If Tenant commits three (3) or more non-monetary defaults in any twelve (12) month period.

9.2 Right to Cure. If Tenant is in monetary default and fails to cure within five (5) business days after notice, or if Tenant is in non-monetary default and fails to cure within the applicable cure period specified in Section 9.1, Landlord shall have the right to perform such obligation on behalf of Tenant and recover all costs as additional rent.

9.3 Termination for Default. If Tenant fails to cure any default within the applicable cure period, Landlord shall have the right to terminate this Lease by delivering written notice to Tenant specifying the default and stating that the Lease will terminate on a date not less than thirty (30) days after delivery of such notice. Upon such termination, Landlord shall have the right to re-enter and repossess the Premises and remove all persons and property, without further notice and without liability for damages, and to relet the Premises to third parties.

9.4 Remedies Cumulative. Landlord's remedies under this Lease are cumulative and not exclusive. Exercise of any remedy shall not preclude the exercise of any other remedy or remedies available at law or in equity.

9.5 Attorneys' Fees. If either party brings an action to enforce the terms of this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs from the non-prevailing party.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

10.1 Restriction on Assignment. Tenant shall not assign, transfer, pledge, or hypothecate this Lease or any interest therein, nor sublet the Premises or any portion thereof, without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or conditioned. Any attempted assignment or subletting in violation of this provision shall be void and shall constitute a material breach of this Lease.

10.2 Conditions to Consent. If Tenant seeks Landlord's consent to an assignment or subletting, Tenant shall provide Landlord with: (a) the name, address, and financial statements of the proposed assignee or subtenant; (b) a copy of the proposed assignment or sublease agreement; (c) evidence of the financial ability of the proposed assignee or subtenant to perform all obligations under this Lease; and (d) a statement explaining the business reasons for the assignment or subletting.

10.3 Profit Sharing. If Tenant assigns this Lease or sublets the Premises for a rental rate or other consideration exceeding that payable by Tenant under this Lease, Tenant shall pay to Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any such excess or profit as additional rent within fifteen (15) days of Tenant's receipt of such payment from the assignee or subtenant.

11. ENVIRONMENTAL AND HAZARDOUS MATERIALS

11.1 Environmental Compliance. Tenant shall comply with all environmental laws, regulations, and ordinances relating to the Premises and Tenant's operations, including the California Environmental Quality Act, the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation and Liability Act, the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, and the Clean Water Act.

11.2 Tenant's Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall be solely responsible for any environmental contamination caused by Tenant's use, handling, storage, or disposal of Hazardous Materials in violation of law. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any spill, release, or discharge of Hazardous Materials and shall immediately take measures to contain and remediate such release in compliance with law.

11.3 Phase I Environmental Assessment. Prior to the Commencement Date, Landlord provided Tenant with a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment dated January 15, 2021, prepared by Environmental Services Group, Inc. Tenant acknowledges having received and reviewed such assessment and accepts the Premises subject to the conditions disclosed therein.

12. LANDLORD'S ACCESS AND ENTRY

12.1 Right to Enter. Landlord and Landlord's agents, contractors, and representatives shall have the right to enter the Premises at reasonable times and upon reasonable notice for the purposes of: (a) inspecting the Premises; (b) maintaining or repairing the building structure or common area systems; (c) showing the Premises to prospective tenants, lenders, or purchasers during the final ninety (90) days of the Term; or (d) addressing any emergency or hazardous condition. Except in emergencies, Landlord shall provide Tenant with at least forty-eight (48) hours' written or verbal notice before entry.

12.2 Emergency Entry. In case of emergency (including fire, flood, explosion, imminent danger to persons or property, or other urgent circumstances), Landlord may enter the Premises without notice and may take all actions necessary to protect the Premises and surrounding property or to prevent injury to persons.

13. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

13.1 Casualty Loss. If the Premises are damaged by fire, explosion, or other casualty, the rent shall abate proportionately with the extent of damage that makes the Premises unusable for Tenant's business. Landlord shall promptly repair the Premises. If the building is damaged to the extent that more than twenty-five percent (25%) of the Rentable Square Feet of the entire building is rendered untenantable, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice to the other within thirty (30) days after the date of such casualty.

13.2 Condemnation. If any material portion of the Premises or the building access or parking areas are taken by eminent domain or threatened with taking, either party may terminate this Lease. If less than ten percent (10%) of the Rentable Square Feet of the Premises is taken, Tenant shall continue to pay full Base Rent.

14. GENERAL PROVISIONS

14.1 Term of Lease. This Lease is entered into for the periods specified in Section 3 and shall continue thereafter only upon renewal pursuant to Section 3.2 or by written agreement of the parties.

14.2 Notice. Any notice required or permitted under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by certified or registered mail, return receipt requested, sent by reputable overnight courier service, or sent by email with read receipt requested. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to Coyote Creek Business Park Owner LLC, 3850 Lenoir Street, San Diego, California 92110, or such other address as Landlord may designate in writing. Notices to Tenant shall be sent to Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, California 95833, or such other address as Tenant may designate in writing.

14.3 Severability. If any provision of this Lease is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable, or in violation of law, such provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or if it cannot be reformed, such provision shall be severed from this Lease. The invalidity or unenforceability of any provision shall not affect the validity or enforceability of any other provision.

14.4 Waiver. No waiver by Landlord or Tenant of any breach or default by the other party shall be effective unless in writing signed by the waiving party. Waiver of one breach or default shall not constitute waiver of any other or subsequent breach or default.

14.5 Entire Agreement. This Lease, together with any exhibits or attachments hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral, relating to such subject matter. No modification of this Lease shall be effective unless in writing signed by both Landlord and Tenant.

14.6 Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to principles of conflicts of law. Each party irrevocably consents to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Santa Clara County, California for resolution of any disputes arising under this Lease.

14.7 Interpretation. The section headings contained in this Lease are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Use of the term "Lease" refers to this instrument and all exhibits and attachments hereto. The pronouns "he," "she," "it," and "they" shall include all genders and shall refer to persons or entities as appropriate.

14.8 Authority. Each party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Lease and that the person executing this Lease on its behalf is authorized to do so.

14.9 Binding Effect. This Lease shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties hereto and their respective successors and assigns.

14.10 Time of Essence. Time is of the essence with respect to all dates and time periods specified in this Lease for performance of obligations by either party.
