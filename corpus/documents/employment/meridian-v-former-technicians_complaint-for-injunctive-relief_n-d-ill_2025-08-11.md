NATURE OF THE ACTION 
1. This is an action for breach of contract, trade secret misappropriation, and tortious
interference with contractual relations. The lawsuit arises out of two high-level former executives 
of Meridian breaching their contractual obligations to the Company. The defendants also 
misappropriated Meridian's confidential and trade secret information in violation of their legal 
duties to the Company.
