RETENTION BONUS AGREEMENT

This Retention Bonus Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of February 15, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Meridian Mechanical of Washington, LLC, a Washington limited liability company with its principal place of business located at 3310 South Pine Street, Tacoma, Washington 98409 ("Company"), and Talia Ruiz-Montoya ("Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Employee is employed by Company as a Commercial Service Coordinator at the Tacoma branch, at an annual base salary of $71,500.00;

WHEREAS, Employee has been a valued member of the Company's team and has made significant contributions to the Company's operations and success;

WHEREAS, Company desires to retain Employee's continued services and expertise, and Employee is willing to commit to remain employed with Company on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. RETENTION BONUS

Company shall award to Employee a retention bonus in the aggregate amount of Twelve Thousand Dollars ($12,000.00) (the "Retention Bonus"), subject to the terms, conditions, and restrictions set forth in this Agreement. The Retention Bonus is in addition to Employee's regular compensation and benefits and is intended as a special inducement for Employee to remain in the employ of Company through the dates specified herein.

2. PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The Retention Bonus shall be paid to Employee in two equal installments, less applicable federal, state, and local withholding taxes, Social Security taxes, Medicare taxes, and any other legally required deductions:

a. First Installment: Six Thousand Dollars ($6,000.00) shall be paid on or before August 15, 2024.

b. Second Installment: Six Thousand Dollars ($6,000.00) shall be paid on or before February 15, 2025.

All payments shall be made via Employee's regular payroll method, and all payments shall be subject to applicable federal, state, and local tax withholding and employment tax obligations. Employee acknowledges that Employee is solely responsible for all tax consequences arising from the receipt of the Retention Bonus. Company shall provide Employee with appropriate tax reporting documentation as required by law.

3. CONDITIONS

Employee's receipt of the Retention Bonus is conditioned upon the following:

a. Continued Employment. Employee must remain in active, full-time employment with Company in good standing through and including the date each installment of the Retention Bonus is scheduled to be paid. Specifically, Employee must be employed through August 15, 2024, to receive the first installment, and through February 15, 2025, to receive the second installment. Employee's employment must be in an active, non-leave status on each respective payment date, except for approved paid time off.

b. No Voluntary Resignation. If Employee voluntarily resigns or requests to resign from employment with Company prior to either scheduled payment date, Employee shall forfeit all remaining unpaid portions of the Retention Bonus. For purposes of this Agreement, "voluntary resignation" means Employee's initiation of separation from employment with Company, whether orally or in writing, for any reason whatsoever, including but not limited to personal reasons, relocation, health matters, or desire to pursue other employment opportunities.

c. Satisfactory Performance. Throughout the period from the Effective Date through each payment date, Employee shall maintain satisfactory job performance and shall not be subject to any disciplinary action, suspension, or written warning. Employee shall comply with all Company policies, procedures, and practices in effect from time to time, including the Company's employee handbook, safety policies, and attendance policies.

d. No Cause for Discharge. Employee shall not have been terminated for Cause by Company prior to the applicable payment date. For purposes of this Agreement, "Cause" means: (i) violation of Company's Code of Conduct or material violation of Company policies; (ii) incompetence, negligence, or repeated failure to perform job duties; (iii) insubordination or willful misconduct; (iv) theft, dishonesty, or fraud; (v) attendance or punctuality issues that materially interfere with business operations; (vi) unauthorized absence or repeated unexcused absences; (vii) use, possession, distribution, or being under the influence of illegal drugs or alcohol during work or on Company premises; (viii) violation of Company's safety policies or procedures; or (ix) any other conduct that materially and adversely affects the Company's interests or reputation.

e. No Cause for Discharge Not for Lack of Work. If Employee is terminated by Company without Cause, including but not limited to termination due to lack of work, reduction in force, or business restructuring, Employee shall forfeit the Retention Bonus or any unpaid portion thereof. Lack of work or business necessity shall not constitute Cause for purposes of this Agreement, and such termination shall result in forfeiture of the Retention Bonus.

4. CLAWBACK

a. General Clawback Provision. In the event Employee violates any material term of this Agreement or fails to satisfy any condition to receipt of the Retention Bonus, Company shall have the right to recover and reclaim any Retention Bonus payments previously made to Employee. Such recovery may be accomplished through offset against Employee's final paycheck, request for reimbursement, or any other lawful collection method available to Company.

Clawback Upon Competition. If, within twelve (12) months after receiving any installment of the Retention Bonus, Employee resigns and accepts employment with, or provides services to, a business that competes with the Company within fifty (50) miles of any Company branch in the State of Washington, Employee shall repay the full amount of such installment within thirty (30) days after the Company's written demand.

5. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

a. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflicts of law principles. Washington state employment law shall apply to all matters relating to the Retention Bonus and Employee's rights and obligations hereunder.

b. Jurisdiction. Employee and Company hereby consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts located in Pierce County, Washington, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington, as applicable, for the resolution of any disputes arising from or relating to this Agreement.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with Employee's employment agreement (if any) and the Company's employee handbook and policies in effect from time to time, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Employee and an authorized representative of Company.

7. SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such provision shall be severed, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect to the extent permitted by law.

8. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT

Except as expressly modified by this Agreement, Employee's employment with Company remains at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without cause and with or without notice. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment contract for any specified term or changes Employee's at-will employment status.

9. NO ASSIGNMENT

Neither this Agreement nor any rights or obligations hereunder may be assigned by Employee without the prior written consent of Company. Any attempted assignment in violation of this section shall be void. Company may assign this Agreement to any successor or affiliate without Employee's consent.

10. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

a. Notices. Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered in person, by certified mail (return receipt requested), by reputable overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices to Employee shall be addressed to Employee at Employee's address on file with Company. Notices to Company shall be addressed to the address set forth above, attention Human Resources.

b. Waiver. The waiver by either party of any breach or violation of any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of any other or subsequent breach or violation.

c. Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

d. Taxes. Employee acknowledges that the Retention Bonus is subject to all applicable federal, state, and local income tax withholding and employment tax obligations. Employee is solely responsible for any tax liabilities arising from the receipt of the Retention Bonus, including estimated quarterly tax payments if applicable.
