TRAINING REPAYMENT AGREEMENT

THIS TRAINING REPAYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., a business entity organized and existing under the laws of California (the "Company"), and Delia Kilgore, an individual ("Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company is willing to provide financial assistance to Employee for participation in a training program that will enhance Employee's professional skills and qualifications in the plumbing and HVAC industry; and

WHEREAS, the Company and Employee desire to set forth the terms and conditions under which such training assistance will be provided, including the conditions under which Employee may be required to repay certain training costs if Employee's employment is terminated under certain circumstances; and

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that this training will result in Employee obtaining a transferable professional credential and skills that benefit both Employee's career development and the Company's operations.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Company and Employee agree as follows:

1. TRAINING PROGRAM

1.1 The Company agrees to provide financial assistance to Employee for participation in the following training program: NATE Core and HVAC Service Specialty certification course. This training program may include, without limitation, professional certification programs such as North American Technician Excellence (NATE) certification, manufacturer-specific vocational and refrigeration (VRF) system training, apprenticeship year programs, journeyman preparation courses, or other specialized technical training determined by the Company to be beneficial to Employee's development as a skilled trades professional.

1.2 Employee acknowledges that the training program identified herein is designed to provide Employee with recognized, industry-standard qualifications and credentials that are portable and transferable among employers in the plumbing and HVAC industry. Employee further acknowledges that such credentials enhance Employee's professional marketability and career prospects within the industry.

1.3 The specific curriculum, training schedule, location, and any prerequisites for the training program shall be determined by the Company in its sole discretion, and the Company may provide Employee with written notice of such details separately from this Agreement. Employee agrees to comply with all requirements of the training program and to maintain satisfactory progress as defined by the training provider and the Company.

2. TRAINING COSTS

2.1 The Company agrees to pay for all costs associated with Employee's participation in the training program, which costs are estimated at $3,450.00. Such costs may include, without limitation, tuition fees, examination fees, course materials, textbooks, online learning platform access, travel and lodging expenses incurred in connection with the training, meals provided during training sessions, and any administrative fees charged by the training provider or the Company.

2.2 The Company will pay the training costs directly to the training provider or will reimburse Employee for costs paid by Employee, upon presentation of receipts and evidence of payment satisfactory to the Company. The Company reserves the right to verify all training costs and may request itemized invoices or receipts prior to payment.

2.3 Employee acknowledges that the training costs represent a substantial investment by the Company in Employee's professional development. Employee further acknowledges that the training costs are distinct from and in addition to Employee's regular wages and compensation.

3. TRAINING COMPLETION DATE

3.1 Employee agrees to complete all requirements of the training program on or before April 10, 2026 (the "Completion Date"), unless the Completion Date is extended in writing by mutual agreement of the parties. Employee understands that satisfactory completion of the training program requires not only attendance but also successful demonstration of competency as determined by the training provider.

3.2 Employee agrees to notify the Company promptly upon successful completion of the training program and to provide the Company with copies of any certificates, licenses, credentials, or examination results obtained upon completion.

3.3 If Employee fails to complete the training program by the Completion Date or fails to achieve satisfactory results, the parties may, at the Company's discretion, discuss extended timelines or alternative arrangements. However, the Company's financial obligation with respect to costs already incurred shall not be affected by Employee's failure to complete the program, and the repayment obligations set forth herein shall apply as if the training had been completed.

4. REPAYMENT OF TRAINING COSTS

Repayment of Training Costs. If Employee voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause within twenty-four (24) months after completing the Training Program, Employee shall repay the Company the Training Costs of $3,450.00, reduced by one twenty-fourth (1/24) for each full month of employment completed after the Training Program. Employee authorizes the Company to deduct any amount owed from final wages to the extent permitted by law.

4.2 Employee acknowledges that the training provided by the Company is contingent upon Employee's continued employment with the Company for a reasonable period following completion of the training program. The repayment obligation exists to protect the Company's investment in Employee's development and to ensure that the Company receives a reasonable benefit from providing such training assistance.

4.3 The repayment obligations described herein shall apply regardless of whether Employee's termination is voluntary, involuntary, for cause, without cause, or due to resignation. The sole exception shall be termination by the Company without cause, as defined in the Company's employee handbook or as otherwise determined by the Company's management, in which case the Company may, at its discretion, waive the repayment obligation in whole or in part.

5. VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

5.1 Employee expressly acknowledges and agrees that Employee's participation in the training program is entirely voluntary. The Company is not requiring Employee to participate in this training program as a condition of employment. Employee has not been coerced, threatened, or promised any specific employment benefit or promotion in exchange for agreeing to participate in the training program or signing this Agreement.

5.2 Employee certifies that Employee is signing this Agreement of Employee's own free will, with full understanding of the terms and conditions, and after having had a reasonable opportunity to review the document and to ask questions or seek legal counsel if Employee desires to do so.

5.3 Employee further acknowledges that, notwithstanding the voluntary nature of the training program itself, once Employee agrees to participate and the Company begins paying training costs, the repayment obligations set forth in Section 4 shall be binding and enforceable against Employee in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

5.4 Employee understands that the training program is designed to provide Employee with a transferable credential and professional skills that will benefit Employee throughout Employee's career in the plumbing and HVAC industry, regardless of whether Employee remains employed with the Company. The Company acknowledges that Employee has the right to pursue career opportunities with other employers, and the Company is not attempting to restrict Employee's mobility or professional development through this Agreement.

6. ACKNOWLEDGMENTS AND REPRESENTATIONS

6.1 Employee represents and warrants that (a) Employee has the full legal capacity to enter into this Agreement; (b) Employee is at least eighteen years of age; (c) Employee is not subject to any court order, judgment, or other legal restriction that would prevent Employee from entering into this Agreement; and (d) Employee has disclosed to the Company any and all prior training reimbursement agreements or similar obligations owed to other employers.

6.2 Employee acknowledges that Employee has read and fully understands all terms and conditions of this Agreement. Employee further acknowledges that Employee has been provided with sufficient time to review this Agreement and to consult with legal counsel or other advisors if Employee chooses to do so.

6.3 The Company acknowledges that the training program will provide Employee with professional qualifications and certifications that are recognized throughout the industry and are not proprietary to the Company. The Company does not claim ownership of any professional credentials, certifications, or licenses obtained by Employee through the training program, and Employee shall retain all rights to use such credentials in connection with employment with any employer.

7. CONDITIONS OF CONTINUED EMPLOYMENT

7.1 Employee must remain in good standing as an employee of the Company throughout the training program. This means Employee must comply with all Company policies, maintain satisfactory performance and conduct, and continue to perform Employee's regular job duties while participating in the training program.

7.2 If Employee is suspended, placed on disciplinary probation, or receives written notice of performance deficiency during the training program, the Company may, at its discretion, suspend Employee's participation in the training program and withhold further training cost payments. However, if Employee's suspension or disciplinary action is subsequently determined to be unjust or is reversed, the Company shall resume the training program and make timely payment of any training costs incurred.

8. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT

8.1 This Agreement does not constitute an employment agreement and does not alter the at-will nature of Employee's employment with the Company. Employee remains employed on an at-will basis, meaning Employee or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice, except as otherwise required by law.

8.2 The execution of this Agreement by the Company shall not be construed as a promise of continued employment, promotion, or any specific employment benefits beyond those otherwise provided by the Company to similarly situated employees.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral. No modification, amendment, or waiver of any term or condition of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both the Company and Employee.

9.2 If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect to the greatest extent permitted by law.

10. GOVERNING LAW

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflicts of law principles. The parties expressly agree that the substantive law of California shall apply to all matters arising under or related to this Agreement, including matters of contract interpretation, remedies, and enforcement.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in California, and the parties hereby consent to the personal jurisdiction of such courts and waive any objection based on forum non conveniens or any other jurisdictional defense.

10.3 The parties acknowledge that California is the state where Employee is employed and where the Company is located, and that California law provides appropriate protections and remedies for both employees and employers in training-related disputes.

11. SEVERABILITY

11.1 If any provision of this Agreement is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, void, or unenforceable, such provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or if such reformation is not possible, the provision shall be severed from this Agreement. The remaining provisions shall continue to apply and shall not be affected by the severance of any invalid provision.

12. NOTICES

Notice shall be deemed given when received or, if sent by mail, three business days after mailing.
