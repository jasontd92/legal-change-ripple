EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into effective as of January 1, 2021 (the "Effective Date"), between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF ILLINOIS, LLC, an Illinois limited liability company with a principal place of business located at 1455 Busse Road, Elk Grove Village, Illinois 60007 (the "Company"), and Carter Sorenson, an individual (the "Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company is engaged in the business of providing heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HVAC) and plumbing installation, maintenance, and service for residential and commercial clients throughout Illinois; and

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Employee as a technician and the Employee desires to accept employment with the Company, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein; and

WHEREAS, the Company seeks to protect its legitimate business interests, including but not limited to its confidential information, trade secrets, customer relationships, and business operations through this Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that the Employee will receive certain benefits beyond the base compensation specified herein, which constitutes adequate consideration for the Employee's agreement to the restrictive covenants contained herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, promises, and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 Position and Title. The Company hereby employs the Employee in the position of Lead Installation Technician at the Company's Naperville, Illinois branch (the "Position"). The Employee accepts such employment and agrees to perform the duties and responsibilities associated with said Position.

1.2 Scope of Duties. The Employee's duties and responsibilities shall include, but are not limited to: installation, maintenance, repair, and service of HVAC systems and plumbing systems; performing diagnostic work on mechanical and electrical components; interpreting technical drawings, blueprints, and manufacturer specifications; performing both new construction installation work and renovation or retrofit work; responding to emergency service calls; completing work orders and service documentation; maintaining tools and equipment in proper working condition; complying with all applicable federal, state, and local building codes, safety regulations, and environmental standards; communicating with customers regarding service performed and pricing; and such other duties as may be reasonably assigned by the Company from time to time. The Employee acknowledges that the Position may require work at various customer locations, on-call emergency service availability, and flexible scheduling based on customer service demands and seasonal workload fluctuations.

1.3 Independent Contractor Status. The Employee is and shall be at all times considered an employee of the Company, not an independent contractor. The Employee shall be subject to the Company's supervision, direction, and control with respect to both the results to be accomplished and the manner and means of accomplishment.

1.4 Employment Commencement. Employment under this Agreement shall commence on May 11, 2021 (the "Start Date"), unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Company and Employee. The Employee shall be responsible for completing all required paperwork, including I-9 verification, tax withholding forms, and background check authorizations, prior to or on the Start Date.

1.5 Duties of Faithfulness and Loyalty. The Employee agrees and covenants to perform all duties in a professional manner, with good faith and loyalty to the Company. The Employee shall represent the Company professionally at all times while performing work, including maintaining appropriate appearance and conduct with customers. The Employee further covenants to comply with all Company policies, procedures, safety protocols, and rules, as established and modified by the Company from time to time.

2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Hourly Rate. For all services rendered under this Agreement, the Company shall pay the Employee an hourly wage of $34.00 per hour per hour (the "Base Compensation"). This wage is subject to applicable federal, state, and local payroll taxes and withholdings as required by law.

2.2 Pay Periods and Frequency. The Employee shall be paid on a bi-weekly basis, with payment processed on the Company's standard payroll schedule. All compensation shall be deposited directly into the Employee's designated bank account via automated clearinghouse (ACH) transfer, unless the Employee requests alternative payment arrangements in writing.

2.3 Overtime Compensation. The Employee shall be compensated for overtime hours in accordance with the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and all applicable Illinois wage and hour laws. The Company shall calculate overtime compensation at the rate of one and one-half times the Employee's regular hourly rate for all hours worked in excess of forty (40) hours in any workweek. The Employee is not exempt from overtime requirements and is classified as a non-exempt employee.

2.4 Bonuses and Incentives. The Company may, at its sole discretion, provide performance bonuses, incentive compensation, or other supplemental pay based on individual performance metrics, team performance, customer satisfaction ratings, or other criteria established by the Company. Any such bonuses or incentives shall be subject to the Company's written bonus policy, as may be provided to the Employee.

2.5 Reimbursement of Expenses. The Employee shall be reimbursed for reasonable and pre-approved business expenses incurred in the performance of duties, including mileage for travel to and from customer service locations (calculated at the rate established by the Company), materials used in demonstrations or training, and other direct expenses authorized by the Company in writing. The Employee must submit itemized expense reports with appropriate documentation within thirty (30) days of incurring expenses to be eligible for reimbursement.

2.6 Tools and Equipment. The Company shall provide necessary tools and equipment required for the Employee to perform his duties, or shall reimburse the Employee for the cost of tools and equipment approved in advance by the Company. The Employee is responsible for the proper maintenance and care of all Company-provided tools and equipment. Any loss or damage to Company equipment due to the Employee's negligence or misuse shall be the responsibility of the Employee, and the Company may deduct the cost of repair or replacement from the Employee's compensation.

3. BENEFITS

3.1 Paid Time Off. The Employee shall accrue paid time off (PTO) in accordance with the Company's PTO policy. PTO includes time for personal illness, medical appointments, vacation, and personal business. The specific accrual rate and usage policies shall be set forth in the Company's employee handbook. PTO requests shall be submitted to the Employee's supervisor for approval, and approval shall not be unreasonably withheld for legitimate personal needs.

3.2 Holidays. The Employee shall receive paid time off on the following holidays, as recognized by the Company: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. If the Employee is required to work on a recognized holiday, the Employee shall be compensated in accordance with the Company's holiday pay policy.

3.3 Health Insurance. The Company may, at its sole discretion, offer health insurance coverage to employees who meet eligibility requirements. Details regarding health insurance plans, coverage options, employee contributions, and enrollment procedures shall be communicated to eligible employees separately.

3.4 Workers' Compensation. The Employee is covered by the Company's workers' compensation insurance policy as required by Illinois law. The Employee shall immediately report any work-related injury or illness to his supervisor and shall comply with all workers' compensation reporting and claims procedures.

3.5 Vehicle. To the extent the Employee is required to travel to customer locations or perform mobile service work, the Company shall either provide a vehicle or reimburse the Employee for mileage at a rate determined by the Company from time to time, not to be less than the rate allowed by the Internal Revenue Service.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 At-Will Employment. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that employment under this Agreement is at-will, meaning that either the Company or the Employee may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any reason or for no reason, with or without notice, subject to applicable state and federal law. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating an employment contract for any specific duration.

4.2 Termination by Company. The Company may terminate the Employee's employment at any time. In the event the Company terminates the Employee's employment, the Employee shall be entitled to receive all accrued and unpaid wages for services rendered and any accrued but unused PTO as required by Illinois law, payable on the final paycheck. The Company shall have no obligation to provide severance pay or any other compensation or benefits beyond this final paycheck.

4.3 Termination by Employee. In the event the Employee terminates this Agreement or voluntarily leaves employment with the Company, under no circumstances shall the Company be obligated to pay termination benefits, severance pay, bonus, or any compensation beyond the final earned wages with accrued and unused PTO. Voluntary termination by the Employee does not relieve the Employee of his obligations under the Confidentiality and Restrictive Covenants provisions of this Agreement.

4.4 Return of Property. Upon termination of employment for any reason, the Employee shall immediately return all Company property in his possession, including but not limited to tools, equipment, vehicles, keys, access cards, documents, files, electronic devices, and any other property belonging to the Company. The Employee shall cooperate fully in the transition of projects and handover of work to other Company employees or contractors. Failure to return Company property shall be grounds for withholding final compensation to the extent permitted by law.

4.5 Survival of Obligations. The Employee's obligations under the Confidential Information and Restrictive Covenants sections of this Agreement shall survive and continue in full force and effect following termination of employment for any reason and for any duration thereof.

5. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

5.1 Definition of Confidential Information. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that any and all Company products, information, data, documents, materials, methodologies, ideas, concepts, techniques, know-how, plans, designs, programs, systems or processes of any and every kind, nature or description, which the Employee may learn, create, receive, use, become aware of or work with while employed by the Company, including but not limited to business practices and procedures, proprietary installation techniques, product development information, customer lists, customer prospect materials, customer service records, customer pricing information, estimates and bids, marketing strategies, financial information, supplier contracts and pricing, vendor relationships, internal communications, operational procedures, and any other information not generally known to the public or to the industry, are owned by, proprietary to and constitute the trade secrets of the Company (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Information").

5.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Employee further agrees, covenants, represents and warrants that he will treat the Information as strictly confidential and will never disclose such Information to anyone without the Company's prior written permission and consent, except to the extent that disclosure is required by law or a court of competent jurisdiction. The Employee shall limit access to the Information to those persons within the Company who have a legitimate need to know such Information in the performance of their duties.

5.3 Use of Information. The Employee further agrees, covenants, represents and warrants that he will use the Information only in furtherance of the Company's business and interests, and that he will make no use of such Information in any other present or future activities, employment or business ventures, regardless of whether such activities or ventures are intended to or actually generate revenues or financial benefits to the Employee or any other person or entity.

5.4 Return of Information. In the event the employment relationship terminates, for any reason or whatever reason, the Employee further agrees that, before his departure from the Company, he will return any and all Company products, equipment, documents, files, information, and materials that he may have in his possession or under his control, including any copies, duplicates, or reproductions thereof. The Employee further agrees to destroy any personal copies or notes he may have retained and to certify to the Company in writing that all such materials have been returned or destroyed.

5.5 Irreparable Harm. The Employee acknowledges that the Information is valuable and unique, and that unauthorized disclosure or use would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Employee agrees that the Company shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

6. ADEQUATE CONSIDERATION

6.1 Consideration for Restrictive Covenants. The Employee acknowledges that, in addition to the base compensation provided under Section 2 of this Agreement, the Employee receives and shall receive substantial additional consideration for agreeing to the restrictive covenants and confidentiality obligations set forth herein. Such additional consideration includes, but is not limited to:

(a) Continued employment with the Company for a minimum of two (2) years from the Start Date, subject to compliance with this Agreement and applicable law, representing significant job security and stability;

(b) Professional development and training opportunities, including on-the-job training, access to technical certifications and professional development programs, and exposure to diverse HVAC and plumbing systems and techniques;

(c) Access to the Company's tools, equipment, vehicles, and resources necessary to develop and enhance the Employee's technical skills and professional reputation in the industry;

(d) Professional reputation and goodwill associated with working for an established and respected HVAC and plumbing contractor in the Illinois market;

(e) Opportunity for advancement and potential for increased compensation based on performance, tenure, and acquisition of additional skills and certifications;

(f) Access to a stable customer base and diverse service opportunities that enhance the Employee's technical expertise and market value; and

(g) Such additional financial or professional benefits as the Company may provide from time to time in its discretion, including but not limited to bonuses, incentive compensation, or expanded benefits.

6.2 Acknowledgment. The Employee specifically acknowledges that the consideration described above is substantial and valuable, and that such consideration, in addition to the base compensation and benefits provided herein, adequately compensates the Employee for the restrictions and obligations imposed by the Confidential Information and Restrictive Covenants sections of this Agreement.

7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Introduction. The Employee acknowledges that in the course of employment with the Company, the Employee will develop relationships with the Company's customers, will become familiar with the Company's customer base and service territories, will gain knowledge of the Company's business operations and customer service methods, and will be exposed to the Company's confidential and proprietary Information. The Employee further acknowledges that the Company has legitimate business interests in protecting its customer relationships, goodwill, trade secrets, and business operations.

7.2 Covenant Not to Compete.

Covenant Not to Compete. For twelve (12) months following termination of employment, Employee shall not, within the Illinois counties of Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake and Will, provide plumbing, heating, ventilation or air-conditioning installation, service or repair work to or for any competitor of the Company. Covenant Not to Solicit. For eighteen (18) months following termination, Employee shall not solicit any customer for whom Employee performed work during the last twelve (12) months of employment, or solicit any Company employee to leave the Company. Employee acknowledges receipt of this Agreement at least fourteen (14) calendar days before signing and has been advised to consult with an attorney.

7.3 Covenant Not to Solicit Customers. During the term of employment and for a period of two (2) years following the termination of employment for any reason, the Employee agrees, covenants, and binds himself not to solicit, contact, do business with, or attempt to establish any business relationship with any customer or prospective customer of the Company that the Employee serviced or became aware of through employment with the Company, either on behalf of the Employee or on behalf of any other person, business, or entity. This restriction shall apply to any customers or prospective customers for whom the Employee performed service work, provided estimates or bids, or otherwise had contact during employment, as well as any customers that were part of the Company's active customer base during the Employee's tenure.

7.4 Covenant Not to Solicit Employees. During the term of employment and for a period of two (2) years following the termination of employment for any reason, the Employee agrees and covenants not to solicit, recruit, encourage, or attempt to persuade any employee or independent contractor of the Company to leave the Company's employment or engagement, or to disclose to any person the identity or contact information of any Company employee or contractor, for the purpose of facilitating such recruitment or solicitation.

7.5 Non-Disparagement. The Employee agrees and covenants that, during the term of employment and at all times thereafter, the Employee shall not make any negative, disparaging, or derogatory statements or comments regarding the Company, its owners, management, operations, financial condition, customer service, work quality, or reputation, whether such statements are made publicly or privately, orally or in writing, including but not limited to communications on social media platforms. The Company likewise agrees not to make disparaging statements regarding the Employee, except as may be required in response to inquiries regarding job performance or eligibility for rehire.

7.6 Reasonableness and Legitimacy. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that all restrictive covenants contained in this Section 7 are reasonable in temporal scope, geographic scope, and subject matter scope, considering the nature of the Company's business, the Employee's position, the scope of the Employee's exposure to confidential Information and customer relationships, and the Company's legitimate business interests in protecting its trade secrets, customer relationships, and goodwill.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to principles of conflict of laws. The parties acknowledge that this Agreement is entered into in Illinois and is intended to be performed in Illinois.

8.2 Exclusive Jurisdiction. The parties irrevocably consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois for resolution of any disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement. The parties waive any claim that such courts lack personal jurisdiction and waive any right to transfer or remove any action to any other court.

8.3 Injunctive Relief. The parties acknowledge that breach of the Confidential Information or Restrictive Covenants provisions of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company, for which monetary damages would not be an adequate remedy. The parties agree that the Company shall be entitled to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable remedies, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, to enforce the provisions of this Agreement.

8.4 Attorneys' Fees. In any legal proceeding to enforce the terms of this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs from the non-prevailing party.

9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 At-Will Status. The Employee further reiterates and acknowledges that employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time, for any reason or for no reason, with or without notice, subject to applicable state and federal law.

9.2 Compliance with Laws. The Employee shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and statutes in the performance of duties under this Agreement, including but not limited to occupational safety and health regulations, building codes, environmental regulations, and consumer protection laws.

9.3 Background Check and Credentials. The Employee's employment is contingent upon successful completion of a criminal background check and verification of any licenses, certifications, or credentials required to perform the Position. The Company reserves the right to terminate employment if the background check reveals information that the Company determines, in its sole discretion, makes the Employee unsuitable for employment.

9.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, including any exhibits or attachments hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral. There are no understandings, agreements, or representations other than those contained in this written Agreement.

9.5 Modifications and Amendments. This Agreement may not be modified, amended, or supplemented except by written agreement signed by both the Company and the Employee. Any oral promises or statements made by the Company's representatives shall have no force or effect.

9.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, unenforceable, or void by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect to the maximum extent permitted by law. If any restrictive covenant is found to be overly broad, the parties agree that the court shall have authority to modify such provision to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

9.7 Waiver. No waiver of any provision or breach of this Agreement shall constitute a waiver of any other provision or breach. The failure of either party to enforce any right or provision shall not constitute a waiver of such right or provision.

9.8 Interpretation. This Agreement shall be interpreted fairly according to its terms and shall not be construed strictly for or against either party regardless of who drafted it. The headings and captions are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation or legal effect of the Agreement.

9.9 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other party. Any attempted assignment in violation of this provision shall be void. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor entity that acquires substantially all of the assets or business of the Company.

9.10 Successors and Assigns. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties hereto and their respective successors, assigns, heirs, and personal representatives.

9.11 Notice. Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by certified mail return receipt requested, sent by reputable overnight courier service, or sent by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices to the Company shall be addressed to its principal place of business. Notices to the Employee shall be addressed to the most recent address provided to the Company.

9.12 Independent Legal Advice. The Employee acknowledges that the Employee has been advised to consult with an independent attorney of the Employee's choosing regarding the terms of this Agreement prior to execution, and that the Employee understands the legal implications and consequences of the restrictive covenants contained herein.
