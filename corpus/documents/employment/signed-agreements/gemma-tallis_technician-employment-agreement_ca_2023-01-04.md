EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of the 1st day of March, 2017 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation, doing business as Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air, located at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, California 95833 (hereinafter referred to as the "Company"), and Gemma Tallis (hereinafter referred to as the "Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company is engaged in the business of providing professional plumbing, heating, air conditioning, and related mechanical services to residential and commercial customers throughout California;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Employee as a service technician and installer, and the Employee desires to accept such employment, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein;

WHEREAS, in the course of performing the Employee's duties, the Employee will have access to and become familiar with confidential information and trade secrets of the Company that are not generally known to the public or to the Company's competitors, including customer lists, customer prospect materials, customer service records, technical information, proprietary methods and procedures, business practices, pricing information, marketing materials, and other valuable confidential business information;

WHEREAS, the Employee understands and agrees that the Employee will be entrusted by the Company and its clients with confidential information and materials pertaining to the Company's business, including but not limited to Company products, business practices and procedures, future business plans for equipment and service development, customer lists and customer prospect materials, customer service records, technical specifications and diagnostic procedures, together with marketing procedures and supplier relationships, all of which were developed by the Company or represent the accumulated experience and knowledge of the Company;

WHEREAS, it is mutually recognized that the Company has a legitimate business interest in protecting its name, goodwill, existing client relationships and confidential business information and trade secrets;

WHEREAS, the parties desire to establish clear terms and conditions of employment, including the Employee's compensation, duties, responsibilities, and obligations to maintain the confidentiality of the Company's proprietary information;

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual promises, agreements and covenants herein contained, and other good and valuable consideration, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties, intending to be legally bound, do hereby agree as follows:

1. POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 Position. The Company hereby employs the Employee, and the Employee hereby accepts such employment, commencing on January 18, 2023, as a Installation Technician assigned to the Fresno, California branch of the Company (the "Start Date").

1.2 Duties and Responsibilities. The Employee shall perform all duties and responsibilities associated with the position of Installation Technician, which may include but are not limited to the installation, maintenance, diagnosis, repair, and troubleshooting of plumbing systems, heating systems, and air conditioning systems for the Company's residential and commercial customers. The Employee shall follow all Company policies, procedures, standards, and work protocols as set forth in the Company's employee handbook and as directed by the Employee's supervisors or branch manager from time to time. The Employee shall perform all work in a professional, courteous, safe, and competent manner, consistent with all applicable California building codes, California licensing requirements, industry standards, and best practices. The Employee shall maintain professionalism and courtesy in all interactions with customers, vendors, and Company personnel.

1.3 Place of Work. The Employee's primary place of work shall be at the Company's Fresno, California branch location and service area. The Employee shall travel to customer locations throughout the designated service territory as determined by the Company to perform service work, installations, repairs, and maintenance. The Employee shall report to the assigned location at the times and on the dates directed by the Company. The Employee may be required to work extended hours, evenings, weekends, or holidays depending upon customer needs and business requirements.

1.4 Reporting Relationship. The Employee shall report directly to the branch manager or such other supervisor as the Company may designate from time to time. The Employee shall comply with all reasonable requests and directives from supervisory personnel.

1.5 Modifications to Duties and Title. The Company reserves the right to modify the Employee's job duties, responsibilities, title, rate of pay, hours of work, location, and reporting relationships at any time in its sole discretion, without notice, and without creating any claim for damages. The Employee will be notified of any significant changes to the position.

2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Hourly Rate. The Company shall pay the Employee a base hourly wage of $30.00 per hour per hour. This hourly rate shall be reviewed and may be adjusted annually at the sole discretion of the Company based upon the Employee's performance, experience, length of service, business conditions, and other factors deemed relevant by the Company.

2.2 Payment Schedule. The Company shall pay the Employee on a biweekly schedule via check or direct deposit, as directed by the Company. All compensation shall be subject to applicable federal, state, and local tax withholdings, deductions, and other legally mandated deductions.

2.3 Overtime Compensation. The Employee shall be entitled to overtime compensation in accordance with the Fair Labor Standards Act and California Labor Code requirements. Overtime compensation shall be calculated and paid at the applicable overtime rate as follows: one and one-half times the regular hourly rate for all hours worked in excess of eight hours in a single workday, or forty hours in a single workweek, whichever results in the greater compensation; and double time for all hours worked in excess of twelve hours in a single workday or for all hours worked in excess of eight hours on the seventh consecutive day of work in a single workweek. All overtime calculations shall be performed in accordance with applicable California Labor Code provisions.

2.4 Service Vehicle. The Company shall provide the Employee with a fully equipped and properly maintained service vehicle for use in performing the Employee's assigned job duties. The service vehicle shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Company. The Employee shall use the Company vehicle only for authorized Company business purposes and shall maintain the vehicle in good condition and repair, reporting all maintenance needs, damage, and mechanical problems to the Company promptly. The Employee shall not use the Company vehicle for personal use, recreational purposes, or any unauthorized purposes, and shall not permit any unauthorized persons to operate or ride in the vehicle. The Employee shall return the vehicle to the Company at the end of each business day or as directed by the Company. The Employee shall comply with all traffic laws, safety regulations, and reasonable Company vehicle policies while operating the Company vehicle.

2.5 Tools and Equipment. The Company shall provide the Employee with all standard tools, equipment, and materials necessary to perform the Employee's job duties, including but not limited to hand tools, power tools, specialized plumbing and HVAC diagnostic and service equipment, and personal protective safety equipment. The Employee shall be responsible for the proper safekeeping and maintenance of all Company-provided tools and equipment and shall return such items to the Company in the same condition as received, normal wear and tear excepted, upon termination of employment or at any time upon request by the Company. The Employee shall not remove Company tools or equipment from Company premises without prior written authorization from the Company. The Employee shall bear the full cost of replacement or repair for any tools or equipment that are lost, damaged, or destroyed due to the Employee's negligence, carelessness, or intentional misconduct.

2.6 Benefits. The Employee shall be entitled to such employee benefits as the Company may offer to similarly situated employees in accordance with the Company's benefit plans, policies, and practices as in effect from time to time. The Company may modify, amend, suspend, or terminate any benefit plans or programs at any time in its sole discretion without notice. The Employee is responsible for understanding the terms and conditions of any benefit plans in which the Employee participates.

3. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT

3.1 Employment Status. The Employee understands and expressly agrees that employment with the Company is "at-will" employment. This means that either the Company or the Employee may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any lawful reason or no reason whatsoever, and without advance notice or cause, except as may be required by applicable law. Nothing in this Agreement creates or implies any contract of employment for any definite term or period of time. The at-will nature of the employment relationship shall remain in full force and effect during the Employee's employment with the Company.

3.2 No Implied Contract. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that no oral statements, representations, prior course of dealing, or prior employment experiences with the Company create any express or implied contract of employment for any definite term, period, or duration. The Employee also acknowledges that the Employee's position, compensation, title, duties, work location, schedule, and all other terms and conditions of employment may be changed at any time at the sole discretion of the Company without consent from the Employee.

3.3 Right to Modify Terms. The Company reserves the right to modify, supplement, amend, or supplement the terms and conditions of this Agreement at any time in its sole discretion. If the Company makes material modifications to the terms of this Agreement, the Company will notify the Employee of such changes in writing. If the Employee continues to work for the Company following notice of any material changes, the Employee shall be deemed to have accepted such modifications as binding.

4. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND TRADE SECRETS

4.1 Acknowledgment of Confidential Information. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that in the course of employment, the Employee will have access to and become familiar with confidential information and trade secrets of the Company that are not generally known to the public or to the Company's competitors. The Employee acknowledges and understands the significant value and sensitivity of such information to the Company's business operations, competitive position, customer relationships, and financial success.

4.2 Definition of Confidential Information. For purposes of this Agreement, Confidential Information includes but is not limited to: (a) customer lists and customer prospect materials and leads; (b) customer service records, preferences, service history, and billing information; (c) pricing information, cost data, profit margins, and rate structures; (d) technical information, specifications, and procedures relating to plumbing and HVAC systems, repairs, installations, maintenance, and diagnostic methods; (e) proprietary methods, techniques, systems, and practices developed or used by the Company; (f) marketing and business plans, strategies, and promotional materials; (g) financial information and performance data; (h) supplier information, relationships, and contact information; (i) software, computer programs, and technology systems; (j) internal manuals, guidelines, training materials, and operational procedures; (k) equipment specifications and capabilities; and (l) any other information identified by the Company as confidential or which reasonably should be understood to be confidential based on the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

4.3 Obligation of Confidentiality. The Employee agrees that the Employee shall not, at any time during employment or after termination of employment for any reason, disclose, use, or permit the disclosure or use of any Confidential Information or trade secrets for the Employee's own benefit or for the benefit of any third party or competitor without the prior express written consent of the Company. The Employee shall maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information and trade secrets at all times, taking all necessary and reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure, access, use, or misuse.

4.4 Permitted Disclosure. Disclosure of Confidential Information is permitted only when required by law, valid court order, subpoena, or regulatory demand, provided that the Employee shall notify the Company of any such required disclosure as promptly as possible to permit the Company to seek protective measures or an injunction.

4.5 Duration of Obligations. The Employee's obligations with respect to Confidential Information and trade secrets shall survive termination of employment and shall remain in effect in perpetuity with respect to information that qualifies as a trade secret under California law and indefinitely with respect to other Confidential Information.

5. INVENTION ASSIGNMENT

5.1 Assignment of Inventions and Work Product. The Employee agrees that all inventions, discoveries, improvements, innovations, works of authorship, and other creative work product, including without limitation any modifications to Company equipment, procedures, or services, created, developed, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Employee, whether during work hours or during off-duty time, whether at Company facilities or elsewhere, that in any way relate to the Company's business, operations, products, services, or customer relationships, are the sole and exclusive property of the Company, and the Employee hereby irrevocably assigns all right, title, and interest in such inventions and work product to the Company.

5.2 California Labor Code Section 2870. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, the Employee acknowledges receipt of and understands the following notice required by California Labor Code Section 2870:

"Pursuant to California Labor Code Section 2870, an employee shall not assign, or be required to assign, an invention to an employer if the invention:

(a) is developed entirely on the employee's own time without using the employer's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information; and

(b) does not relate at the time of conception to the employer's existing or prospective business or research.

The foregoing provision does not prohibit an employer from requiring an employee to assign an invention otherwise directed to the employer by contract, provided such contract was executed by the employee and the employer (or representative thereof) after a thorough discussion with the employee regarding the provisions of Section 2870, or after the employee's receipt of written notice thereof.

California Labor Code Section 2870 does not prohibit agreements between employer and employee by which an employee agrees to submit for employer review and determination disclosures of potential employee inventions before product commercialization, providing that the employer is under a duty to evaluate such submissions in a timely fashion.

All agreements contained herein are subject to the provisions of Labor Code Section 2870."

5.3 Cooperation. The Employee agrees to cooperate fully with the Company at the Company's expense and to execute any documents necessary or desirable for the Company to obtain, perfect, or protect patent, copyright, or other intellectual property protection for any such inventions or work product.

6. NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Employee's employment with the Company and for a period of one (1) year immediately following termination of employment for any reason, the Employee agrees not to solicit, recruit, encourage, or attempt to hire or engage any employee of the Company, or to solicit any employee to leave the Company's employment or reduce the scope of their employment, without the prior express written consent of the Company. This restriction applies whether such solicitation is direct or indirect, and whether undertaken by the Employee personally or through a third party, intermediary, or competing business.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers Through Use of Trade Secrets.

Non-Solicitation of Customers Through Use of Trade Secrets. Employee shall not, during or after employment, use the Company's trade secrets, including its customer lists, service histories and Comfort Club membership data, to solicit any Company customer. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from engaging in any lawful profession, trade or business after employment ends, consistent with California Business and Professions Code Section 16600.

6.3 Inevitable Disclosure Doctrine. The Employee acknowledges that if the Employee accepts employment with a competitor or starts a competing business during or after employment with the Company, the Employee may be subject to claims under the doctrine of inevitable disclosure if the Employee's responsibilities with the competitor would necessarily involve disclosure, use, or application of the Company's trade secrets or confidential information.

7. RETURN OF PROPERTY

7.1 Obligation to Return Property. Upon termination of employment for any reason, or at any time during employment upon request by the Company, the Employee shall immediately return to the Company in good condition, all property of the Company in the Employee's possession, custody, or control, including but not limited to:

(a) The Company service vehicle and all keys, door openers, and access devices thereto;
(b) All tools, equipment, and materials provided by the Company;
(c) All uniforms, clothing, safety equipment, protective gear, and identification badges provided by the Company;
(d) All files, records, documents, memoranda, reports, and materials containing or relating to Confidential Information or trade secrets;
(e) All electronic devices provided by the Company, including cellular phones, smartphones, computers, tablets, laptops, access cards, and keys;
(f) All Company credit cards, purchase orders, and financial instruments;
(g) Any other property belonging to the Company or its customers.

7.2 Condition of Returned Property. All property shall be returned in the same condition as originally received, normal wear and tear excepted. The Company may deduct from the Employee's final paycheck, to the extent permitted by California law, any cost associated with replacing, repairing, or recovering property that has been damaged or destroyed by the Employee through negligence, carelessness, or intentional misconduct. The Company may pursue other collection and recovery remedies available by law for any unreturned or damaged Company property.

7.3 Electronic Information and Data. The Employee shall ensure that all electronic data, files, and information stored on any Company electronic devices, systems, networks, or cloud storage shall be transferred to the Company or deleted as directed by the Company. The Employee shall cooperate with the Company in transferring any work-related data, customer information, or files created or developed during employment to the Company's systems or storage.

7.4 Verification of Return. Before the Employee receives the Employee's final paycheck, the Employee shall certify and verify to the Company in writing that all Company property has been returned. Failure to return all Company property may result in withholding of the Employee's final paycheck and accrued paid time off to the extent permitted by law.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY ACKNOWLEDGMENT

8.1 Acknowledgment. The Employee has carefully read and fully understands all confidentiality and trade secret protection provisions of this Agreement. The Employee acknowledges the Company's legitimate business interests in protecting its confidential information, trade secrets, customer relationships, and proprietary business information. The Employee acknowledges that the Employee has had full opportunity to ask questions about these provisions and to consult with legal counsel if desired.

8.2 Irreparable Harm. The Employee acknowledges and agrees that any breach of the confidentiality provisions of this Agreement may cause irreparable injury to the Company that cannot be adequately remedied by monetary damages alone. Accordingly, the Employee agrees that the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, temporary restraining orders, and other equitable remedies to prevent any unauthorized disclosure, use, or misuse of Confidential Information or trade secrets.

9. MUTUAL ARBITRATION AGREEMENT

9.1 Incorporation by Reference. The parties acknowledge that this employment relationship is subject to a separate Mutual Arbitration Agreement entered into by and between the Company and the Employee (the "Arbitration Agreement"). The terms, conditions, and procedures set forth in the Arbitration Agreement are incorporated herein by reference and are binding upon both parties. In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this Agreement and the provisions of the Arbitration Agreement, the Arbitration Agreement shall govern with respect to dispute resolution procedures.

9.2 Scope of Arbitration. Any and all disputes, claims, or controversies arising out of or relating to this employment, the termination of employment, or the interpretation of this Agreement, including but not limited to claims for wages, overtime, benefits, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, breach of contract, tort claims, and any other claims arising from the employment relationship, shall be subject to binding arbitration pursuant to the Arbitration Agreement rather than litigation in court.

10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

10.1 Status. The Employee is an employee of the Company and is not an independent contractor, agent, partner, or joint venturer. The Employee is not authorized to bind the Company, represent the Company, or enter into contracts on behalf of the Company except as specifically authorized in writing by the Company. The Employee shall not represent to third parties or customers that the Employee has any authority to bind the Company or make commitments on behalf of the Company except as specifically authorized.

11. REPRESENTATIONS OF EMPLOYEE

11.1 Authority. The Employee represents and warrants that the Employee has the full right and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform all obligations set forth herein, and that there are no legal impediments to the Employee's performance.

11.2 Qualifications and Licenses. The Employee represents and warrants that the Employee possesses all licenses, certifications, permits, and qualifications required by California law to perform the position of Installation Technician in California, including any required plumbing or HVAC licenses and certifications. The Employee is solely responsible for obtaining and maintaining all required licenses and certifications in good standing throughout the term of employment and shall provide proof of such licenses and certifications upon request.

11.3 No Conflicting Obligations. The Employee represents and warrants that the Employee is not subject to any non-compete agreement, non-solicitation agreement, confidentiality agreement, invention assignment, or other restrictive covenant with any other employer, entity, or person that would prevent or restrict the Employee from performing the duties and obligations of this position or complying with the terms of this Agreement.

11.4 Compliance with Law. The Employee shall comply at all times with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, licensing requirements, building codes, and industry standards in performing the Employee's job duties and responsibilities.

12. SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE

12.1 Safety Requirements. The Employee shall comply with all Occupational Safety and Health Administration requirements, Company safety policies and procedures, industry safety standards, and reasonable Company directives regarding workplace safety. The Employee shall immediately report all workplace injuries, hazards, unsafe conditions, and accidents to the Company.

12.2 Substance Abuse and Drug Testing. The Employee agrees to comply with the Company's substance abuse and drug testing policies as set forth in the Company employee handbook or as communicated by the Company in writing.

12.3 Background Investigation. The Employee acknowledges that continued employment is conditioned upon successful completion of a background investigation satisfactory to the Company. The Employee consents to background investigation and verification of information provided by the Employee.

13. GOVERNING LAW

13.1 Choice of Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws or conflict of provisions principles.

13.2 Venue and Jurisdiction. Except as otherwise provided in the Mutual Arbitration Agreement, the parties agree that any legal action or proceeding arising out of this Agreement or the employment relationship shall be subject to the dispute resolution and arbitration provisions in the Mutual Arbitration Agreement. To the extent arbitration is not required or applicable, the parties irrevocably consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Sacramento County, California, and waive any objections based on venue or inconvenient forum.

13.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, unenforceable, or in violation of law, such provision shall be reformed and construed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or if such provision cannot be reformed, such provision shall be severed, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect and shall be enforced to the greatest extent permitted by law.

13.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Mutual Arbitration Agreement and the Company employee handbook, constitutes the entire, complete, and final agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter and terms and conditions of employment, and supersedes and replaces all prior negotiations, representations, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral, except with respect to confidential information and trade secrets protected by common law or statute.

13.5 Amendments and Modifications. This Agreement may be amended, modified, supplemented, or changed only by a written document signed by both the Company and the Employee. No oral representations or agreements shall be binding upon either party.
