TECHNICIAN EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of January 1, 2019 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF WASHINGTON, LLC, a Washington limited liability company with principal offices located at 3310 South Pine Street, Tacoma, Washington 98409 ("Company"), and Renee Mbeki ("Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Company is engaged in the business of providing plumbing, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning services; and

WHEREAS, Company desires to employ Employee in a professional capacity, and Employee desires to accept such employment, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Company and Employee agree as follows:

1. POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 Position. Company hereby employs Employee as a Service Plumber at Company's Tacoma, Washington branch location, effective as of January 19, 2021 ("Effective Date of Employment"). Employee's principal duties shall include performing plumbing, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning services, including but not limited to installation, repair, maintenance, and inspection of mechanical systems; responding to customer service calls; diagnosing system failures; performing preventive maintenance; and such other duties as may be reasonably assigned by Company management from time to time in connection with Employee's position.

1.2 Duties and Responsibilities. Employee shall perform all duties assigned in a professional manner and in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and safety standards. Employee shall maintain all required licenses and certifications for the state of Washington and shall notify Company immediately of any lapse, suspension, or revocation of such licenses or certifications. Employee shall adhere to Company's established policies, procedures, and safety protocols as set forth in the Employee Handbook and as may be amended from time to time. Employee shall treat all customers, colleagues, and third parties with professionalism and courtesy.

1.3 Work Schedule and Location. Employee's work schedule shall be determined by Company management and may include regular hours, on-call duties, emergency service calls, and travel to various customer locations throughout the Tacoma service area and surrounding regions. Company reserves the right to modify work schedules, reassign duties, or transfer Employee to other Company locations or branches as business needs require.

1.4 Performance Standards. Employee shall meet all performance standards established by Company, including but not limited to quality of work, customer satisfaction, attendance and punctuality, and compliance with safety regulations. Company may conduct regular performance reviews and may provide Employee with performance improvement plans, additional training, or other corrective measures as deemed necessary.

2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Hourly Rate. Employee shall be compensated at an hourly rate of $46.50 per hour per hour for all hours worked ("Hourly Rate"). This rate shall be subject to adjustment at Company's sole discretion based on performance, experience, market conditions, and other factors deemed relevant by Company management.

2.2 Payment Schedule. Employee shall be paid bi-weekly via direct deposit to Employee's designated financial institution or by check at Company's election. All compensation shall be subject to applicable federal, state, and local tax withholdings and other legally required deductions.

2.3 Overtime Compensation. Employee shall be classified as a non-exempt employee under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Any hours worked in excess of forty (40) hours per week shall be compensated at the rate of one and one-half times the Hourly Rate, in accordance with all applicable federal and state wage and hour laws.

2.4 Paid Time Off. Employee shall be entitled to accrue paid time off ("PTO") in accordance with Company's PTO policy as set forth in the Employee Handbook. PTO includes vacation days, sick days, and personal days. The accrual rate and usage policies shall be established by Company and communicated to Employee in writing. Accrued but unused PTO may be subject to forfeiture or payout in accordance with applicable Washington law and Company policy.

2.5 Expenses and Reimbursements. Employee shall be reimbursed for all reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred on Company's behalf, including mileage, meals, lodging, and other job-related costs, upon submission of itemized receipts and in accordance with Company's expense reimbursement policy. Employee is responsible for maintaining accurate records of all expenses.

2.6 Benefits. Employee shall be eligible to participate in Company's group health insurance plan, including medical, dental, and vision coverage, on the same terms and conditions as other similarly situated employees. Company's contribution to Employee's health insurance premiums shall be as determined by Company's Board of Managers from time to time. All benefits are subject to the terms of the applicable plan documents and insurance policies, which shall control in the event of any conflict with this Agreement.

3. EMPLOYMENT STATUS

3.1 At-Will Employment. Employee's employment is at-will, meaning that either Party may terminate this employment relationship at any time, for any reason, with or without cause, and with or without notice, unless otherwise required by law. No representative of Company has the authority to make any promise of continued employment or to bind Company to any employment terms other than as expressly set forth in this Agreement.

3.2 Modification of Terms. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the terms and conditions of employment. Company reserves the right to modify, add to, or delete from the terms and conditions of employment, including compensation, benefits, duties, and other conditions of employment, at any time in its sole discretion, upon notice to Employee.

4. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

4.1 Definition of Confidential Information. During the course of employment, Employee shall have access to and become acquainted with various Confidential Information belonging to Company, including but not limited to: customer lists and customer contact information; customer service histories and records; pricing information and cost data; business methods and processes; trade secrets; proprietary techniques; financial information; marketing strategies; supplier relationships; employee information; and any other information marked as confidential or reasonably understood by Employee to be confidential or proprietary.

4.2 Obligations with Respect to Confidential Information. Employee acknowledges that all Confidential Information is the exclusive property of Company and has been developed at substantial cost and effort. Employee agrees that during employment and at all times thereafter, Employee shall:

a. Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any third party without the express written consent of Company management;

b. Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing duties for Company and not for any personal benefit or gain;

c. Not remove any Confidential Information from Company premises or make unauthorized copies of any such information;

d. Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon termination of employment, as directed by Company; and

e. Cooperate with Company in protecting Confidential Information from unauthorized disclosure or use.

4.3 Exceptions. Confidential Information shall not include information that: (a) becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by Employee; (b) is rightfully received by Employee from a third party without confidentiality obligations; (c) is independently developed by Employee without use of or reference to Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided Employee provides prompt notice to Company to permit Company to seek protective measures.

4.4 Remedies. Employee acknowledges that breach of this Confidentiality provision may cause irreparable harm to Company for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, Company shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

5. NONCOMPETITION

5.1 Acknowledgment and Disclosure. Employee acknowledges that Company has provided written disclosure of the noncompetition covenant contained in this Section 5 no later than at the time of Employee's acceptance of this offer of employment, or if employment has already commenced, no later than the date of this Agreement, in compliance with Washington Revised Code Section 19.86.140.

Noncompetition. For twelve (12) months after the end of Employee's employment for any reason, Employee shall not, within twenty-five (25) miles of the Company branch at which Employee was primarily assigned, perform residential or commercial plumbing, heating, ventilation or air-conditioning service work for any business that competes with the Company. The parties intend this covenant to be enforceable to the fullest extent permitted by RCW 49.62; this Section shall not apply if Employee's earnings from the Company, when annualized, are equal to or less than the threshold amount then in effect under RCW 49.62.020.

6. NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 Non-Solicitation of Customers. Employee acknowledges that Company has invested substantial time, resources, and reputation in developing its customer relationships. Accordingly, Employee agrees that, for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly:

a. Solicit or attempt to solicit the business of any customer or prospective customer of Company with whom Employee worked or about whom Employee became acquainted during employment, for the purpose of providing plumbing, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, or mechanical services competitive with those offered by Company;

b. Encourage or induce any customer to cease doing business with Company or to redirect its business to a competitor; or

c. Divert or attempt to divert any customer business to any other entity in which Employee has a direct or indirect interest.

6.2 Definition of Customers. For purposes of this Non-Solicitation provision, "customers" shall include all customers of Company's Tacoma branch and all other Company branches with which Employee worked or interacted during employment, as well as any prospective customers with whom Employee had contact during employment.

6.3 Permitted Activities. Nothing in this Non-Solicitation provision shall prohibit Employee from accepting business from customers who independently contact Employee, provided Employee does not actively solicit such business. However, Employee must disclose such customer contacts to Company, and Company may assert its rights under this provision as to any customers reasonably believed to have been solicited.

6.4 Equitable Relief. Employee acknowledges that breach of this Non-Solicitation provision may cause irreparable harm to Company. Accordingly, Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance, in addition to any monetary damages.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 At-Will Relationship. As stated above, employment under this Agreement is at-will. Either Party may terminate employment at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without advance notice, except as required by law.

7.2 Termination by Company. Company may terminate Employee's employment immediately for cause or without cause. "Cause" shall include, but not be limited to: (a) violation of Company policies; (b) misconduct, dishonesty, or violation of law; (c) poor performance or failure to meet performance standards; (d) breach of this Agreement; (e) violation of safety regulations; (f) insubordination; (g) unauthorized absence; or (h) any other conduct that Company determines is harmful to Company's business, reputation, or operations.

7.3 Termination by Employee. Employee may terminate employment by providing written notice to Company, with such notice to be provided at least two (2) weeks in advance of the intended termination date. Company may waive this notice requirement and may require Employee to leave immediately upon notice of termination.

7.4 Final Compensation. Upon termination of employment, Employee shall receive final payment of all accrued compensation due, less applicable withholdings, within the time period required by Washington law. Any accrued but unused PTO shall be paid in accordance with Company policy and Washington law.

7.5 Return of Company Property. Upon termination, Employee shall immediately return to Company all Company property in Employee's possession, including but not limited to: keys, tools, equipment, vehicles, access cards, electronic devices, documents, customer lists, and any other Company materials. Employee shall also provide written notice of all passwords and account access information.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

8.2 Venue. Employee and Company irrevocably consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in the county where Employee resides at the time of the dispute or the time of termination of employment, whichever is more favorable to the party seeking to enforce this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to jurisdiction or venue in such courts and waives any right to transfer the action to a court of different jurisdiction or venue.

8.3 Waiver of Jury Trial. To the extent permitted by law, each Party waives its right to a trial by jury in any legal proceeding arising out of this Agreement.

9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Employee Handbook and any other written policies and procedures of Company, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the terms and conditions of employment and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral. No promises, inducements, or agreements have been made except as set forth in this Agreement.

9.2 Amendment and Modification. This Agreement may not be amended, modified, or supplemented except by a written instrument signed by both Company and Employee. Any amendment that materially changes the at-will nature of employment must comply with Washington law.

9.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or if that is not possible, shall be severed from this Agreement. The remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

9.4 Reformation and Blue Pencil. Employee and Company agree that if any court determines that any restrictive covenant (including the noncompetition or non-solicitation provisions) is overly broad or overbroad in scope, duration, or geography, such court is authorized to reform and modify such provision to the extent necessary to make it enforceable under applicable law.

9.5 Waiver. The failure of either Party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not be deemed a waiver of such provision or the right to enforce it at a later date. No single or partial exercise of any right or remedy shall preclude any other or further exercise thereof or the exercise of any other right or remedy.

9.6 Successors and Assigns. This Agreement shall inure to the benefit of and be binding upon the Parties and their respective successors and assigns. Employee acknowledges that this Agreement is personal in nature and may not be assigned without Company's written consent. Company may assign its rights and obligations under this Agreement to any successor entity or purchaser of Company's business, and such assignment shall not relieve Company of its obligations hereunder.

9.7 Independent Contractor Status. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to make Employee an independent contractor, partner, or agent of Company. Employee is an employee of Company and is subject to Company's direction, control, and supervision.

9.8 No Third Party Beneficiaries. This Agreement is intended solely for the benefit of Company and Employee and their respective successors and assigns. No third party has any rights or claims under this Agreement.

9.9 Notices. All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, or sent by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices to Company shall be addressed to the Company's principal office. Notices to Employee shall be addressed to Employee's last known address.

9.10 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures and PDF counterparts shall have the same force and effect as original signatures.

9.11 Equitable Relief. Employee and Company acknowledge that breaches of the Confidential Information, Noncompetition, and Non-Solicitation provisions may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, each Party shall be entitled to seek preliminary and permanent injunctive relief to prevent breaches or enforce compliance with these provisions.

9.12 Survival. The provisions of Sections 4 (Confidential Information), 5 (Noncompetition), 6 (Non-Solicitation), 8 (Governing Law and Venue), and this Section 9 shall survive any termination of employment and remain in full force and effect.

9.13 Compliance with Law. This Agreement shall be interpreted and enforced in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, including without limitation the Fair Labor Standards Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, the Washington Minimum Wage Act, and the Washington Paid Leave Act.
