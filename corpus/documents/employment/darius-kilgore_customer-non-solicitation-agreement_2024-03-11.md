CONFIDENTIALITY AND CUSTOMER NON-SOLICITATION AGREEMENT

THIS CONFIDENTIALITY AND CUSTOMER NON-SOLICITATION AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of March 11, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company with its principal place of business at 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, Texas 77041 ("Company"), and DARIUS KILGORE, an individual ("Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Employee is employed by Company as a Comfort Advisor (commission sales) at Company's San Antonio branch location; and

WHEREAS, in the course of Employee's employment, Employee will have access to certain confidential and proprietary information of Company, including but not limited to customer information, pricing data, and membership information for Company's Comfort Club program; and

WHEREAS, Company desires to protect its confidential information and customer relationships by establishing certain restrictions on Employee's activities during and after the term of Employee's employment with Company; and

WHEREAS, Company is willing to grant Employee access to such confidential information, and Employee is willing to accept such access, upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Company and Employee agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to the business, operations, customers, and finances of Company and its affiliates, including but not limited to: (a) customer names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, and account information; (b) pricing information, cost structures, and discount schedules; (c) Comfort Club membership lists and member pricing; (d) service records and customer service histories; (e) marketing plans and customer acquisition strategies; (f) technical information relating to Company's service delivery and operations; (g) financial information, including revenue data, profit margins, and operational costs; and (h) any other information that is not generally known to the public or to Company's competitors. Confidential Information includes information disclosed to Company by its vendors, manufacturers, and other business partners that Company is obligated to maintain in confidence.

1.2 "Company Group" means Company, its parent companies, subsidiary companies, and affiliated entities.

1.3 "Employment Period" means the period beginning on Employee's hire date and continuing through the date of Employee's termination of employment with Company for any reason.

1.4 "Restricted Territory" means the geographic area served by Company's San Antonio branch, including the counties of Bexar, Guadalupe, Comal, Caldwell, and Hays, Texas.

1.5 "Customer" means any person or business entity that has received services from Company or has been a member of Company's Comfort Club program at any time during the twelve (12) months preceding Employee's termination of employment.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 Acknowledgment of Confidential Information. Employee acknowledges that in the course of employment with Company, Employee will have access to extensive Confidential Information belonging to Company, including customer lists, pricing information, Comfort Club membership data, and other proprietary business information. Employee acknowledges that such Confidential Information is valuable, unique, and not generally available to the public or to Company's competitors, and that Company has invested significant time, effort, and expense in developing and maintaining such Confidential Information.

2.2 Obligation of Confidentiality. Employee agrees to maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence both during the Employment Period and for a period of three (3) years following termination of employment with Company for any reason. Employee will not disclose any Confidential Information to any person or entity outside the Company Group without the prior written consent of an authorized representative of Company, except as required by law or legal process. Employee will not use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the performance of Employee's duties as a Comfort Advisor for Company.

2.3 Return of Materials. Upon termination of employment for any reason, Employee will immediately return to Company all documents, electronic files, customer information, pricing materials, and other materials containing or reflecting Confidential Information. Employee will not retain any copies, excerpts, or summaries of any Confidential Information.

2.4 Prior Employment Obligations. Employee represents and warrants that Employee's performance of all terms of this Agreement will not breach any obligation to keep in confidence proprietary information obtained during prior employment. Employee will not disclose to Company or use in connection with employment by Company any confidential or proprietary information obtained in confidence from any prior employer, nor will Employee bring onto Company's premises or use any documents or property belonging to any prior employer in violation of any agreement with that employer.

2.5 Third Party Information. Employee understands that Company receives from third parties, including manufacturers, suppliers, and business partners, certain confidential or proprietary information subject to duties of confidentiality. Employee agrees to hold such third-party information in confidence and to use it only as authorized by Company and only for the purposes specified by the third party.

3. CUSTOMER NON-SOLICITATION

3.1 Scope of Restriction. During the Employment Period and for a period of two (2) years following termination of employment with Company for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, encourage, or attempt to solicit for business any Customer of Company, whether for services offered by Company or for services offered by any competitor of Company.

3.2 Definition of Solicitation. "Solicitation" includes any direct or indirect communication, contact, or attempt to establish a business relationship with a Customer for the purpose of providing plumbing services, HVAC services, or any other services competitive with those offered by Company. Solicitation includes personal contact, telephone contact, email contact, social media contact, or any other form of communication or outreach.

Customer Non-Solicitation. For twenty-four (24) months following the end of Employee's employment, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, divert or accept plumbing or HVAC business from any customer or Comfort Club member of the Company with whom Employee had material contact during the last eighteen (18) months of employment.

3.4 Permitted Activities. Nothing in this Section 3 shall prevent Employee from: (a) rendering services to any Customer who independently contacts Employee without any solicitation by Employee; provided that Employee does not disclose Company's Confidential Information; or (b) providing services in a geographic area outside the Restricted Territory; provided that Employee does not solicit any Customer having a principal place of business within the Restricted Territory.

4. EMPLOYEE NON-SOLICITATION

4.1 Employee Non-Solicitation Covenant. During the Employment Period and for a period of eighteen (18) months following termination of employment with Company for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, encourage, or attempt to recruit for employment or independent contractor engagement any person employed by Company or engaged as an independent contractor by Company. This restriction applies to all employees and contractors of the Company Group.

4.2 Prohibited Conduct. Employee agrees not to encourage, assist, or participate in the recruitment of Company employees or contractors, including by providing employee lists, contact information, or assistance to any third party seeking to recruit Company employees or contractors.

5. TERM AND DURATION

5.1 Effective Period. This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and continues during the Employment Period. The confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 2 shall survive termination of employment and continue for three (3) years following termination. The customer non-solicitation covenant set forth in Section 3 shall survive termination of employment and continue for two (2) years following termination. The employee non-solicitation covenant set forth in Section 4 shall survive termination of employment and continue for eighteen (18) months following termination.

6. REMEDIES

6.1 Injunctive Relief. Employee acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement will cause irreparable harm to Company for which monetary damages are an inadequate remedy. In addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, including temporary restraining orders, preliminary injunctions, and permanent injunctions, to prevent any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement. The right to seek injunctive relief shall not require Company to post any bond.

6.2 Liquidated Damages. Without limiting any other remedy, if Employee breaches any provision of Section 3 or Section 4 of this Agreement, Employee shall pay to Company liquidated damages in the amount of $5,000 for each Customer solicited in violation of Section 3, and $2,500 for each employee or contractor solicited in violation of Section 4, in addition to any other damages Company may recover at law or in equity.

6.3 Attorneys' Fees. In the event of any legal action or proceeding arising from or relating to this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing party.

6.4 Remedies Are Cumulative. All remedies available under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

7. CONSIDERATION

7.1 Adequate Consideration. Employee acknowledges that this Agreement is supported by adequate consideration, including: (a) access to Company's Confidential Information, including customer lists, pricing data, and Comfort Club membership information; (b) employment by Company; (c) the opportunity to earn commissions on customer sales; and (d) any continued employment following the Effective Date.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

8.1 Texas Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

8.2 Jurisdiction and Venue. Employee consents to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Harris County, Texas, for any legal action or proceeding arising from or relating to this Agreement. Employee waives any objection to venue in such courts based on inconvenience or any claim that such courts are an improper forum.

9. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

9.1 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, unenforceable, or contrary to law, such provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable while preserving the intent of the parties. If any provision cannot be reformed, it shall be severed, and all other provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

9.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with any offer letter or employment agreement between Company and Employee, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations, whether written or oral, relating to such subject matter.

9.3 Amendments. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by an authorized representative of Company and by Employee.

9.4 No Waiver. The failure of Company to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of such provision or a waiver of Company's right to enforce such provision at any future time.

9.5 Equitable Estoppel. Employee acknowledges that Company's failure to discover a breach of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of Company's rights, and Company shall be entitled to enforce this Agreement at any time without regard to the length of time that has elapsed since a breach occurred.

9.6 Successors and Assigns. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of Company and its successors and assigns. Employee's obligations under this Agreement are personal and may not be assigned or delegated.
