This Addendum to the Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC Employee Handbook is effective as of January 1, 2025, and applies to all employees of Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC, a Texas limited liability company with principal offices located at 4521 Westheimer Road, Suite 140, Houston, Texas 77027. This Addendum supplements and, where applicable, supersedes conflicting provisions in the Employee Handbook previously distributed to employees. All employees are required to review and acknowledge receipt of this Addendum in accordance with Company procedures.

1. FINAL PAY

Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC is committed to compliance with all applicable Texas wage and hour laws, including the requirements set forth in the Texas Labor Code regarding the timely payment of wages to employees upon separation of employment.

When an employee's employment is terminated, whether by resignation, layoff, or discharge, the Company shall provide the employee with all earned and unpaid wages by the earlier of (a) the next regularly scheduled payday or (b) not later than the fifteenth (15th) day after the date of termination. All earned wages include compensation for all hours worked, accrued paid time off in accordance with Company policy, and any other forms of compensation earned during the period of employment. Earned wages do not include speculative bonuses, commissions not yet due, or benefits that have not vested according to the terms of the applicable plan.

Upon termination, the employee must return all Company property, including vehicles, tools, uniforms, electronic equipment, and access cards or credentials. The Company may deduct from final wages the reasonable cost of repairing or replacing Company property damaged by the employee's negligence or misuse, provided that such deduction does not reduce the employee's final paycheck below minimum wage for the hours worked during the final pay period, in accordance with applicable law.

Employees who resign in writing with at least two (2) weeks' notice shall receive final payment by the date specified in Section 61.014 of the Texas Labor Code. Employees who terminate employment without notice or who are separated for cause shall receive final payment as required by law. The Company shall provide each terminated employee with an itemized statement of deductions, if any, from final wages, in accordance with the Texas Payday Law.

2. JURY DUTY AND VOTING

Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC recognizes the civic importance of jury service and voting and complies fully with all Texas statutory requirements regarding employee leave for these purposes.

An employee who is summoned to serve as a juror shall be permitted to take leave from work to fulfill that obligation. The Company will not discharge, threaten, or otherwise discriminate against an employee for responding to a jury summons, attending jury selection, serving on a jury, or appearing in court as a prospective juror. An employee summoned for jury duty should notify his or her supervisor or Human Resources as soon as practicable upon receipt of the jury summons.

Employees shall be permitted to use paid time off, subject to Company policy, for the time spent in jury service. If the employee is released from jury duty before the end of a scheduled shift, the employee should return to work as directed by his or her supervisor, subject to any restrictions imposed by the court. Employees are responsible for providing the Company with a copy of the jury summons and, following completion of jury service, any court documentation indicating the dates of service.

Employees are entitled to time off to vote in any election held in Texas without loss of pay or adverse employment action. An employee may take up to two (2) hours of paid leave, or such longer period as may be required by applicable law, to vote in any general, special, or primary election. The employee should notify his or her supervisor in advance if possible so that the absence can be scheduled to minimize operational disruption. Voting leave is in addition to any other paid time off to which the employee is entitled.

3. HEAT STRESS PREVENTION

All employees, particularly those engaged in fieldwork or outdoor duties, must strictly comply with the Company's Heat Stress Prevention Program. Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC operates primarily throughout the greater Houston metropolitan area and surrounding regions, where summer temperatures regularly exceed 95 degrees Fahrenheit and present significant health and safety risks.

Supervisors and crew leaders shall implement appropriate rest periods, hydration breaks, and work schedules to reduce heat exposure and prevent heat-related illness. Fieldwork shall be scheduled to avoid peak heat hours whenever operationally feasible. During summer months (June through September), extended work shifts shall be scheduled to begin before 10:00 a.m. and conclude by 3:00 p.m. whenever possible, with longer breaks during midday hours when temperatures are highest.

All field crews shall be provided with adequate potable water, with a minimum of one gallon of water per employee per shift, available at all job sites. Water coolers or dispensers shall be accessible to employees at least once every two (2) hours during fieldwork. Employees are required to drink water regularly and may take additional hydration breaks as needed.

Employees must immediately report any symptoms of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, including dizziness, nausea, excessive fatigue, confusion, rapid heartbeat, or loss of consciousness. Any employee exhibiting such symptoms shall be moved to a cool location and provided immediate first aid. Supervisors must contact emergency services (911) if symptoms do not improve within fifteen (15) minutes or if the employee appears seriously ill. No employee shall be required to continue work while experiencing heat-related illness symptoms until cleared to do so by medical personnel.

All supervisors, foremen, and crew leaders receive annual training in heat stress recognition, prevention, and response. Employees are required to participate in this safety training and to report any unsafe conditions or inadequate protective measures to their supervisor and to the Company's Safety Department. Failure to comply with heat stress prevention procedures or refusal to take required breaks may result in disciplinary action.

4. FIREARMS IN VEHICLES

Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC respects the lawful rights of employees under Texas law while maintaining a safe workplace environment.

In accordance with Texas Labor Code Section 52.061, no employee shall be prohibited from transporting a firearm in a vehicle parked on Company property, provided that the firearm is (a) lawfully owned by the employee; (b) not prohibited by federal law; and (c) secured in a locked vehicle or locked container within the vehicle. This protection applies only to firearms stored in vehicles on Company property used for employee parking and does not apply to vehicles used primarily for the delivery of Company services or equipment.

Employees are strictly prohibited from bringing firearms into the Company's offices, dispatch centers, warehouses, or other interior facilities, regardless of whether such facilities are located on Company-owned or leased property. Employees are also prohibited from carrying loaded firearms or having firearms accessible within reach while operating Company vehicles or personal vehicles engaged in Company business.

Any violation of this firearms policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination of employment. Employees who have questions regarding the scope of this policy should contact Human Resources at (713) 555-0147 before bringing a firearm to any Company location.

5. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

Meridian Mechanical of Texas, LLC may require certain employees in positions involving access to confidential business information, customer lists, trade secrets, or substantial relationships with customers to enter into restrictive covenant agreements, including non-compete, non-solicitation, and confidentiality provisions.

Any such restrictive covenant agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Texas Business and Commerce Code Sections 15.50, 15.51, and 15.52 (the "Texas Restrictive Covenant Act"). Restrictive covenants entered into or enforced by the Company shall be enforceable only to the extent permitted by applicable Texas law and shall be limited in scope, duration, and geographic area to those restrictions reasonably necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, including its trade secrets, confidential business or professional information, substantial relationships with prospective or existing customers or clients, and its goodwill associated with ongoing business or professional practice.

Any restrictive covenant proposed by the Company will be provided to the affected employee in writing and will specify the legitimate business interests to be protected, the scope of prohibited activities, the geographic scope, and the time period for which the restriction applies. Employees should review any restrictive covenant carefully and contact Human Resources or seek independent legal advice if they have questions regarding the terms or enforceability of such agreements.

The Company reserves the right to seek injunctive relief and other equitable remedies to enforce valid restrictive covenants and shall recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs in any legal action to enforce such covenants to the extent permitted by law.
