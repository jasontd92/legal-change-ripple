RETENTION BONUS AGREEMENT

This Retention Bonus Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of April 1, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between Meridian Mechanical of Washington, LLC, a Washington limited liability company with its principal place of business at 3310 South Pine Street, Tacoma, Washington 98409 ("Company"), and Rafael Underhill, an individual residing in the State of Washington ("Employee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company has recently acquired substantially all of the assets and business operations of Pinecrest Plumbing & Drain ("Acquisition");

WHEREAS, the successful integration and transition of the Acquisition operations is material to the Company's business objectives and strategic plans;

WHEREAS, Employee currently serves as Service Manager for the Company's Spokane branch with significant responsibilities relating to field operations and customer relationships; and

WHEREAS, the Company desires to incentivize and retain Employee's continued services throughout the integration period and recognizes that Employee's ongoing commitment, institutional knowledge, and leadership are critical to the successful transition of acquired business operations.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. RETENTION BONUS

The Company shall pay to Employee a retention bonus in the aggregate amount of Thirty Thousand Dollars ($30,000.00) ("Retention Bonus"), conditioned upon Employee's continued employment with the Company and Employee's satisfaction of the conditions set forth in this Agreement. The Retention Bonus is in addition to Employee's regular compensation and shall not be construed as a substitute for, or reduction of, Employee's annual base salary of One Hundred Four Thousand Dollars ($104,000.00), nor shall it limit Employee's eligibility for other bonuses, incentive compensation, benefits, or allowances to which Employee may otherwise be entitled under the Company's compensation plans and policies.

2. PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The Retention Bonus shall be paid to Employee in three equal installments of Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00) each, according to the following schedule, provided that Employee continues to satisfy all conditions set forth in Section 4 of this Agreement:

a. First Installment: Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00) shall be paid on or before October 1, 2025;

b. Second Installment: Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00) shall be paid on or before April 1, 2026;

c. Third Installment: Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00) shall be paid on or before October 1, 2026.

Each installment shall be paid by check or by electronic direct deposit in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices. The Company shall process each payment net of all applicable federal, state, and local income tax withholdings and payroll deductions required by law. The Company reserves the right to adjust the timing of any scheduled payment by up to thirty (30) days in its reasonable discretion if administrative or operational circumstances warrant such adjustment.

3. POSITION AND CONTINUED EMPLOYMENT

Employee shall continue to serve as Service Manager for the Company's Spokane branch with the same general duties, responsibilities, and reporting relationships in effect as of the Effective Date. Employee's continued employment shall remain subject at all times to the Company's employment policies, benefits plans, and working conditions as they exist from time to time, including any modifications, restructurings, or policy changes that the Company may implement on a company-wide basis or in connection with the integration of the Acquisition. Nothing in this Agreement shall limit the Company's right to modify Employee's job duties, title, location, compensation structure, or benefits, provided that such changes comply with applicable law.

4. CONDITIONS OF PAYMENT

The payment of each installment of the Retention Bonus is expressly conditioned upon satisfaction of all of the following conditions as of the scheduled payment date:

a. Continuous Employment. Employee shall remain continuously employed by the Company on a full-time basis through and including the date of each scheduled installment payment. If Employee's employment is terminated for any reason prior to any scheduled payment date—whether by the Company with or without Cause, by Employee with or without Good Reason, or by operation of law or other circumstance—Employee shall immediately forfeit all unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus, and Employee shall have no further right, claim, or demand with respect to such unpaid amounts. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if Employee is on an approved leave of absence (including military leave, jury duty, or other statutory leave) of ninety (90) days or less, such leave shall not constitute a termination of employment for purposes of this Section; however, any approved leave extending beyond ninety (90) consecutive days shall be treated as a termination of employment unless formally extended or renewed in writing by the Company.

b. Satisfactory Job Performance. Employee shall maintain satisfactory performance in the discharge of Employee's duties and responsibilities as determined by the Company in its reasonable business discretion. If the Company determines that Employee's performance is unsatisfactory in any material respect, the Company shall provide Employee with written notice describing the performance deficiency and indicating that such deficiency could affect payment of the next scheduled installment. Employee shall have thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of such notice within which to cure or substantially remedy the identified deficiency. Failure to cure or substantially remedy the deficiency within such thirty-day period shall result in forfeiture of the next scheduled installment at the Company's sole discretion.

c. Compliance with Policies and Law. Employee shall continuously comply with all applicable Company policies, procedures, workplace rules, and practices, as well as all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations. Employee shall maintain compliance with safety requirements, confidentiality obligations, and all other material Company policies. Material violation of any Company policy or applicable law shall be grounds for immediate forfeiture of any unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus at the Company's sole discretion.

d. No Material Breach or Misconduct. Employee shall not engage in any conduct constituting fraud, theft, dishonesty, violation of fiduciary duty, gross negligence, willful misconduct, insubordination, or other serious breach of duty toward the Company or its business operations. The occurrence of any such conduct shall result in immediate forfeiture of all remaining unpaid installments.

5. CLAWBACK AND REPAYMENT OBLIGATIONS

a. Repayment Upon Termination or Separation. If, within twenty-four (24) months following the payment of any installment of the Retention Bonus, Employee's employment with the Company is terminated or separated under any of the following circumstances, Employee shall, at the Company's sole election, either (i) repay to the Company the full amount of the Retention Bonus previously paid to Employee, or (ii) authorize the Company to offset such amount against any final paycheck, accrued vacation payout, severance payment, or other compensation otherwise due to Employee:

(i) Employee voluntarily terminates employment with the Company without Good Reason as defined in Section 5(b) below;

(ii) Employee is terminated by the Company for Cause as defined in Section 5(c) below; or

(iii) Employee engages in conduct that violates any material Company policy or applicable law in a manner that would reasonably constitute grounds for termination.

The Company shall provide written notice of its election under this Section within thirty (30) days of the occurrence of the triggering event. If the Company elects repayment, Employee shall remit the required amount to the Company within fifteen (15) business days of receiving such notice. If Employee disputes the triggering event or the Company's determination, Employee may request a meeting with the Company's Human Resources department to discuss the matter before final determination.

b. Definition of Good Reason. For purposes of this Agreement, Employee shall have "Good Reason" to terminate employment if Employee terminates employment in response to any of the following actions by the Company, provided that Employee provides the Company with written notice of the condition within thirty (30) days of its occurrence and allows the Company thirty (30) days to cure the condition:

(i) A material diminution of Employee's job duties, responsibilities, or authority without Employee's consent;

(ii) A material reduction in Employee's annual base salary, bonus opportunity, or benefits without Employee's consent;

(iii) A relocation of Employee's principal work location by more than fifty (50) miles from the Spokane branch location; or

(iv) A material breach of this Agreement or of Employee's employment agreement that remains uncured following thirty (30) days' written notice to the Company.

c. Definition of Cause. For purposes of this Agreement, the Company shall have "Cause" to terminate Employee for any of the following reasons:

(i) Employee's conviction of any felony or any crime of dishonesty, fraud, or moral turpitude;

(ii) Willful or grossly negligent misconduct in the performance of Employee's duties that causes material harm to the Company;

(iii) Material breach of this Agreement, any confidentiality or non-compete agreement, or other material agreement with the Company that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice;

(iv) Chronic absenteeism, tardiness, or pattern of unauthorized absences;

(v) Violation of applicable federal, state, or local law, or material violation of Company policy, in a manner that materially affects the Company's business or reputation; or

(vi) Negligence or inattention to duties resulting in repeated performance deficiencies.

Clawback Upon Competition. If, within twelve (12) months after receiving any installment of the Retention Bonus, Employee resigns and accepts employment with, or provides services to, a business that competes with the Company within fifty (50) miles of any Company branch in the State of Washington, Employee shall repay the full amount of such installment within thirty (30) days after the Company's written demand.

6. TAX TREATMENT AND WITHHOLDING

a. Payroll Tax Withholding. Each installment payment of the Retention Bonus shall be subject to applicable federal, state, and local income tax withholding and all other required payroll deductions and taxes. The Company shall calculate, withhold, and timely deposit all such taxes and withholdings in accordance with applicable law and Company practice. Employee acknowledges that the Company will treat the Retention Bonus as supplemental wages for federal income tax purposes.

b. Employee's Tax Responsibility. Employee acknowledges that the withholding rate applied to the Retention Bonus by the Company may not represent Employee's total federal, state, or local tax liability with respect to the bonus. Employee shall be solely responsible for satisfying any additional income tax obligations, estimated tax payments, or other tax liabilities arising from receipt of the Retention Bonus. Employee should consult with a qualified tax professional regarding Employee's specific tax obligations.

c. No Deduction Against Other Benefits. The Retention Bonus shall not be credited against, deducted from, or treated as an offset to any other compensation, severance, vacation payout, retirement benefits, or other benefits to which Employee may be entitled under Company policies or applicable law, except as expressly provided in Section 5(a) above.

7. NO OBLIGATION TO CONTINUE EMPLOYMENT

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating a contract of employment for any definite term, as guaranteeing Employee's continued employment for any specified period, or as imposing any obligation on the Company to continue Employee's employment beyond the date of any payment hereunder or at all. Employee's employment with the Company shall remain at-will and subject to termination at any time by either party, with or without cause and with or without notice, in accordance with applicable law. The provision of the Retention Bonus does not modify or alter the at-will nature of the employment relationship between the parties.

8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, understandings, and agreements, whether oral or written, concerning the Retention Bonus or Employee's entitlement to bonus compensation beyond Employee's regular base salary. No modification, amendment, or waiver of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both the Company and Employee.

9. SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Agreement is determined by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable, or in violation of applicable law, such provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or, if reformation is impossible, such provision shall be severed from this Agreement. The remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect and shall be interpreted to the fullest extent permitted by law.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to or application of its conflict of laws principles. The parties irrevocably consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in Pierce County, Washington for any legal action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

11. BINDING EFFECT AND SUCCESSORS

This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Company, Employee, and their respective successors and permitted assigns. This Agreement is personal in character and may not be assigned by Employee without the prior written consent of the Company, which consent may be withheld in the Company's sole discretion.

12. COUNTERPARTS AND EXECUTION

This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Execution and delivery of this Agreement by electronic transmission, facsimile, or PDF shall have the same force and effect as delivery of an original manually executed document.
