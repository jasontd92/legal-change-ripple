SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

THIS SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is entered into effective as of October 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company with a principal place of business at 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, Texas 77041 ("Contractor"), and PRECISION DUCT & EXHAUST, INC., a Texas corporation with a principal place of business at 6120 Brittmoore Road, Houston, Texas 77041 ("Subcontractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Contractor has been awarded a contract by the Texas Department of Transportation for the FM 1960 Interchange Maintenance Facility project, located in Harris County, Texas, Contract Number FHWA-1273, which project is funded in part by federal-aid highway funds administered by the Federal Highway Administration (the "Project"); and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires to engage Subcontractor to perform certain work on the Project, and Subcontractor desires to perform such work, all upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Contractor and Subcontractor agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF WORK

1.1 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, equipment, materials, and supervision necessary to perform the complete sheet-metal ductwork fabrication and installation work (the "Work") as described herein and as shown on the Project drawings and specifications. The Work shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement, the Project specifications, all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations, and industry standards for sheet-metal ductwork installation, including but not limited to the Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Contractors' National Association (SMACNA) standards and the International Mechanical Code.

1.2 The Work includes, but is not limited to, the following:

(a) Furnishing and fabricating all ductwork components, including straight duct sections, elbows, transitions, takeoffs, and hangers from 24-gauge galvanized steel;

(b) Installation of all supply and return ductwork within the Maintenance Facility building envelope, including routing through mechanical spaces, attic areas, and wall cavities as detailed on the mechanical drawings;

(c) Field connections, sealing, and support of all ductwork systems with appropriate hangers, vibration isolators, and fastening devices;

(d) Testing and balancing of ductwork for air-tightness in accordance with ASHRAE 90.1-2019 standards;

(e) Preparation of shop drawings showing fabrication details, dimensions, material specifications, and installation procedures for Contractor's review and approval prior to fabrication;

(f) Coordination with mechanical, electrical, and structural trades to ensure proper installation sequencing and avoidance of conflicts;

(g) Removal and disposal of packaging materials and job site debris related to ductwork installation; and

(h) Any other work reasonably necessary to complete the ductwork installation as a fully functional system.

1.3 The Work shall be substantially completed by December 15, 2024, and shall be finally completed by December 31, 2024, unless extended by written change order due to causes beyond Subcontractor's reasonable control.

1.4 Subcontractor shall provide a qualified foreman or supervisor on site during all work hours and shall maintain a minimum crew of two journeyman sheet-metal workers at all times during active ductwork installation periods.

2. PRICE

2.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Work for a fixed price of Two Hundred Eighty-Six Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($286,400.00) (the "Contract Price"), including all labor, materials, equipment, overhead, and profit. This price is all-inclusive and shall not be subject to adjustment except as provided in Section 5 (Changes) or as otherwise expressly provided in this Agreement.

2.2 The Contract Price includes, but is not limited to: all materials such as galvanized steel sheet, fasteners, sealants, and hangers; labor for fabrication and installation; equipment such as portable shears, brakes, and lifts; worker's compensation insurance; general liability insurance; and all indirect costs and profit.

2.3 Subcontractor warrants that the Contract Price is adequate to perform all Work in a fully professional manner and that Subcontractor has carefully examined all Project drawings, specifications, and site conditions in determining the Contract Price.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor based on the estimated percentage of Work completed each month, submitted by Subcontractor and approved by Contractor in writing. Subcontractor shall submit a monthly progress invoice by the last business day of each calendar month, supported by photographs, timecards, and a detailed breakdown of Work completed.

3.2 Contractor shall make progress payments equal to ninety percent (90%) of the approved invoice amount within twenty-one (21) calendar days following Contractor's receipt and approval of the invoice. The remaining ten percent (10%) shall be retained until final completion and acceptance of all Work.

3.3 The final payment, including all retained percentages, shall be made within thirty (30) calendar days following final completion, acceptance, and filing of all lien waivers and releases as required by Section 11.

3.4 Payment shall be made by check or electronic transfer to such account as Subcontractor may designate in writing. Subcontractor shall be responsible for providing accurate banking information and any changes thereto shall be communicated in writing to Contractor at least ten (10) business days prior to the next scheduled payment.

3.5 No payment shall be deemed an acceptance of defective or incomplete Work or a waiver of Contractor's rights to require correction or rework.

3.6 Contractor shall have the right to withhold or deduct from payments any amounts due for: (a) incomplete or non-conforming Work; (b) damage caused by Subcontractor; (c) failure to maintain required insurance; (d) liens or claims filed by Subcontractor or Subcontractor's suppliers; or (e) any other back charges as provided in Section 10.

4. FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Acknowledgment of Federal Funding. Subcontractor acknowledges that the Project is partially funded through federal-aid highway funds administered by the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) under Contract FHWA-1273. As such, the Work performed under this Agreement shall be subject to all applicable federal laws, regulations, and requirements, including but not limited to the Federal-Aid Highway Program requirements.

4.2 Federal Labor Standards. Subcontractor and all its workers shall comply with the Davis-Bacon Act, 40 U.S.C. § 3141 et seq., and the prevailing wage rates established by the Department of Labor for Harris County, Texas for sheet-metal workers and general laborers. Subcontractor shall pay all workers at or above the prevailing wage rates and shall submit certified weekly payroll records to Contractor as required.

4.3 Equal Employment Opportunity. Subcontractor shall comply with Executive Order 11246, as amended, and 41 CFR Chapter 60, and shall not discriminate against any employee or applicant for employment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, or national origin. Subcontractor shall include non-discrimination clauses in all purchase orders and agreements with its suppliers and shall require all lower-tier contractors and suppliers to comply with these requirements.

4.4 Buy American Requirements. Materials incorporated into the Work shall comply with Buy American Act requirements, 23 U.S.C. § 313. All steel materials, including galvanized sheet steel and fasteners, shall be produced in the United States. Subcontractor shall certify the domestic origin of all materials prior to fabrication and shall maintain documentation of such certifications.

4.5 Safety and Health. Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) requirements, 29 CFR Part 1926, and all OSHA standards applicable to sheet-metal fabrication and installation work. Subcontractor shall maintain a safe work environment, provide appropriate personal protective equipment to all workers, and shall hold safety meetings at least weekly.

4.6 Environmental Compliance. Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations, including the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, and Resource Conservation and Recovery Act. Subcontractor shall properly manage and dispose of all waste materials, hazardous substances, and recyclable materials in accordance with applicable law and Project requirements.

4.7 Reporting Requirements. Subcontractor shall submit all federal compliance documentation, including certified payrolls, prevailing wage certifications, and material certifications, within five (5) business days of the end of each week worked. Failure to provide timely documentation may result in work stoppage and withholding of payment.

4.8 Federal Flow-Down Clauses. The parties agree that the federal flow-down requirements contained in the prime contract between Contractor and the Texas Department of Transportation are incorporated herein by this reference and shall apply with equal force to Subcontractor's performance of the Work.

5. CHANGES

5.1 No changes to the scope of Work or the Contract Price shall be made except by written change order signed by authorized representatives of both Contractor and Subcontractor.

5.2 Subcontractor may submit a request for change order within five (5) business days of becoming aware of the condition requiring the change. The request shall include a detailed description of the change, the reason for the change, the impact on schedule, and a detailed cost estimate including labor hours at prevailing wage rates, materials with quotes, equipment costs, and overhead allocation.

5.3 Contractor shall review all change order requests within ten (10) business days and shall notify Subcontractor of approval, denial, or request for additional information.

5.4 No Work shall be performed pursuant to a change order request until a written change order executed by both parties has been issued. Subcontractor shall be entitled to no compensation for work performed outside the original scope without a prior executed change order.

5.5 The Contract Price shall be adjusted by the amount stated in the approved written change order, which shall specify any changes to the completion date.

6. WARRANTY

6.1 Subcontractor warrants that all Work shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards, Project specifications, and applicable law. All materials shall be new, of first quality, and properly installed.

6.2 Subcontractor warrants that all ductwork shall be properly sealed, air-tight, and free from leaks when tested in accordance with ASHRAE standards. Any leakage or deficiency shall be corrected by Subcontractor at no additional cost to Contractor.

6.3 Subcontractor warrants that the ductwork system shall be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of one (1) year from the date of final acceptance by Contractor. During this warranty period, Subcontractor shall repair or replace any defective work at no cost to Contractor.

6.4 The warranty shall be personal to Contractor and shall not pass to the Project owner or any other party unless expressly authorized by Subcontractor in writing.

7. INSURANCE

7.1 Subcontractor shall procure and maintain at its own expense comprehensive general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, covering bodily injury, property damage, and contractual liability.

7.2 Subcontractor shall maintain workers' compensation insurance as required by the State of Texas, with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident for all employees. Subcontractor shall carry employers' liability insurance with limits of not less than $500,000 each accident.

7.3 Subcontractor shall maintain Commercial Automobile Liability insurance covering all vehicles used in connection with the Work, with combined single limit coverage of not less than $1,000,000.

7.4 All insurance policies shall be issued by insurers rated A-:VII or better by A.M. Best's Guide. All policies shall be primary and non-contributory with respect to any insurance carried by Contractor and shall include a waiver of subrogation in favor of Contractor.

7.5 Contractor shall be named as an additional insured on all liability policies. Subcontractor shall provide certificates of insurance prior to commencing any Work and shall provide renewal certificates at least ten (10) days prior to expiration of existing policies. Failure to maintain required insurance shall be grounds for suspension of work and termination of this Agreement.

7.6 Subcontractor shall require all suppliers and lower-tier contractors to maintain insurance comparable to that required of Subcontractor hereunder.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Contractor, the Texas Department of Transportation, the Federal Highway Administration, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents (collectively, the "Indemnitees") against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorney's fees and court costs, arising out of or resulting from: (a) the performance or failure to perform the Work; (b) any breach of this Agreement by Subcontractor; (c) injury to any person or damage to any property caused by Subcontractor or its employees; (d) any violation of law by Subcontractor; or (e) any act or omission of Subcontractor or its agents, employees, or suppliers.

8.2 Subcontractor shall defend the Indemnitees in any action arising from the foregoing at Subcontractor's sole cost and expense. Contractor shall have the right to select counsel of its choice, and Subcontractor shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses thereof.

8.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Subcontractor shall not be obligated to indemnify Contractor for losses, damages, or claims arising solely from the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or the Indemnitees, excluding any comparative negligence or shared responsibility of Subcontractor.

8.4 Subcontractor's indemnification obligations shall not be limited by the amount of insurance carried by Subcontractor or by any other limitation of liability contained in this Agreement.

9. SCHEDULE AND TIME

9.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Work in a manner that maintains the Project schedule. The anticipated start date is October 15, 2024, with substantial completion by December 15, 2024.

9.2 Time is of the essence in this Agreement. Failure to maintain schedule shall be grounds for termination and completion of the Work by Contractor at Subcontractor's expense.

9.3 Subcontractor shall cooperate fully with other trades and shall schedule its work to avoid conflicts and delays. Subcontractor shall attend all Project coordination meetings held by Contractor and shall participate in trade coordination.

9.4 Delays caused by Subcontractor shall not entitle Subcontractor to time extensions. Delays caused by Contractor or other contractors, if approved in writing by the Project engineer, may result in time extensions but shall not entitle Subcontractor to additional compensation except as provided by change order.

10. BACKCHARGES

10.1 Contractor may assess back charges against Subcontractor for any of the following: (a) costs of correcting defective or non-conforming Work; (b) costs of removing and re-installing Work performed improperly; (c) costs incurred due to Subcontractor's failure to coordinate with other trades; (d) damage to Project or property caused by Subcontractor; (e) costs of cleaning up Work areas left in unsatisfactory condition; or (f) any other costs incurred by Contractor as a result of Subcontractor's breach.

10.2 Back charges shall be deducted from the next monthly progress payment or final payment due to Subcontractor. Subcontractor shall have five (5) business days after notice of a proposed back charge to dispute the same in writing with detailed explanation. Failure to timely dispute shall constitute acceptance of the back charge.

10.3 Back charges shall not be subject to any limitation period and may be assessed against final payment or retained amounts.

11. MECHANICS' LIEN AND LIEN WAIVERS

11.1 Subcontractor, for itself and for its subcontractors, employees, agents, suppliers, and material suppliers, covenants and agrees that no mechanic's liens, stop notices, or claims shall be filed or maintained against the Project, the Project site, or any improvements thereon on account of any work, labor, materials, services, or equipment performed or furnished by Subcontractor.

11.2 Prior to each progress payment, Subcontractor shall provide a conditional lien waiver waiving all mechanic's lien rights for Work and materials incorporated through the date of the invoice.

11.3 Prior to final payment, Subcontractor shall provide an unconditional final lien waiver releasing all mechanic's lien rights and claims in connection with the Work. Final payment shall not be made unless a satisfactory final lien waiver is provided.

11.4 Subcontractor shall ensure that all material suppliers and lower-tier contractors provide evidence that their employees and suppliers have been paid and have released all lien rights.

12. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

12.1 Contractor shall have the right to inspect all Work at any time. All Work shall be subject to inspection by the Project engineer and Contractor representatives.

12.2 Any Work found to be non-conforming with Project specifications or this Agreement shall be rejected and shall be corrected or removed and replaced by Subcontractor at Subcontractor's sole cost, with no adjustment to the Contract Price or schedule.

12.3 Final acceptance of the Work shall occur only when Subcontractor has corrected all deficiencies noted by Contractor and the Project engineer, has performed all required testing and commissioning, and has provided all required documentation and certifications.

12.4 Acceptance of the Work by Contractor or the Project owner shall not constitute a waiver of Subcontractor's obligations or Contractor's rights under the warranty provisions of Section 6.

13. SAFETY

13.1 Subcontractor shall be solely responsible for safety of its operations and for compliance with all OSHA requirements and applicable safety laws. Subcontractor shall establish and maintain a safe job site, provide appropriate safeguards, and ensure all workers wear required personal protective equipment.

13.2 Subcontractor shall immediately report any accidents, injuries, or near-misses to Contractor and shall cooperate fully with any investigation.

13.3 Subcontractor shall remove any worker from the site if such worker is found to be working in an unsafe manner or in violation of safety requirements.

14. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE

14.1 Contractor may terminate this Agreement and all of Subcontractor's right to further payment if: (a) Subcontractor fails to maintain the required insurance; (b) Subcontractor fails to pay prevailing wages or comply with federal requirements; (c) Subcontractor abandons the Work; (d) Subcontractor fails to correct defective Work after reasonable notice and opportunity to cure; (e) Subcontractor files for bankruptcy or becomes insolvent; or (f) any other material breach of this Agreement by Subcontractor.

14.2 Upon termination, Contractor may complete the Work using other contractors, and Subcontractor shall be liable for all costs exceeding the Contract Price, including overhead, profit, and delay costs.

14.3 Contractor shall provide written notice of termination and shall allow five (5) business days to cure, except in cases of abandonment or safety violations, which shall be immediately effective.

15. GOVERNING LAW

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflicts of law principles. All disputes arising from this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in Harris County, Texas.

15.2 Subcontractor waives any objection to venue and submits to the jurisdiction of such courts.

16. GENERAL PROVISIONS

16.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements. No modification shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

16.2 Subcontractor shall not assign this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Contractor, which consent may be withheld in Contractor's sole discretion.

16.3 Subcontractor shall not subcontract any portion of the Work without prior written approval from Contractor.

16.4 Subcontractor shall be an independent contractor and not an employee of Contractor. Subcontractor is responsible for all payroll deductions, tax withholdings, and statutory contributions.

16.5 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
