SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

THIS SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT (this "Subcontract") is made and entered into effective as of the 3rd day of September, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between TITAN PEAK BUILDERS, INC., a Texas corporation with offices located at 2200 North Loop West, Houston, Texas 77018 ("Contractor"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company with offices located at 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, Texas 77041 ("Subcontractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Contractor has been awarded a prime contract by the Texas Department of Transportation ("Owner") for the FM 1960 Interchange Maintenance Facility project, being a federal-aid project located in Harris County, Texas (the "Project"); and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires to retain Subcontractor to perform certain plumbing and HVAC work on the Project on the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements hereinafter contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Contractor and Subcontractor agree as follows:

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Definitions. As used in this Subcontract, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:

"Changes" means any modification to the Scope of Work, whether constituting an addition, deletion, or other alteration to the Work, including changes in the means, methods, sequences, or procedures for performing the Work, or changes in the time for performance.

"Contract Documents" means, collectively, this Subcontract, all exhibits hereto (including Exhibit C, the FHWA-1273 form), the prime contract plans and specifications, all modifications, amendments, change orders, and addenda thereto.

"Day" or "Days" means calendar days unless otherwise expressly stated as "working days" or "business days."

"FHWA-1273" means the Federal-Aid Highway Program Project Labor Agreement form, as attached as Exhibit C and required by federal law for projects receiving federal-aid highway funds.

"Plans and Specifications" means the final construction documents, including all plans, specifications, details, schedules, and technical standards prepared for the Project by the Owner's design professionals, as may be amended or supplemented from time to time.

"Subcontract Price" means the total contract price of One Million One Hundred Eighty-Four Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($1,184,500.00), subject to adjustment as provided herein.

"Work" means all labor, materials, equipment, services, and other items necessary to complete the plumbing and HVAC work as more particularly described in Section 2 hereof.

1.2 Interpretation. The headings and captions in this Subcontract are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation hereof. References to days shall mean calendar days unless otherwise noted. Use of the term "including" shall mean "including, but not limited to." This Subcontract shall be interpreted according to its fair meaning and not strictly construed for or against either party based upon who drafted it.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK

2.1 General Scope. Subcontractor shall perform all labor, furnish all materials and equipment, and provide all services necessary to complete all plumbing and HVAC work required for the FM 1960 Interchange Maintenance Facility project, including but not limited to the following:

(a) All rough-in plumbing installation, including water supply lines, drainage systems, vent stacks, and related piping, sized and installed in accordance with the Plans and Specifications and all applicable codes;

(b) All finished plumbing work, including installation of fixtures, faucets, traps, cleanouts, and all other plumbing appurtenances;

(c) All HVAC equipment installation, including furnace, air conditioning units, heat pump systems, ductwork, thermostats, controls, and related equipment;

(d) All testing, balancing, and commissioning of mechanical systems to achieve design parameters and Owner approval;

(e) All connections to main water, sewer, gas, and electrical services as required;

(f) All removal and disposal of packaging materials and construction debris generated by Subcontractor's work;

(g) All warranties, manuals, and closeout documentation for all installed equipment and systems; and

(h) All other work reasonably implied by the Plans and Specifications or necessary to provide a complete, fully functional, and code-compliant plumbing and HVAC installation.

2.2 Quality Standards. All Work shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards and best practices. All materials shall be new, of good quality, and of the grades, types, and specifications called for in the Contract Documents. All work shall be performed in full compliance with the Plans and Specifications, all applicable building codes, plumbing codes, mechanical codes, electrical codes, and all other applicable laws, regulations, and ordinances.

2.3 Coordination with Other Trades. Subcontractor shall coordinate with all other trades and contractors at the Project site, including electrical contractors, general laborers, and other mechanical contractors. Subcontractor shall be responsible for scheduling of its own work to accommodate the overall Project schedule and shall cooperate with Contractor in addressing any scheduling conflicts or coordination issues. Subcontractor shall be responsible for protecting its own installed Work from damage caused by other trades and shall immediately notify Contractor of any damage to previously installed Work.

2.4 Project Site Conditions. Subcontractor acknowledges that it has visited the Project site, is familiar with the general site conditions, topography, access routes, and existing structures, and understands the scope and nature of the work required. Subcontractor has had the opportunity to review the Contract Documents and all related information necessary to make an accurate bid.

2.5 Permits and Licenses. Subcontractor shall maintain, at its own expense, all valid plumbing and HVAC licenses and certifications required by the State of Texas and Harris County to perform the Work. Subcontractor shall be responsible for obtaining and paying for any trade-specific permits required to perform its work, unless such permits are specifically included in Contractor's general permits.

SECTION 3. SUBCONTRACT PRICE

3.1 Total Price. The total Subcontract Price for full performance of all Work as described in Section 2 hereof shall be One Million One Hundred Eighty-Four Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($1,184,500.00), plus applicable Texas sales tax if not included in this lump sum. This price shall be firm and shall not be subject to adjustment except as expressly provided in Section 4 (Changes) or as otherwise agreed in writing by both parties.

Firm Price. The Subcontract Price is firm and shall not be increased on account of any increase in the cost of labor, materials, equipment, taxes, duties, tariffs or other charges, whether or not foreseeable, except to the extent Contractor actually recovers a corresponding increase from the Owner under the Prime Contract, and then only in the amount so recovered.

3.2 Price Includes All Costs. The Subcontract Price includes all costs, expenses, overhead, profit, labor, materials, equipment, mobilization, demobilization, insurance, bonding, permits, licenses, testing, inspections, warranties, contingencies, and all other costs necessary to complete the Work in accordance with the Contract Documents and all applicable requirements. No additional compensation shall be due to Subcontractor except as expressly provided herein for authorized Changes.

3.3 Breakdown of Pricing. For purposes of evaluating and authorizing Changes and for other administrative purposes, Subcontractor has provided a detailed cost breakdown by trade category as follows:

Rough Plumbing (Materials & Labor):          $287,000.00
Finished Plumbing (Materials & Labor):       $156,500.00
HVAC Equipment and Installation:             $598,000.00
Testing, Balancing & Commissioning:          $89,500.00
Closeout Documentation & Warranties:         $53,500.00

Total:                                      $1,184,500.00

Contractor shall use this breakdown solely for purposes of evaluating Change orders and shall not be bound by any allocation of costs among categories. Any Change order shall be priced based on the actual cost of the change plus Subcontractor's reasonable overhead and profit, as agreed between the parties.

SECTION 4. PAYMENT

4.1 Invoicing and Payment Schedule. Upon completion of portions of Work monthly or at such intervals as Contractor may approve, Subcontractor shall submit to Contractor a request for payment on AIA Form G702 and G703, or such other form as Contractor may require, documenting the Work completed during the billing period, materials stored on site, and other costs incurred. All invoices shall include sufficient documentation to substantiate the amounts claimed, including photographs, material delivery receipts, payroll records, and equipment usage logs, as reasonably requested by Contractor.

4.2 Payment Timing. Contractor shall make payment to Subcontractor within ten (10) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner for the Work performed by Subcontractor. This is a "pay-when-paid" provision, and Subcontractor acknowledges that Contractor's obligation to pay is expressly conditioned upon and dependent upon Contractor's receipt of payment from Owner. Subcontractor waives any right to interest or penalties due to delayed payment until such time as Contractor has been paid by Owner for the work in question. Subcontractor's sole remedy for non-payment by Owner shall be to proceed against Contractor's payment bond or to file a claim directly against Owner, if available.

4.3 Retainage. Contractor shall retain ten percent (10%) of each progress payment to Subcontractor until final completion and acceptance of the Work. Retainage shall be held by Contractor as security for Subcontractor's performance and for the satisfaction of any claims, liens, or obligations arising from or related to the Work. The retainage shall be paid to Subcontractor within thirty (30) days after final acceptance of the Work and satisfaction of all of the conditions set forth in Section 4.4 hereof.

4.4 Conditions Precedent to Final Payment. Final payment to Subcontractor shall not become due and payable until all of the following express conditions precedent have been satisfied:

(a) Completion and acceptance of all Work by Contractor and Owner in accordance with the Contract Documents;

(b) Provision by Subcontractor of evidence satisfactory to Contractor that there are no outstanding claims, obligations, or liens for labor, services, materials, equipment, wages, taxes, utilities, or other items in connection with the Work, including, if requested by Contractor, written waivers of mechanic's liens from all subcontractors, suppliers, and material vendors engaged by Subcontractor;

(c) Execution and delivery by Subcontractor of a comprehensive release of all claims and liens in a form satisfactory to Contractor, running to and in favor of both Contractor and Owner;

(d) Delivery of all required warranties, operation manuals, spare parts, and closeout documentation;

(e) Correction of all punch list items and deficiencies identified by Contractor or Owner;

(f) Final testing, balancing, and certification that all mechanical systems are operating in accordance with design specifications;

(g) Demonstration that all prevailing wage requirements have been satisfied, if applicable, including submission of certified payroll records; and

(h) Complete satisfaction of all claims, demands, disputes, obligations, and responsibilities of Subcontractor arising out of or related to this Subcontract, whether between Contractor and Subcontractor or between Subcontractor and any third party.

4.5 Consequences of Claims or Liens. If any claim, obligation, lien, or unsatisfied obligation arises from or is related to the Work, whether prior to or after final payment is made, Subcontractor shall pay, refund, or deliver to Contractor all monies that Owner and/or Contractor shall pay in satisfying, discharging, defending against, or resolving any such claim, obligation, lien, or any action brought thereon, together with all costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and disbursements, incurred in connection therewith. Additionally, Subcontractor shall pay to Contractor such amounts as Contractor shall, in its sole discretion, determine to be necessary to protect Contractor and Owner. In lieu of such payment, Subcontractor may, at Contractor's sole discretion, deliver a surety bond satisfactory to Contractor and Owner. Any such refund or payment shall be made by Subcontractor within ten (10) days of request by Contractor.

4.6 Right of Set-Off. Contractor shall have the right to set-off and deduct from any payment due to Subcontractor any sums owed by Subcontractor to Contractor, including but not limited to costs of correcting deficient work, costs of defending against liens or claims, costs of remedying damaged property, or any other amounts for which Subcontractor is obligated under the Subcontract. Such set-off shall not constitute a waiver of any other rights or remedies available to Contractor.

4.7 No Waiver by Payment. Payment by Contractor shall not constitute acceptance of deficient or non-conforming Work, nor shall it constitute a waiver of any of Contractor's rights under this Subcontract or under law. Contractor reserves all rights to pursue claims against Subcontractor for deficient Work, breach of contract, or other breaches, regardless of whether partial payments have been made.

SECTION 5. CHANGES

5.1 Change Order Procedure. No Change to the Scope of Work shall be undertaken by Subcontractor except upon written authorization from Contractor in the form of a written Change order, amendment, or other written directive executed by an authorized representative of Contractor. Verbal or telephone instructions shall not constitute authorization for Changes and shall not entitle Subcontractor to additional compensation.

5.2 Pricing of Changes. Any Change order shall include a detailed description of the change, the reason for the change, the impact on the schedule, and the cost of the change. Subcontractor shall submit proposed pricing for any Change order within five (5) business days of notification, with supporting documentation including material costs, labor hours, equipment costs, and overhead and profit allocation. Contractor shall have the right to accept, reject, or negotiate the proposed Change order pricing. No payment shall be due for any Change unless and until a written Change order has been executed by both parties.

5.3 Schedule Impact. Subcontractor shall identify in all Change order requests the impact, if any, on the Project schedule, including any impact to the completion date or to the schedule for other trades. Subcontractor shall not be entitled to a schedule extension unless the Change is directed by Contractor and cannot reasonably be accomplished within the existing schedule as a result of factors not within Subcontractor's control.

5.4 Constructive Changes. Subcontractor shall notify Contractor promptly if Subcontractor believes that any directive, condition, or circumstance constitutes an unauthorized change to the Work. Failure to provide such notice shall constitute a waiver of any claim for a constructive Change. Subcontractor shall continue performing the Work as directed while pursuing any claim for a constructive Change through the procedures set forth herein.

SECTION 6. SCHEDULE

6.1 Completion Date. Subcontractor shall complete all Work by December 15, 2024, unless extended by written Change order as provided in Section 5 hereof. Time is of the essence in this Subcontract, and failure to meet the scheduled completion date shall be a material breach.

6.2 Detailed Schedule. Within five (5) days of execution of this Subcontract, Subcontractor shall submit to Contractor a detailed construction schedule showing the sequence of Work, the duration of each phase, labor and equipment deployment, critical path activities, and the relationship of Subcontractor's work to other trades. The schedule shall be in a format acceptable to Contractor, typically in Microsoft Project or similar software.

6.3 Schedule Compliance. Subcontractor shall perform the Work in accordance with the approved schedule. Subcontractor shall maintain adequate labor and equipment at the Project site to sustain the scheduled rate of progress. If Subcontractor falls behind schedule through no fault of Contractor, Subcontractor shall, at its own expense, add additional labor and equipment as necessary to regain the scheduled progress without additional compensation from Contractor.

6.4 Delay Damages. Subcontractor acknowledges that delays in performance may cause significant damages to Contractor and Owner, and that such damages are difficult or impossible to calculate with precision. Accordingly, if Subcontractor fails to complete the Work by the scheduled completion date (or any extended date as authorized in writing), Contractor shall be entitled to recover liquidated damages from Subcontractor in the amount of One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00) per calendar day of delay, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%) of the Subcontract Price. Subcontractor waives any claim that such liquidated damages are a penalty, acknowledging that they represent a reasonable pre-estimate of the actual damages likely to result from delay.

6.5 No Delays to Other Trades. Subcontractor shall not delay or interfere with the work of any other trades or contractors at the Project site. Subcontractor shall schedule its work to coordinate with other trades and shall work around the schedules of other contractors. Delays caused by Subcontractor shall not extend the time for completion.

SECTION 7. INSURANCE

7.1 Types and Amounts of Insurance. Subcontractor shall obtain and maintain, at its sole cost and expense, throughout the term of this Subcontract and for any extended warranty periods, the following insurance coverage:

(a) Workers' Compensation Insurance in compliance with the statutory requirements of the State of Texas, with limits not less than the minimum required by law, including an endorsement waiving the right of subrogation in favor of Contractor;

(b) Commercial General Liability Insurance covering bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury, with a combined single limit of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per occurrence and Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) annual aggregate;

(c) Automobile Liability Insurance covering owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles, with a combined single limit of not less than Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00) per occurrence;

(d) Tools and Equipment Insurance covering all Subcontractor's tools and equipment at the Project site, with replacement value coverage; and

(e) HVAC Equipment and Performance Bond in the amount of not less than Ten Percent (10%) of the Subcontract Price, guaranteeing the performance and functionality of all HVAC equipment and systems installed by Subcontractor for a minimum period of one (1) year following substantial completion.

7.2 Additional Insured. Contractor and Owner shall be named as additional insureds on all liability policies required under Section 7.1. The insurance shall provide primary coverage to Contractor and Owner without right of contribution from any insurance carried by Contractor or Owner. Certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage shall be provided to Contractor prior to commencing any Work at the Project site.

7.3 Insurance Requirements for Subcontractors. Subcontractor shall require all of its sub-subcontractors and material suppliers to maintain insurance coverage substantially similar to that required herein, with Contractor and Owner named as additional insureds. Subcontractor shall require all sub-subcontractors to provide certificates of insurance to Subcontractor and Contractor prior to commencing work.

7.4 No Limitation of Liability. The insurance required by this Section shall not be construed as limiting Subcontractor's indemnification obligations or liability hereunder. The limits of insurance shall not limit Subcontractor's obligations to indemnify and defend Contractor and Owner.

SECTION 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Broad-Form Indemnity. Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Contractor, Owner, and their respective officers, directors, employees, agents, and representatives from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees, court costs, and expert fees) arising out of or related to:

(a) Any bodily injury, death, or property damage caused by Subcontractor's negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law;

(b) Any failure of Subcontractor to comply with the terms of this Subcontract or applicable law;

(c) Any defective or non-conforming Work performed by Subcontractor;

(d) Any liens, claims, or obligations arising from or related to labor, materials, or services furnished by Subcontractor;

(e) Any damage to property caused by Subcontractor's operations or by the Work performed by Subcontractor; and

(f) Any environmental contamination or hazardous materials brought to the Project site or released by Subcontractor in connection with the Work.

8.2 Scope and Extent. The indemnity contained in Section 8.1 shall apply to the fullest extent permitted by law, including indemnification for the sole negligence of Contractor or Owner to the extent not prohibited by Section 8.3 below. Subcontractor's obligation to indemnify shall be primary and shall not be limited by the amount of insurance maintained by Subcontractor or by any insurance carried by Contractor or Owner.

8.3 Savings Clause. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in Section 8.1 or 8.2, if applicable law limits the scope of indemnification that may be required from Subcontractor, then the indemnification obligations contained herein shall be limited to the maximum extent permitted under such applicable law. To the extent that Chapter 151 of the Texas Insurance Code or any other applicable law prohibits indemnification for the sole negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or Owner, such indemnification is expressly excluded from the scope of indemnification required hereunder. Subcontractor shall be required to indemnify only to the extent permitted by law.

8.4 Defense. Subcontractor shall defend Contractor and Owner against any claim, suit, or action at Subcontractor's sole cost, including the retention and payment of defense counsel selected by Contractor. The defense obligation shall arise immediately upon notice of a claim and shall continue throughout the pendency of the claim, regardless of whether the claim is ultimately determined to be covered under the indemnity.

8.5 Subcontractor's Own Negligence. Subcontractor expressly acknowledges and agrees that the indemnification obligations contained herein are intended to apply even in cases where the claim arises in whole or in part from the negligence of Contractor or Owner, to the extent permitted by law. This represents the intent of the parties to allocate risk to Subcontractor as consideration for the Subcontract Price.

SECTION 9. FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS

9.1 Federal-Aid Project. Subcontractor acknowledges that this Project is funded in whole or in part with federal-aid highway funds and is therefore subject to all federal requirements applicable to such projects. Subcontractor shall comply with all such federal requirements, including but not limited to those set forth in the Contract Documents and in the FHWA-1273 Project Labor Agreement attached as Exhibit C.

9.2 Project Labor Agreement. Subcontractor shall be bound by and shall comply fully with the Project Labor Agreement (FHWA-1273) attached hereto as Exhibit C. The Project Labor Agreement is hereby incorporated by reference as if fully set forth herein and shall be physically incorporated into the Contract Documents. Subcontractor shall execute the Project Labor Agreement within three (3) days of execution of this Subcontract if it has not already done so.

9.3 Prevailing Wage Requirements. All laborers and mechanics employed by Subcontractor in connection with the Work shall be paid prevailing wages as determined by the Texas Department of Transportation and/or the federal Department of Labor, as applicable. Subcontractor shall maintain certified payroll records showing the name, classification, hours worked, and wages paid to each employee, and shall provide such records to Contractor and Owner upon request. Failure to pay prevailing wages shall constitute material breach and shall result in liability for wage underpayment plus penalties and interest.

9.4 Buy America Requirements. All steel and iron products incorporated into the Work shall comply with the Buy America requirements of the federal highway program, as applicable. Subcontractor shall ensure that all manufactured steel and iron used in the plumbing and HVAC systems originate from the United States and shall provide certifications of compliance upon request. Any non-compliant materials shall be removed and replaced at Subcontractor's sole cost.

9.5 Civil Rights Compliance. Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable federal civil rights laws and regulations, including Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, as amended, and shall not discriminate in the hiring, promotion, or treatment of employees based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or other protected status. Subcontractor shall include this requirement in all subcontracts and agreements.

9.6 Debarment and Suspension. Subcontractor certifies and warrants that it is not debarred, suspended, or otherwise excluded from participation in federal contracts or programs. Subcontractor shall immediately notify Contractor if Subcontractor becomes aware of any debarment, suspension, or exclusion action.

9.7 Audit Rights. Subcontractor shall maintain all records related to the Work and shall make such records available for audit by Contractor, Owner, and any federal or state agency authorized to conduct such audit. Subcontractor shall retain all records for a minimum of three (3) years following final payment.

SECTION 10. TERMINATION

10.1 Termination for Cause. Contractor may terminate this Subcontract and the employment of Subcontractor immediately upon written notice if any of the following conditions exist:

(a) Subcontractor fails to pay prevailing wages as required;

(b) Subcontractor fails to maintain required insurance or bonding;

(c) Subcontractor abandons the Work or fails to provide adequate labor and equipment to sustain scheduled progress;

(d) Subcontractor becomes insolvent, bankrupt, or unable to pay its debts as they become due;

(e) Subcontractor fails to correct deficient Work within forty-eight (48) hours of notice from Contractor;

(f) Subcontractor violates any applicable law or regulation in performing the Work;

(g) Subcontractor breaches any material provision of this Subcontract and fails to cure such breach within seven (7) days of written notice; or

(h) Subcontractor's work is unsafe or creates a hazard to persons or property at the Project site.

10.2 Termination for Convenience. Contractor may terminate this Subcontract for convenience, without cause, upon seven (7) days' written notice to Subcontractor. In the event of termination for convenience, Contractor shall pay Subcontractor for all Work properly performed through the date of termination, plus reasonable demobilization costs, provided that Subcontractor shall not be entitled to recovery of lost profit on unperformed Work.

10.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Subcontractor shall immediately:

(a) Cease all Work at the Project site;

(b) Remove all tools, equipment, and materials belonging to Subcontractor from the Project site within forty-eight (48) hours or such other period as Contractor may allow;

(c) Cooperate with Contractor and any successor contractor in understanding the status of the Work and transitioning Work to others;

(d) Deliver all keys, access cards, drawings, and documentation to Contractor; and

(e) Secure and protect any Work-in-progress to prevent damage or deterioration.

10.4 Claim Limitations. Subcontractor's sole remedy upon termination shall be payment for Work performed and demobilization costs as set forth above. Subcontractor waives any claim for lost profit, delay damages, disruption costs, or other consequential damages resulting from termination.

SECTION 11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 Texas Law. This Subcontract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles. The provisions of the Texas Construction Liability Act and other applicable Texas law regarding construction disputes shall apply.

11.2 Venue. Subcontractor consents to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Harris County, Texas, and shall not object to venue in such courts. Subcontractor waives any claim of inconvenient forum.

11.3 Dispute Resolution. Prior to initiating any legal action, the parties shall attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation between the project manager of Contractor and an authorized representative of Subcontractor. If negotiation is unsuccessful, the parties may pursue mediation or arbitration as provided in the prime contract, if applicable. All cost-sharing provisions for alternative dispute resolution shall be borne equally by Contractor and Subcontractor unless otherwise agreed.

SECTION 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Representations and Warranties. Subcontractor represents and warrants to Contractor that:

(a) Subcontractor is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the State of Texas;

(b) Subcontractor has the authority to enter into and perform this Subcontract;

(c) Subcontractor has reviewed the Contract Documents and all applicable requirements and understands the scope and nature of the Work;

(d) All information provided by Subcontractor in its bid is true, accurate, and complete;

(e) Subcontractor possesses all necessary licenses, certifications, and qualifications to perform the Work;

(f) There are no liens, claims, or encumbrances on Subcontractor's assets or business that would impair its ability to perform; and

(g) Subcontractor has adequate financial resources and bonding capacity to perform all obligations under this Subcontract.

12.2 Independent Contractor. Subcontractor is an independent contractor and is not an employee of Contractor. Subcontractor shall be solely responsible for all payroll taxes, workers' compensation insurance, and other employment-related obligations for its employees.

12.3 Assignment and Subcontracting. Subcontractor shall not assign or transfer any rights or obligations under this Subcontract without the prior written consent of Contractor. Any assignment or transfer without such consent shall be void and shall constitute grounds for immediate termination. Subcontractor shall be fully responsible and liable to Contractor for the performance of any sub-subcontractors retained by Subcontractor.

12.4 Notices. Any notice required under this Subcontract shall be in writing and shall be delivered in person, sent by overnight courier, or sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble to this Subcontract. Notice shall be deemed received upon delivery or upon the date shown on the return receipt.

12.5 Safety and OSHA Compliance. Subcontractor shall implement and maintain a comprehensive safety program in compliance with the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) standards, Texas safety standards, and all other applicable safety laws and regulations. Subcontractor shall ensure that all employees receive proper safety training and shall immediately correct any unsafe conditions. Subcontractor shall be solely responsible for safety at the Project site, including the provision of safety equipment, training, and supervision.

12.6 Environmental Compliance. Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local environmental laws and regulations, including the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, and Texas Environmental Quality Act. Subcontractor shall properly manage, store, and dispose of all hazardous materials, and shall immediately report any environmental incident to Contractor and Owner.

12.7 Site Conduct. Subcontractor's employees and agents shall conduct themselves in a professional manner while at the Project site. Subcontractor shall not allow any employee to report to work in an impaired condition, shall not permit alcohol or illegal drugs at the Project site, and shall ensure that all employees comply with Owner's site rules and access requirements.

12.8 Inspection and Acceptance. Contractor and Owner, at any time and as often as deemed necessary, shall have the right to inspect all Work. Subcontractor shall cooperate fully with any inspections and shall provide access to all Work areas, materials, and records. Inspection by Contractor or Owner shall not constitute acceptance and shall not relieve Subcontractor of any obligation to perform quality work.

12.9 Warranty of Work. Subcontractor warrants that all Work shall be performed in a first-class, professional, and workmanlike manner, free from defects in materials and workmanship, and in full compliance with the Contract Documents and all applicable laws and standards. Subcontractor shall obtain and furnish manufacturer warranties for all equipment installed, and shall maintain Subcontractor's own warranty on all labor and installation for a period of one (1) year from the date of substantial completion, during which time Subcontractor shall correct any deficiencies at no additional cost to Contractor or Owner.

12.10 Entire Agreement. This Subcontract, together with the exhibits hereto and the Contract Documents by reference, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral. No modifications or amendments to this Subcontract shall be valid or binding unless executed in writing by both Contractor and Subcontractor.

12.11 Severability. If any provision of this Subcontract is held to be invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

12.12 Waiver. No waiver of any breach or default under this Subcontract shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom the waiver is sought. Failure of Contractor to insist upon performance of any obligation shall not constitute a waiver of that obligation or any other obligation, nor shall it prevent Contractor from enforcing such obligation at any later time.

12.13 Survival. The provisions of Sections 8 (Indemnification), 7 (Insurance), 12.9 (Warranty), and 12.14 (Limitation of Liability) shall survive the completion of the Work and the final payment to Subcontractor, and shall remain in full force and effect for any period expressly stated or for so long as any claim may be asserted.

12.14 Limitation of Liability. NOTWITHSTANDING ANYTHING TO THE CONTRARY IN THIS SUBCONTRACT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, OR LOST REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS SUBCONTRACT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. THE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIMITED TO DIRECT DAMAGES ACTUALLY SUSTAINED AND RECOVERABLE UNDER LAW. THIS LIMITATION SHALL NOT APPLY TO CLAIMS FOR INDEMNIFICATION, INSURANCE COVERAGE, OR BREACH OF INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS.

12.15 Authority of Representative. Contractor's Project Manager is authorized to act on behalf of Contractor in day-to-day project matters, including requests for Changes, approval of schedules, and inspection of Work. However, only an officer of Contractor may authorize Changes in writing, approve change order pricing, and modify the terms of this Subcontract.
