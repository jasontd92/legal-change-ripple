SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

THIS SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of June 9, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN MECHANICAL GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation doing business as Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air, with its principal place of business at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, California 95833 ("Contractor"), and EMBER & STONE INSULATION, LLC, a California limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 7440 Florin Mall Drive, Sacramento, California 95823 ("Subcontractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Contractor has entered into that certain Construction Agreement dated May 19, 2025 with Harborview Capitol Tower LLC, a California limited liability company ("Owner"), for the Capitol Tower Chiller Replacement Project located at 2650 Capitol Avenue, Sacramento, California 95816 (the "Prime Contract"), as amended; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Prime Contract, Contractor is obligated to perform certain mechanical piping and duct insulation work; and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires to engage Subcontractor to perform certain portions of such work, and Subcontractor is willing to undertake such work upon the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Contractor and Subcontractor agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INCORPORATION BY REFERENCE

1.1 The following capitalized terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:

"Contract Documents" means the Prime Contract, including all amendments, addenda, and exhibits thereto, plans and specifications prepared by Sierra Valley Engineering, Inc., applicable building codes and standards, and this Subcontract Agreement.

"Project" means the Capitol Tower Chiller Replacement Project, a comprehensive mechanical system replacement and upgrade at the Capitol Tower office complex in downtown Sacramento, including removal of the existing 500-ton centrifugal chiller, replacement with a new 400-ton magnetic bearing chiller, associated piping replacement, ductwork modifications, and all related insulation work.

"Work" means all labor, materials, equipment, and services required to be performed by Subcontractor under this Agreement, as more particularly described in Section 2 hereof and in the Contract Documents.

"Days" means calendar days unless otherwise specified as "business days."

1.2 The Prime Contract is hereby incorporated by reference as if fully set forth herein. In the event of any conflict between the Prime Contract and this Subcontract, this Subcontract shall control with respect to Subcontractor's obligations and rights. Subcontractor shall be bound by all terms, conditions, specifications, and requirements of the Prime Contract as they relate to Subcontractor's Work, including but not limited to the schedule, quality standards, safety requirements, and general conditions.

1.3 The following documents are also incorporated by reference and form a part of the Contract Documents: (a) the specifications dated April 15, 2025 prepared by Sierra Valley Engineering, Inc.; (b) the construction drawings sheets numbered M-1 through M-18 and HVAC-1 through HVAC-12, dated May 2, 2025; and (c) all addenda and change orders issued to Contractor by Owner through the date of this Agreement.

2. SCOPE OF WORK

2.1 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the following work in a professional and workmanlike manner:

2.1.1 Removal and disposal of all existing mineral fiber and rigid foam insulation from mechanical piping and ductwork associated with the existing chiller system, including removal from approximately 450 linear feet of 2-inch through 10-inch diameter piping and approximately 280 linear feet of ductwork ranging from 18 inches to 42 inches in equivalent diameter.

2.1.2 Installation of new high-performance closed-cell foam insulation on all chilled water piping, including supply, return, and condenser water lines, with a minimum thickness of 1.5 inches on all piping 3 inches and larger, and 1 inch on piping smaller than 3 inches, in accordance with ASHRAE 90.1-2019 standards.

2.1.3 Installation of new fiberglass duct insulation wrap, minimum 2 inches thick R-8 rated, with vapor barrier on all supply and return ductwork serving the chilled water fan coil units and air handling units.

2.1.4 Installation of auxiliary insulation and vapor barrier protection on all cold water condensation-prone surfaces, including pipe hangers, supports, and connection fittings.

2.1.5 Sealing all insulation joints and penetrations with approved closed-cell spray foam sealant and acrylic latex caulk in accordance with manufacturer specifications and building code requirements.

2.1.6 Cleanup and removal of all waste materials, packaging, and debris resulting from Subcontractor's work activities.

2.1.7 Coordination with Contractor and other trades performing concurrent work, including the chiller installation contractor and ductwork contractors.

2.1.8 Cooperation with inspections by Owner's mechanical engineer and the Sacramento Building and Fire Safety Division, including providing test reports and certifications as reasonably required.

2.2 Subcontractor shall perform all Work in strict compliance with the Contract Documents, all applicable federal, state, and local building codes and regulations, and the California Building Code (2022 Edition). All materials shall be new, first-quality products unless otherwise specifically approved in writing by Contractor and Owner.

2.3 The Work is estimated to require approximately four (4) weeks of on-site labor. Subcontractor shall provide the Contractor with a detailed work schedule within five (5) business days of the Effective Date of this Agreement, subject to Contractor's approval, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.

2.4 Subcontractor shall coordinate the commencement of its Work with Contractor's project superintendent, Edward Martinez, at telephone number (916) 555-0147. Subcontractor shall not commence Work until receiving written authorization from Contractor.

3. CONTRACT PRICE AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 The total Contract Price for all Work to be performed by Subcontractor shall be Two Hundred Fourteen Thousand Eight Hundred Dollars ($214,800.00) (the "Contract Price"), which price shall be fixed and shall not increase except upon written change order executed by both Contractor and Subcontractor in accordance with Section 9 of this Agreement.

3.2 The Contract Price is inclusive of and includes all costs for labor, materials, equipment, tools, waste disposal, permit fees, testing and inspections, insurance premiums, workers' compensation, payroll taxes, overhead, profit, and all other costs incurred in the complete performance of the Work, whether or not specifically enumerated herein.

3.3 The Contract Price shall be invoiced in accordance with the following schedule:

3.3.1 Upon execution of this Agreement and ten (10) days before commencing Work: ten percent (10%) in the amount of $21,480.00;

3.3.2 Upon completion of piping insulation and submission of progress documentation: forty percent (40%) in the amount of $85,920.00;

3.3.3 Upon completion of duct insulation and submission of test reports: thirty-five percent (35%) in the amount of $75,180.00;

3.3.4 Upon final completion, inspection approval, and submission of all certifications and closeout documentation: fifteen percent (15%) in the amount of $32,220.00.

3.4 Subcontractor shall submit invoices to Contractor at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, California 95833, Attention: Accounts Payable, with a copy to the Project Superintendent.

4. PAYMENT TERMS AND CONDITIONS

4.1 This Subcontract is a "pay-when-paid" contract. Subcontractor shall not be entitled to payment from Contractor until Contractor has received payment from the Owner for the corresponding portion of Work performed by Subcontractor. Contractor shall not be obligated to pay Subcontractor's invoice until Contractor has received the corresponding payment from Owner under the Prime Contract.

4.2 Notwithstanding the pay-when-paid condition, if payment is not received by Contractor from Owner within sixty (60) days after Contractor's submission of the corresponding progress payment request to Owner, Contractor shall, within five (5) business days thereafter, notify Subcontractor in writing of the nonpayment, provided that such notification shall not obligate Contractor to pay Subcontractor if Contractor has not yet received payment from Owner.

4.3 Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of Contractor's receipt of payment from Owner for the corresponding portion of the Work, unless Contractor is withholding payment due to disputed Work quality, incomplete Work, or noncompliance with the Contract Documents, in which case payment may be withheld pending resolution of the dispute or cure of the deficiency.

4.4 Contractor may withhold payment for any Work that does not comply with the Contract Documents, that is defective or deficient in quality, or that creates safety hazards. Contractor shall provide written notice specifying the nature of any deficiencies. Subcontractor shall have ten (10) days to cure such deficiencies, and Contractor may perform such corrective work and deduct the reasonable costs thereof from the Contract Price or from any amounts due to Subcontractor.

4.5 Contractor shall have the right to offset against any amounts due to Subcontractor any costs incurred by Contractor to correct deficient Work, any damages caused by Subcontractor's Work or conduct, or any costs resulting from Subcontractor's failure to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or Contract Documents.

4.6 Any failure by Contractor to pay an invoice shall not constitute a material breach of this Agreement if such failure results from Contractor's non-receipt of payment from Owner, and in such event, the time for Contractor's performance obligations shall be extended accordingly.

5. SCHEDULE

5.1 Subcontractor shall commence Work on or about July 7, 2025, and shall achieve Substantial Completion of all Work by August 18, 2025, unless otherwise modified by written change order or delay notice.

5.2 Subcontractor shall maintain a detailed schedule prepared in Microsoft Project or equivalent project management software, updated weekly and submitted to Contractor every Friday. The schedule shall identify all critical path activities, long-lead material deliveries, and coordination points with other trades.

5.3 Subcontractor shall coordinate its work schedule with Contractor's other subcontractors, including Pinnacle Mechanical, Inc. (chiller installation), Superior Ductwork Solutions (ductwork modification), and various site trades. Subcontractor shall attend weekly coordination meetings held each Tuesday at 10:00 a.m. at the Project site.

5.4 If Subcontractor causes delay to the Project critical path or other work activities, Subcontractor shall be liable to Contractor for all consequential costs and delays incurred by Contractor and other subcontractors, including extended overhead, remobilization, and extended rental equipment costs.

5.5 Time is of the essence in this Agreement. Subcontractor shall not be excused from performance except for delays caused by Contractor, Owner, or events of Force Majeure as defined in Section 11.6 hereof.

5.6 No extensions of time shall be granted unless Subcontractor submits a written request to Contractor within ten (10) days of the event causing the delay, with detailed justification and impact analysis. Contractor's approval of a time extension shall not obligate Contractor to pay Subcontractor for additional costs unless expressly provided in a written change order.

6. INSURANCE

6.1 Before commencing any Work, Subcontractor shall obtain and maintain in full force and effect the following insurance coverage:

6.1.1 Workers' Compensation Insurance with statutory limits as required by California law, including Employers Liability coverage with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence.

6.1.2 Commercial General Liability Insurance with combined single limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate, covering bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury.

6.1.3 Commercial Automobile Liability Insurance with combined single limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence, covering all vehicles used in connection with the Work.

6.1.4 Tools and Equipment Coverage or Inland Marine Insurance covering all tools, equipment, and materials owned or controlled by Subcontractor, with replacement cost coverage of not less than $75,000.

6.2 All insurance policies except Workers' Compensation shall name Contractor and Owner as additional insureds. All policies shall be with insurance companies rated A-:VII or better by A.M. Best and authorized to do business in California.

6.3 Subcontractor shall provide certificates of insurance evidencing all required coverage to Contractor no later than June 30, 2025 and shall maintain continuous coverage throughout the duration of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years after Substantial Completion of the Work.

6.4 All certificates of insurance shall state that coverage will not be cancelled or materially modified without thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Contractor.

6.5 Subcontractor's failure to obtain or maintain required insurance shall not limit Subcontractor's indemnification obligations to Contractor or Owner, and Contractor may obtain such insurance at Subcontractor's expense.

6.6 Subcontractor shall be responsible for all deductibles and self-insured retentions associated with its insurance policies.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 To the fullest extent permitted by California Civil Code Section 2782.05, Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend with counsel approved by Contractor, and hold harmless Contractor and Owner, and their respective officers, directors, employees, agents, and representatives from and against all claims, damages, liability, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of defense, arising out of or caused by:

7.1.1 Subcontractor's performance or failure to perform the Work;

7.1.2 Bodily injury, death, or property damage caused by Subcontractor's acts, omissions, negligence, or breach of this Agreement;

7.1.3 Defects in materials or workmanship provided by Subcontractor;

7.1.4 Subcontractor's violation of applicable laws, codes, or regulations;

7.1.5 Damage to the Project or other property caused by Subcontractor's Work;

7.1.6 Bodily injury or property damage to Subcontractor's employees or agents caused by Subcontractor's negligence or failure to provide a safe work environment.

7.2 Subcontractor's indemnification obligation shall not extend to claims arising solely from the negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or Owner that is not comparative to or contributed to by Subcontractor's acts or omissions, except to the extent required by California Civil Code Section 2782.05.

7.3 Subcontractor shall defend Contractor and Owner in any action or proceeding arising out of claims indemnified hereby, regardless of whether such claims arise from comparative negligence or strict liability, to the extent permitted by California law.

7.4 The indemnity provisions herein shall survive the completion of the Work and the termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

8. QUALITY OF WORK, CLEANUP, AND WARRANTY

8.1 Subcontractor warrants that all Work will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner in full compliance with all Contract Documents, applicable codes, and industry standards. All materials supplied by Subcontractor shall be new, first-quality products and shall conform to specifications.

8.2 At all times, Subcontractor shall keep the Project site free from accumulation of waste materials and rubbish caused by it or by any of its employees, agents, or representatives. Upon completion of Subcontractor's Work, Subcontractor shall remove all tools, equipment, and surplus material from the Project premises and shall leave the Work area in "broom clean" condition or equivalent.

8.3 Should Subcontractor fail to keep or leave the Project site clean and orderly, Contractor may do so and charge the entire cost thereof to Subcontractor, to be deducted from amounts due or payable to Subcontractor.

8.4 Subcontractor shall be responsible for protecting its materials and Work from damage and deterioration and shall promptly replace any damaged materials or Work at no additional cost to Contractor or Owner.

8.5 During the performance of the Work and for three (3) years after acceptance of the Work, Subcontractor warrants that all Work, materials, equipment, and labor furnished under this Agreement will be of good quality and new unless otherwise specifically approved in writing, that the Work will be free from defects not inherent in the quality required or permitted, and that all Work, materials, and equipment will conform with all requirements of this Agreement and the Contract Documents.

8.6 Work, material, or equipment not conforming to these requirements may be rejected. Upon receipt of written notice from Contractor specifying any defects or deficiencies in the Work, Subcontractor shall promptly take action to correct such Work, materials, or equipment. If Subcontractor fails to do so within a reasonable time, not to exceed ten (10) business days after receipt of notice, Contractor may correct it and deduct the reasonable cost of correction from the Contract Price or any amounts due to Subcontractor.

8.7 Subcontractor shall bear the cost of correcting any destroyed or damaged Work, whether completed or partially completed, caused by Subcontractor's correction, removal, or reinstallation of Work considered defective. Neither final acceptance of the Work nor payment shall relieve Subcontractor of responsibility for defective or deficient materials or workmanship.

9. CHANGES TO THE WORK

9.1 No changes to the scope of Work or Contract Price shall be made except by written change order executed by both Contractor and Subcontractor and approved by Contractor's authorized representative.

9.2 Subcontractor shall not proceed with any changed or additional Work until receipt of a fully executed change order. Any Work performed without an executed change order shall be performed at Subcontractor's sole cost and expense.

9.3 All change order requests initiated by Subcontractor shall include a detailed breakdown of labor costs (number of hours and hourly rates), material costs with supporting quotes, equipment costs, and a revised schedule impact analysis.

9.4 Contractor shall not be obligated to pay any additional compensation for delays or additional costs resulting from Subcontractor's failure to clarify requirements or coordinate with other trades prior to commencing Work.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of law principles. 

10.2 Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations, including prevailing wage requirements if applicable to the Project.

10.3 Any legal action or proceeding arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state courts located in Sacramento County, California, and both parties consent to the jurisdiction and venue thereof.

10.4 Before initiating formal legal proceedings, Contractor and Subcontractor agree to meet and confer in good faith to attempt to resolve any disputes. If such discussion does not resolve the matter within fifteen (15) business days, either party may pursue legal remedies.

11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Contractor's Right to Inspect. Contractor shall have the right to inspect all Work, materials, and equipment at any time. Subcontractor shall provide full access to all Work areas and shall provide all requested documentation, test reports, and certifications.

11.2 Permits and Licenses. Subcontractor shall maintain all necessary licenses and permits required to perform the Work and shall comply with all applicable licensing requirements. Subcontractor represents and warrants that it is properly licensed as an insulation contractor in the State of California.

11.3 Safety. Subcontractor shall be solely responsible for safety at its work areas and shall comply with all OSHA regulations, Cal/OSHA regulations, and site-specific safety plans. Subcontractor shall maintain a safe work environment and shall immediately report all accidents and injuries to Contractor.

11.4 Independent Contractor. Subcontractor is an independent contractor and is not an employee of Contractor. Subcontractor is solely responsible for payroll taxes, workers' compensation, and all employee-related matters for its personnel.

11.5 Confidentiality. Subcontractor shall maintain in confidence all proprietary information, cost data, and business information disclosed by Contractor or Owner and shall not disclose such information to third parties without prior written consent.

11.6 Force Majeure. Subcontractor shall not be excused from performance except for delays resulting from unforeseeable events beyond Subcontractor's reasonable control, including acts of God, war, labor strikes not affecting only Subcontractor, and governmental action. Subcontractor shall mitigate any such delay and shall promptly notify Contractor.

11.7 Termination. Contractor may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon five (5) days' written notice if the Project is cancelled or suspended by Owner, and Contractor shall pay Subcontractor only for Work properly performed prior to termination, less any costs and expenses incurred by Contractor related to such termination.

11.8 Contractor may immediately terminate this Agreement for cause if Subcontractor: (a) fails to maintain insurance; (b) abandons the Work; (c) performs Work in violation of applicable laws; (d) creates a safety hazard; or (e) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within ten (10) days of notice.

11.9 Assignment. Subcontractor shall not assign this Agreement or any right or obligation hereunder without the prior written consent of Contractor, which consent may be withheld in Contractor's sole discretion. Any attempted assignment without consent shall be void.

11.10 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Contract Documents incorporated by reference, constitutes the entire agreement between Contractor and Subcontractor concerning the Work and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements.

11.11 Amendments. No amendment or modification of this Agreement shall be valid unless in writing and executed by authorized representatives of both Contractor and Subcontractor.

11.12 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

11.13 No Waiver. No waiver of any provision or breach of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing signed by the party against whom such waiver is sought, and no waiver shall constitute a waiver of any other provision or subsequent breach.

11.14 Lien Waiver. Prior to each progress payment, Subcontractor shall execute and deliver to Contractor a conditional waiver of mechanics' lien in the form required by California law. Prior to final payment, Subcontractor shall execute an unconditional final waiver of mechanics' lien.

11.15 Notice. All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, by overnight courier, by email with read receipt, or by certified mail to the addresses set forth in the opening paragraph hereof.
