SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

THIS SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of March 17, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between CASCADE RIDGE CONTRACTORS, LLC, a Washington limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 4800 Pacific Highway East, Fife, Washington 98424 ("Contractor"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF WASHINGTON, LLC, a Washington limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 3310 South Pine Street, Tacoma, Washington 98409 ("Subcontractor").

RECITALS:

WHEREAS, Contractor has been engaged to perform construction services for the Puyallup Medical Office Building located at 2850 North Meridian Road, Puyallup, Washington 98371, owned by Evergreen Clinic Properties, LLC (the "Project"); and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires to subcontract certain mechanical work to Subcontractor; and

WHEREAS, Subcontractor desires to perform such mechanical work and has submitted its bid for such services dated February 10, 2025;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Work" means all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the mechanical work described in Section 2 of this Agreement, including all HVAC and plumbing systems installation, testing, and commissioning for the Project.

1.2 "Contract Documents" means this Agreement, the General Conditions of the construction contract between Contractor and the Project Owner, the plans and specifications prepared by the Project's architect and engineers, and all addenda and modifications thereto.

1.3 "Project" means the Puyallup Medical Office Building, a four-story medical office and clinic facility located at 2850 North Meridian Road, Puyallup, Washington 98371.

1.4 "Subcontract Price" means the fixed price of Two Million Two Hundred Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($2,215,000.00) for performance of the Work, subject to adjustments as provided herein.

1.5 "Days" means calendar days unless otherwise specified as business days.

2. SCOPE OF WORK

2.1 Subcontractor shall provide and perform all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete all mechanical systems for the Project, including but not limited to:

2.1.1 Design and installation of complete HVAC systems for the Project, including:

(a) Two (2) rooftop air handling units (each 15 tons of cooling capacity) with variable frequency drives and digital control systems;

(b) Complete ductwork sizing, fabrication, and installation throughout all four floors and mechanical spaces;

(c) Supply and return air grilles, registers, and diffusers;

(d) Installation of building energy management system controls integrated with the main BACnet system;

(e) Balancing and testing of all air distribution systems;

(f) Troubleshooting and commissioning of HVAC equipment to manufacturer specifications.

2.1.2 Design and installation of complete plumbing systems, including:

(a) Water service line from public main to building with backflow preventer;

(b) Main distribution piping (copper and PEX as specified) to all floors;

(c) Hot water generation and distribution systems (two 40-gallon instantaneous water heaters with circulation pump);

(d) Installation of all plumbing fixtures including sinks, toilets, urinals, eyewash stations, and emergency showers as required for medical occupancy;

(e) Roof drain systems and interior storm drainage;

(f) Sanitary waste and vent systems complete with grease trap in kitchen area;

(g) Low-pressure water system for medical gas integration points;

(h) Pressure testing, leak detection, and flushing of all piping systems;

(i) System startup and demonstration to Project Owner and Contractor.

2.1.3 Provision and installation of all related mechanical equipment, piping, fittings, fasteners, sealants, insulation, and accessories necessary for a complete and functional mechanical installation.

2.1.4 Compliance with all applicable provisions of the Washington State Energy Code, International Plumbing Code, International Mechanical Code, and all local amendments and standards for Pierce County.

2.2 Subcontractor shall coordinate all Work with Contractor and other trades, maintain the jobsite in a clean and safe condition as it relates to mechanical Work, and remove all mechanical trade debris daily or as directed by Contractor.

2.3 Subcontractor shall provide a competent superintendent and any necessary assistants, all satisfactory to Contractor, at the jobsite during Subcontractor's entire performance of the Work. Any superintendent unsatisfactory to Contractor shall promptly be replaced. The superintendent shall represent Subcontractor in all matters and all directions given to the superintendent by Contractor shall be as binding as if given directly to Subcontractor.

2.4 Subcontractor shall coordinate with the mechanical engineer retained by Contractor, currently Nelson & Associates Mechanical Engineering, Portland, Oregon, regarding any field conditions or deviations from the plans and specifications that affect the mechanical systems.

2.5 Subcontractor shall participate in all required jobsite meetings, including weekly coordination meetings and any pre-construction meetings held prior to commencement of mechanical work.

2.6 Subcontractor shall provide all services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the standards and practices of the mechanical contracting industry in the State of Washington.

3. SUBCONTRACT PRICE

3.1 Subcontractor shall perform all Work for a fixed subcontract price of Two Million Two Hundred Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($2,215,000.00), which price is based on Subcontractor's bid dated February 10, 2025, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference. This price includes all labor, materials, equipment, tools, insurance, permits, testing, and all other costs and expenses necessary to complete the Work.

3.2 The Subcontract Price is inclusive and shall include all costs for:

(a) All labor for fabrication, delivery, installation, testing, and commissioning;

(b) All materials including pipe, fittings, fixtures, equipment, insulation, and fasteners;

(c) All temporary facilities, staging areas, and equipment rental;

(d) All permits and inspections required for mechanical work;

(e) All taxes and licenses;

(f) All overhead and profit.

3.3 The Subcontract Price is fixed and shall not be subject to adjustment except as specifically provided in Section 4 (Material Price Escalation) and Section 6 (Changes and Claims) of this Agreement.

3.4 Invoices shall be submitted monthly and shall itemize labor, materials, and equipment costs in sufficient detail to substantiate work completed. Subcontractor shall submit invoices on the 25th of each month for work completed through the 20th of that month.

4. MATERIAL PRICE ESCALATION

Material Price Escalation. If, between the date of Subcontractor's bid (February 10, 2025) and the date Subcontractor purchases copper tube, copper fittings or HVAC equipment for the Work, the documented price of any such item increases by more than eight percent (8%), Subcontractor may request an equitable adjustment equal to the increase in excess of eight percent (8%), supported by supplier quotations and invoices. Requests must be submitted within twenty-one (21) days after Subcontractor receives notice of the price increase; requests not timely submitted are waived.

5. PAYMENT

5.1 Payment Terms. Contractor shall pay Subcontractor for Work performed in accordance with the following terms:

5.1.1 Monthly Progress Payments. Contractor shall pay Subcontractor monthly progress payments based on the percentage of Work completed, less a five percent (5%) retainage, within thirty (30) days after Contractor receives payment from the Project Owner for the corresponding work. Subcontractor's monthly invoices shall include a detailed breakdown of work completed, labor hours, and material deliveries.

5.1.2 Pay-If-Paid Clause. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Subcontractor acknowledges and agrees that Contractor's obligation to pay Subcontractor for any portion of the Work is conditioned upon Contractor's receipt of payment from the Project Owner for that portion of the Work. In the event Contractor does not receive payment from the Project Owner, Contractor shall have no obligation to pay Subcontractor for work performed. Subcontractor assumes the risk of non-payment by the Project Owner and waives any claims against Contractor for payment in the event the Project Owner fails to pay Contractor. This is a material inducement to Contractor entering into this Agreement at the stated price.

5.1.3 Final Payment. Upon completion of all Work and acceptance by Contractor and the Project Owner, and after submission of a final sworn statement by Subcontractor that all suppliers, laborers, and subcontractors have been paid in full, Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the final invoice including release of all retainage, provided that no liens or claims are pending against Subcontractor or the Project. If any liens or claims are filed, Contractor shall have the right to withhold payment in the amount of such liens or claims pending their resolution.

5.2 Retainage. Contractor shall retain five percent (5%) of each progress payment until final completion and acceptance of all Work. Final retainage shall be due within thirty (30) days after the Project is substantially complete and all mechanical systems are commissioned and operating to specification, provided no deficiencies remain.

5.3 Payment Application and Timing. Subcontractor shall submit invoices to Contractor's Project Manager, David Chen, at the Fife, Washington office. Payment is due thirty (30) days following Contractor's receipt of payment from the Project Owner for the corresponding work. Contractor's payment to Subcontractor is contingent upon Contractor's receipt of funds from the Project Owner, and Subcontractor acknowledges the pay-if-paid nature of this Agreement.

5.4 Mechanic's Lien Waiver. As a condition of receiving each payment, Subcontractor shall execute and deliver to Contractor a conditional waiver of lien for that payment, in form approved by Contractor. Prior to final payment, Subcontractor shall deliver a final unconditional waiver of all lien rights and claims.

5.5 Interest on Late Payments. Late payments by Contractor shall accrue interest at the rate of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less, accruing on a daily basis from the due date until paid in full.

6. CHANGES AND CLAIMS

6.1 Change Order Procedures. No changes to the Scope of Work, Subcontract Price, or schedule shall be binding unless approved in writing by Contractor and signed by an authorized representative of Contractor prior to performance of the changed work. Verbal instructions or changes shall not be binding.

6.2 Change Requests. Subcontractor shall submit all requests for changes or extras in writing to Contractor within seven (7) days of the event or discovery giving rise to the change. The request shall include:

(a) A detailed description of the change or extra work;

(b) The reason for the change (differing site conditions, clarification of plans, added scope, etc.);

(c) The impact on the Subcontract Price, including itemization of labor, materials, and equipment;

(d) The impact on the schedule, if any;

(e) Subcontractor's supporting documentation (quotes, invoices, time records).

6.3 Failure to Timely Notify. Subcontractor shall forfeit any claim for additional compensation or time resulting from changes, differing site conditions, or other matters if written notice is not provided to Contractor within seven (7) days of Subcontractor's discovery of the condition giving rise to the claim. This requirement is a material term and strict compliance is required.

6.4 No Waiver by Performance. Subcontractor's performance of work without Contractor's authorization shall not give rise to any claim for additional payment or time except as may be expressly approved in a written change order.

6.5 Differing Site Conditions. Should Subcontractor encounter conditions materially different from those indicated in the Contract Documents or reasonably anticipated, Subcontractor shall immediately notify Contractor in writing. Contractor shall investigate and, if the conditions are confirmed to be materially different, shall issue an appropriate change order. Subcontractor shall not proceed with altered work pending written authorization.

6.6 Delays and Disruptions. Subcontractor shall be entitled to a time extension only in the event of delays caused by Contractor, the Project Owner, or other contractors over which Subcontractor has no responsibility or control, and then only if Subcontractor provides prompt written notice as required herein. Subcontractor shall not be entitled to additional compensation for delays unless the delay results from a change in the Scope of Work as approved in writing by Contractor.

6.7 Disputes. Any dispute between the parties regarding interpretation of this Agreement, performance obligations, or claims for additional compensation shall be submitted first to good faith negotiation between the parties. If negotiation does not resolve the matter within fourteen (14) days, either party may submit the dispute to mediation or, if permitted by law, binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

7. INSURANCE

7.1 Insurance Requirements. Subcontractor shall obtain and maintain, at its sole cost and expense, comprehensive insurance coverage as follows during the entire term of this Agreement and through final completion and acceptance of all Work:

7.1.1 Workers' Compensation. As required by Washington State law (Title 51 RCW), with employer's liability coverage of not less than One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) per occurrence, One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) disease-policy limit, and Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000) disease-aggregate.

7.1.2 Commercial General Liability. Comprehensive general liability insurance with minimum combined single limits of Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence and Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000) general aggregate, including coverage for:

(a) Bodily injury and property damage;

(b) Contractual liability arising from this Agreement;

(c) Products and completed operations coverage;

(d) Personal injury liability;

(e) Explosion, collapse, and underground property damage.

7.1.3 Commercial Automobile Liability. For all vehicles used in connection with the Work, with minimum combined single limits of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence for bodily injury and property damage, including hired and non-owned vehicle coverage.

7.1.4 Equipment and Tools. Subcontractor shall maintain coverage for all tools, equipment, and materials with replacement cost coverage for any losses.

7.2 Additional Insured. Subcontractor's general liability insurance shall name Contractor, the Project Owner (Evergreen Clinic Properties, LLC), and the Project's lender as additional insureds, with insurance covering liability arising out of Subcontractor's operations. The insurance afforded to additional insureds shall be as broad as the insurance afforded to the first named insured. In the event Subcontractor provides insurance with limits greater than those required, the insurance afforded to additional insureds shall be up to the full limits provided.

7.3 Waiver of Subrogation. Subcontractor's insurance carriers shall include a waiver of subrogation in favor of Contractor and the Project Owner, such that the carriers waive any right of recovery against these parties.

7.4 Certificates of Insurance. Prior to commencing any Work, Subcontractor shall provide Contractor with certificates of insurance evidencing the coverage required herein, issued by A-rated insurers licensed in Washington State. Certificates shall indicate that coverage will not be cancelled or materially changed without thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Contractor.

7.5 Compliance with Project Requirements. To the extent the Project Owner or Contractor's insurance broker has established specific insurance requirements including project-specific liability endorsements, OCIP compliance requirements, or project-specific coverage amendments, Subcontractor shall comply with all such requirements and provide evidence of compliance prior to commencing Work.

7.6 No Limitation on Liability. Maintenance of insurance shall not limit Subcontractor's indemnification obligations or other liability under this Agreement.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Subcontractor. To the fullest extent permitted by Washington law, including RCW 4.24.115, Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend (with counsel acceptable to Contractor), and hold harmless Contractor, the Project Owner, the Project's architect, engineers, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from:

(a) Subcontractor's negligent performance or failure to perform the Work;

(b) Subcontractor's violation of applicable laws, codes, or regulations;

(c) Bodily injury, death, or property damage caused by Subcontractor's negligent acts or omissions;

(d) Subcontractor's breach of this Agreement;

(e) Liens filed by Subcontractor or its suppliers and laborers;

(f) Defects in Subcontractor's Work.

8.2 Limitation on Indemnity. Notwithstanding Section 8.1, Subcontractor shall have no obligation to indemnify Contractor for claims arising solely from Contractor's negligence or sole negligence. Subcontractor's indemnification obligation shall apply only to the extent of Subcontractor's negligence or wrongful acts. For claims involving concurrent or comparative negligence, Subcontractor shall indemnify Contractor only to the extent of Subcontractor's proportionate negligence.

8.3 Title 51 RCW Immunity Waiver. The parties expressly acknowledge that Washington's workers' compensation law (Title 51 RCW) generally prohibits waiver of employer immunity. However, to the fullest extent permitted by RCW 4.24.115 and RCW 7.68.010, each party waives all immunity under Title 51 RCW with respect to claims arising between the parties under this Agreement, provided that such waiver applies only where the party seeking to enforce indemnification has obtained liability insurance as required herein. This mutual waiver of immunity has been expressly negotiated between the parties as a material term of this Agreement.

8.4 Defense. Subcontractor shall select defense counsel reasonably acceptable to Contractor. Subcontractor shall bear all costs of defense, including attorneys' fees, and Contractor shall cooperate in the defense of any claim.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

9.1 Subcontractor is, for all purposes, an independent contractor. Subcontractor is not an employee, agent, or representative of Contractor. Subcontractor shall not directly or indirectly act or represent itself as Contractor's agent, servant, or employee, or make any commitments or incur any liabilities on Contractor's behalf without Contractor's express written authorization.

9.2 Subcontractor is solely responsible for all payroll taxes, withholding, unemployment insurance, and all other employment-related obligations for its employees.

9.3 Subcontractor shall not be entitled to any employee benefits, including health insurance, retirement plans, or paid leave.

9.4 No Agency Relationship. Nothing in this Agreement creates an agency, master-servant, employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship between Contractor and Subcontractor.

10. TERMINATION

10.1 Termination for Cause. Contractor may terminate this Agreement for cause immediately upon written notice if:

(a) Subcontractor fails to maintain insurance as required;

(b) Subcontractor abandons the Work or fails to provide an adequate workforce;

(c) Subcontractor's work is materially deficient or does not comply with the Contract Documents;

(d) Subcontractor becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has a receiver appointed;

(e) Subcontractor fails to comply with applicable laws or safety requirements;

(f) Subcontractor fails to cure any material breach within ten (10) days after written notice.

10.2 Termination for Convenience. Contractor may terminate this Agreement for convenience, without cause, by providing seven (7) days' written notice to Subcontractor. Upon such termination, Contractor shall pay Subcontractor for all Work properly performed through the termination date, including reasonable demobilization costs, but excluding any profit margin on work not performed.

10.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Subcontractor shall:

(a) Immediately cease performance of the Work;

(b) Remove all of Subcontractor's equipment and tools from the jobsite;

(c) Provide all documentation, warranties, and manuals to Contractor;

(d) Cooperate with any replacement contractor;

(e) Execute final lien waivers;

(f) Remain liable for all defects in Work performed prior to termination.

10.4 Remedies Upon Termination for Cause. In addition to termination, if Subcontractor fails to cure any breach, Contractor may:

(a) Perform the Work itself or engage another contractor;

(b) Withhold payment for the cost of correction or replacement;

(c) Pursue any other legal remedy available.

11. PERMITS AND COMPLIANCE

11.1 Subcontractor shall obtain and pay for all permits, licenses, and inspections required for the mechanical work. This includes Washington State Department of Labor and Industries mechanical contractor license requirements and all local mechanical permits for Pierce County and the City of Puyallup.

11.2 Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, codes, and regulations, including:

(a) The International Mechanical Code (IMC);

(b) The International Plumbing Code (IPC);

(c) Washington State Energy Code;

(d) Washington State Building Code;

(e) OSHA requirements;

(f) Environmental regulations;

(g) All local ordinances and amendments.

11.3 Subcontractor shall schedule and pass all required inspections, including rough-in inspections, equipment inspections, and final inspections. Subcontractor shall correct any deficiencies identified by inspectors at no cost to Contractor.

12. WARRANTIES AND COMMISSIONING

12.1 Warranty. Subcontractor warrants that all Work shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with the Contract Documents and all applicable codes and standards. Subcontractor warrants that all equipment and materials shall be new, of good quality, and free from defects.

12.2 Manufacturer Warranties. Subcontractor shall provide to Contractor and the Project Owner all manufacturer warranties for equipment and materials, transferred to the Project Owner where transferable, and shall coordinate any necessary warranty registrations.

12.3 Commissioning and Start-up. Subcontractor shall be responsible for complete commissioning, testing, startup, and demonstration of all mechanical systems. This includes:

(a) Flushing and pressure testing of all piping systems;

(b) Balancing all air distribution systems;

(c) Setting all controls and programming;

(d) Training of Project Owner's operating personnel;

(e) Preparation of as-built systems documentation.

12.4 Deficiency Resolution. Any mechanical systems not operating to specification at the time of Project Owner acceptance shall be corrected by Subcontractor at no cost until full functionality is achieved.

13. SAFETY AND LIABILITY

13.1 Subcontractor shall be responsible for all jobsite safety as it relates to mechanical work, including fall protection, equipment guarding, material handling, and any other applicable OSHA and Washington Department of Labor requirements.

13.2 Subcontractor shall maintain a safe working environment and comply with all safety regulations. Subcontractor shall immediately report any accidents, injuries, or near-misses to Contractor.

13.3 Subcontractor assumes full liability for any accidents, injuries, or damages resulting from Subcontractor's negligence or failure to maintain safe working conditions.

14. LIENS AND CLAIMS

14.1 Subcontractor shall not file or permit the filing of any mechanic's lien against the Project property. Subcontractor's sole remedy for non-payment shall be claims against Contractor through litigation or arbitration, not against Project property.

14.2 Prior to final payment, Subcontractor shall execute a final unconditional waiver of all lien rights and claims relating to the Project.

14.3 Subcontractor shall require all its sub-subcontractors and suppliers to execute waivers of lien prior to receiving payment.

15. INSPECTION AND QUALITY CONTROL

15.1 Contractor shall have the right to inspect all Work at any time. Subcontractor shall cooperate fully with all inspections and testing.

15.2 Subcontractor shall correct any deficient work identified by Contractor or the Project Owner's representative immediately and at no cost to Contractor.

15.3 The Project's mechanical engineer, Nelson & Associates, shall have authority to direct quality corrections and inspect all work for compliance with specifications.

16. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING

16.1 Subcontractor shall not assign any portion of this Agreement or assign any amounts due without Contractor's prior written consent. Any unauthorized assignment shall be void.

16.2 Subcontractor shall not subcontract the Work without Contractor's prior written consent. If Contractor consents, Subcontractor shall remain fully liable for all performance as if no subcontract had occurred.

16.3 All subcontractors must be approved in writing by Contractor prior to engagement.

17. SCHEDULE AND TIME OF PERFORMANCE

17.1 Subcontractor shall commence mechanical work on or about June 2, 2025, and shall substantially complete all HVAC and plumbing installation by November 15, 2025, subject to adjustments for changes and excusable delays.

17.2 Time is of the essence in this Agreement. Subcontractor acknowledges that delays may result in damages to Contractor and the Project Owner.

17.3 Subcontractor shall maintain a schedule and progress report updated weekly, submitted to Contractor each Friday showing work completed, work scheduled for the following week, and any concerns or delays.

17.4 Should Subcontractor fail to maintain progress, Contractor may require Subcontractor to add additional labor and equipment at Subcontractor's cost to accelerate performance.

18. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

18.1 Informal Resolution. The parties shall first attempt to resolve any disputes through good faith negotiation between project managers and company management.

18.2 Mediation. If informal resolution fails within fourteen (14) days, either party may request non-binding mediation conducted by a mutually selected mediator in Tacoma, Washington.

18.3 Arbitration. If mediation does not resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days, either party may submit the dispute to binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, with one arbitrator, conducted in Tacoma, Washington. The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

18.4 Injunctive Relief. Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may seek injunctive relief to prevent irreparable harm or to enforce confidentiality obligations.
