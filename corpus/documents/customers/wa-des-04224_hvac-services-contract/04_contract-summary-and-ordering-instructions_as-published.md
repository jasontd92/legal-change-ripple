Contract Scope: 
Eligible purchasers may use this HVAC Services contract for HVAC equipment/systems they own or manage. The services include:

• Scheduled Preventive Maintenance Services: Inspection, testing, maintenance, adjustments, and part replacements to keep HVAC equipment/systems in proper working order as per original manufacturer specifications.

• Non-Scheduled Services & Repairs: Maintenance and repair of HVAC equipment/systems that are not operating properly, ensuring they function according to original manufacturer specifications.

Restrictions 
This contract is established under RCW 39.26 and complies with RCW 39.12 for prevailing wage requirements as applicable to ordinary maintenance services. It is not intended to meet the requirements of RCW 39.04, RCW 39.80 and RCW 18.43 for public works and engineering services. All purchasers must follow their particular jurisdiction’s laws, rules, and expectations for projects that may include any public works elements.

The DES Facility Professional Services (FPS) team provides state agencies with a wide range of professional architectural, engineering and public works contracting services. For information about DES FPS programs, please visit their website or contact them at EASMail@des.wa.gov.

This contract is for routine preventive maintenance and repairs to restore an HVAC system to an already approved Public Works state. This contract does not allow for upgrades, overhauls, improvements to existing systems, or new system installs, nor does it cover new HVAC equipment/systems or the purchase of HVAC parts alone, only the HVAC parts necessary for the specified services. There is a separate contract for purchasing HVAC parts without services (Contract No. 27723).
