FIRST AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This First Amendment ("Amendment") to the Master Services Agreement dated May 15, 2023 (the "Agreement"), is made and entered into effective as of June 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between CRESTLINE RETAIL TRUST, a Delaware statutory trust ("Client"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company with principal place of business at 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, TX 77041 ("Contractor").

R E C I T A L S

WHEREAS, Client and Contractor entered into that certain Master Services Agreement dated May 15, 2023 (the "Agreement"), pursuant to which Contractor agreed to provide plumbing and HVAC services and materials to Client at Client's retail properties throughout the Greater Houston Metropolitan Area;

WHEREAS, the Agreement included as Exhibit A thereto a schedule of labor rates and materials pricing terms applicable to all services and goods provided under the Agreement;

WHEREAS, economic conditions and increased labor costs have necessitated adjustments to the compensation structure under the Agreement, and the Parties desire to update labor rates effective June 1, 2025, while maintaining current materials markup percentages;

WHEREAS, the Parties further desire to establish a new tariff and material surcharge mechanism for certain commodities, specifically copper tubing, fittings, and HVAC equipment, which are subject to volatile market conditions and significant price fluctuations; and

WHEREAS, Client and Contractor desire to amend the Agreement as set forth herein to implement these changes while keeping all other terms and conditions of the Agreement in full force and effect.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. AMENDMENT OF EXHIBIT A

Effective as of June 1, 2025, the Master Services Agreement is hereby amended by deleting Exhibit A (Schedule of Labor Rates and Materials Pricing) in its entirety and replacing it with the attached Exhibit A (dated June 1, 2025). As of the Effective Date of this Amendment, any and all references to Exhibit A in the Agreement shall be deemed to refer to the attached Exhibit A (dated June 1, 2025).

The revised Exhibit A reflects the following modifications to the compensation structure:

1.1 Labor Rate Adjustment. All labor rates set forth in the original Exhibit A dated May 15, 2023 are hereby increased by four percent (4.0%), effective June 1, 2025. By way of illustration and not limitation, the following rates apply as of the Effective Date: (a) HVAC Technician – Maintenance and Service Calls: $110.00 per hour (increased from $105.77 per hour); (b) HVAC Technician – Installation: $125.00 per hour (increased from $120.19 per hour); (c) Licensed Plumber – General Plumbing Services: $115.00 per hour (increased from $110.58 per hour); (d) Licensed Plumber – Complex Projects and Repairs: $135.00 per hour (increased from $129.81 per hour); and (e) HVAC Apprentice/Helper: $65.00 per hour (increased from $62.50 per hour). These adjusted rates shall apply to all labor services rendered on and after June 1, 2025.

1.2 Materials Markup. The materials markup percentage applicable to all materials and equipment supplied by Contractor under the Agreement shall remain unchanged at cost plus eighteen percent (18.0%). This markup applies to the invoice cost of all materials and equipment supplied by Contractor that are not specifically subject to the Tariff and Material Surcharge set forth in Section 2 below.

2. TARIFF AND MATERIAL SURCHARGE

Effective June 1, 2025, a new Tariff and Material Surcharge provision is added to the Agreement as follows:

2.1 Applicability. For all copper tube, copper tubing fittings, and HVAC equipment (including but not limited to compressors, condensers, evaporator coils, air handlers, furnaces, heat pumps, thermostats, and refrigerants) ordered by Client or purchased by Contractor on behalf of Client with an order date of June 1, 2025 or later, a Tariff and Material Surcharge shall be applied and invoiced to Client.

2.2 Calculation and Pass-Through. The Tariff and Material Surcharge shall be calculated as the difference between the documented cost of the materials at the time of original order and the baseline cost established for such materials as of May 31, 2025. All increases or decreases in documented material costs for the enumerated items shall be passed through to Client at documented cost, without markup, surcharge, or administrative fee. Contractor shall provide Client with copies of supplier invoices or price quotations as documentation of cost.

2.3 Separate Line Item Invoicing. All Tariff and Material Surcharge charges shall be invoiced as a separate line item on invoices rendered by Contractor to Client. Each invoice shall clearly identify: (a) the specific materials or equipment to which the surcharge applies; (b) the documented cost of such materials or equipment; (c) the baseline cost applicable as of May 31, 2025; (d) the amount of the Tariff and Material Surcharge; and (e) the order date of the materials or equipment.

2.4 Client Notice. Contractor shall provide Client with written notice of any material changes to supplier pricing for the enumerated items within five (5) business days of notification from Contractor's suppliers. Such notice shall include the proposed surcharge adjustment and the effective date thereof.

3. NO OTHER CHANGES

Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms, covenants, and conditions of the Master Services Agreement dated May 15, 2023 remain in full force and effect and are unaffected by this Amendment. In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this Amendment and the terms of the original Agreement, the provisions of this Amendment shall control as to matters specifically addressed herein. The Agreement, together with this Amendment, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

4. COUNTERPARTS AND EFFECTIVENESS

This Amendment may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Execution and delivery of this Amendment by electronic transmission (including PDF or facsimile) shall have the same force and effect as delivery of manually executed originals.
