MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into effective as of May 15, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between CRESTLINE RETAIL TRUST, a Maryland real estate investment trust, located at 2800 Post Oak Boulevard, Suite 2200, Houston, Texas 77056 ("Customer"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF TEXAS, LLC, a Texas limited liability company, located at 10850 West Little York Road, Houston, Texas 77041 ("Contractor").

RECITALS

A. Customer owns and operates twenty-two (22) community shopping centers located throughout the State of Texas (the "Properties").

B. Customer desires to retain Contractor to provide HVAC and plumbing maintenance services, emergency repair services, and equipment replacement services at the Properties on an ongoing basis under the terms and conditions set forth herein.

C. Contractor is willing and able to provide such services under a master services framework, with individual service requests issued as Service Orders.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Customer and Contractor agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Service Order" means a written request issued by Customer to Contractor for performance of Services at one or more of the Properties, substantially in the form attached as Exhibit B, and shall include: (a) the location(s) of the Property or Properties where Services are to be performed; (b) a description of the Services requested; (c) the requested start date and expected completion date; (d) any special instructions or requirements; and (e) the estimated budget, if applicable.

1.2 "Services" means the HVAC maintenance, plumbing maintenance, emergency repair, and equipment replacement services described in Section 2 hereof, to be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards and all applicable federal, state, and local laws, codes, and regulations.

1.3 "Materials" means all supplies, parts, refrigerants, lubricants, fasteners, fittings, and other materials necessary or required to complete the Services, as more particularly described in Section 3.

1.4 "Equipment" means HVAC units, plumbing fixtures, and related mechanical equipment installed at the Properties, whether installed prior to this Agreement or installed by Contractor during the term hereof.

1.5 "Property" or "Properties" means the twenty-two (22) community shopping centers owned and operated by Customer located in the State of Texas, as identified in Exhibit C.

1.6 "Business Day" means Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Central Time, excluding federal and state holidays observed by Contractor.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Scope of Services. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and upon receipt of a Service Order, Contractor shall provide the following services at the Properties:

(a) Routine Maintenance. Contractor shall provide scheduled HVAC and plumbing maintenance services, including but not limited to: inspection and cleaning of HVAC equipment; replacement of air filters and belts; lubrication of moving parts; flushing and inspection of plumbing systems; inspection of water heaters and boilers; testing of pressure relief valves; and other preventive maintenance services as specified in Service Orders or as may be reasonably recommended by Contractor to maintain the equipment in good working order.

(b) Emergency Repair Services. Contractor shall respond to emergency repair requests on a twenty-four (24) hour basis, seven (7) days per week, for HVAC system failures, heating and cooling outages, plumbing leaks, water damage incidents, and other critical facility conditions that may impact the operation or tenancy of the Properties. Contractor shall use reasonable efforts to respond to emergency calls within two (2) hours of notification during normal Business Hours and within four (4) hours during off-hours, weather and road conditions permitting.

(c) Non-Emergency Repair Services. Contractor shall perform non-emergency repairs to HVAC systems, plumbing systems, and related equipment, including diagnosis of system failures, repair or replacement of defective components, and restoration of systems to full operational capacity.

(d) Equipment Replacement. Upon Customer's authorization via Service Order, Contractor shall provide equipment replacement services, including supply and installation of new HVAC units, plumbing fixtures, water heaters, and related mechanical equipment at the Properties. Contractor shall coordinate disconnection and removal of existing equipment and shall ensure proper installation in compliance with all applicable codes and manufacturer specifications.

2.2 Standards of Performance. All Services shall be performed: (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards for plumbing and HVAC contractors in the Houston, Texas area; (b) in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, codes, regulations, and permit requirements; (c) by technicians who are appropriately licensed, trained, and insured; (d) with due regard for the safety of persons and property; and (e) in a manner that minimizes disruption to the business operations and tenants at the Properties.

2.3 Customer Responsibilities. Customer shall: (a) provide Contractor with access to the Properties and to Equipment requiring service; (b) cooperate with Contractor and provide information necessary to perform the Services; (c) coordinate with tenants and property managers regarding service access and scheduling; (d) ensure that Properties are safe and that Contractor personnel may work without undue hazard; and (e) promptly report to Contractor any service issues, Equipment failures, or performance concerns.

2.4 Exclusions. Contractor shall not be obligated to provide Services for: (a) Equipment that is not owned or operated by Customer; (b) structural repairs or modifications to buildings; (c) conditions caused by misuse, abuse, neglect, or failure to maintain Equipment; (d) changes to Equipment specifications, modifications, or upgrades not requested and authorized by Customer in writing; (e) services for Equipment that is beyond reasonable repair or for which replacement parts are no longer available; or (f) services for Equipment installed by others if such Equipment was not installed in accordance with applicable codes and standards.

3. WORK ORDERS

3.1 Issuance of Service Orders. Customer shall issue Service Orders to Contractor via email or in writing. Each Service Order shall include: (a) the specific Property location; (b) the type and scope of Services requested; (c) the requested start date and timeline for completion; (d) any special access requirements or restrictions; (e) the name and contact information of the Property manager or contact person; and (f) if known, an estimated budget or budget ceiling for the work.

3.2 Acceptance and Scheduling. Contractor shall acknowledge receipt of each Service Order within one (1) Business Day and shall confirm Contractor's ability to perform the Services within the requested timeframe. If Contractor cannot meet the requested schedule, Contractor shall propose alternative dates. For emergency Services, Contractor shall confirm receipt by telephone or text message.

3.3 Site Conditions and Estimates. Upon arrival at a Property to perform Services, Contractor may discover that the actual scope of work, nature of Equipment defects, or site conditions differ from those described in the Service Order. Contractor shall promptly notify Customer of any material changes to the expected scope of work and shall not proceed with work beyond the original scope without written authorization from Customer or an authorized representative. For work beyond the scope of the original Service Order, Contractor may provide an estimate for Customer's approval before proceeding.

3.4 Changes and Change Orders. No changes to a Service Order shall be valid unless approved in writing by an authorized representative of Customer. Any approved changes, additions, or deletions shall be documented in a written change order specifying the change, the reason for the change, the impact on pricing (if any), and any changes to the timeline or completion date. Contractor shall not be obligated to perform work that differs from the Service Order without a change order.

3.5 Completion and Sign-Off. Upon completion of Services, Contractor shall notify Customer and, if requested by Customer, shall provide a written completion report describing the work performed, materials used, parts installed or replaced, and recommendations for future maintenance or repairs. Customer or an authorized representative shall inspect the work and shall sign off on completion, indicating acceptance of the Services performed.

4. PRICING

4.1 Pricing Structure. Contractor's pricing for Services shall be based on the following rate structure, effective as of the Effective Date and as more particularly set forth in Exhibit A:

(a) Labor Rates. Contractor shall charge for labor services at hourly rates ranging from one hundred twelve dollars ($112.00) per hour to one hundred fifty-eight dollars ($158.00) per hour, depending on the skill level of the technician, the complexity of the work, and the type of service being performed. Rates shall include the technician's time, vehicle dispatch, basic tools and equipment, and travel time within a fifteen (15) mile radius of the applicable Property.

(b) Materials. Contractor shall provide all necessary Materials and shall charge Customer for Materials at Contractor's actual cost plus an eighteen percent (18%) markup to cover handling, storage, inventory, and overhead costs.

(c) Equipment Replacement Pricing. For Equipment replacement work, Contractor shall charge Customer the manufacturer's list price for the Equipment, less a discount of twenty-two percent (22%), plus labor costs for installation and removal of existing Equipment at the applicable hourly rates set forth in Exhibit A.

(d) Travel Time. Travel time to and from Properties shall be billed at the applicable hourly labor rate. For multiple Properties serviced on the same day, travel time between properties shall be prorated based on distance and time.

(e) Emergency Surcharge. For emergency Services requested during non-Business Hours (before 8:00 a.m. or after 5:00 p.m. on Business Days, or on weekends and holidays), Contractor may apply an emergency surcharge of fifty percent (50%) to the applicable hourly labor rate.

(f) Minimum Charges. The minimum charge for any Service Order response shall be one (1) hour of labor, regardless of the actual time spent, unless the work is performed in conjunction with other Services scheduled on the same day.

4.2 Rate Adjustments. Contractor's hourly rates and Material markup shall remain fixed for the initial three-year term of this Agreement. Thereafter, if this Agreement is renewed by mutual agreement, rates may be adjusted upon ninety (90) days' written notice by either party. Any rate adjustment shall not exceed the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index (All Urban Consumers) for the South-Central Region, or four percent (4%), whichever is less.

4.3 Pricing Authority. All pricing is firm for Services performed during the three-year term from May 15, 2023 through May 14, 2026. Any changes to pricing outside the term renewal provisions of Section 4.2 shall require mutual written agreement between Customer and Contractor.

Most Favored Customer. Contractor represents and warrants that the rates, fees and markups set forth in Exhibit A are, and during the Term shall remain, no less favorable than the rates, fees and markups Contractor charges any other commercial customer for substantially similar Services performed in the same State in comparable annual volumes. If Contractor offers or agrees to more favorable rates, fees, markups or price concessions to any such customer, Contractor shall notify Customer in writing within thirty (30) days and the more favorable pricing shall apply to Customer automatically, retroactive to the date first extended to such other customer.

4.4 Contractor Cost and Expense. The hourly rates and Material costs set forth herein shall include all of Contractor's direct costs for labor, Materials, equipment, vehicles, insurance, licensing, and overhead. Customer shall not be responsible for any additional charges, surcharges, or costs beyond those expressly set forth in this Agreement, except for change orders approved in writing by Customer.

4.5 Sales Tax. All pricing set forth in Exhibit A is exclusive of applicable Texas sales tax. Contractor shall separately itemize and collect all applicable sales tax on invoices, and Customer shall be responsible for payment of such tax.
