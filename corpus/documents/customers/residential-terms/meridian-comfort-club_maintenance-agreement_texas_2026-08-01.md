MERIDIAN COMFORT CLUB

The Meridian Comfort Club is a membership program offered by Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air for preventive maintenance and inspection of HVAC systems at the Member's residence. This Agreement establishes the terms under which Meridian provides inspections and maintenance services to Members at a discounted rate. This Program is a maintenance plan only and constitutes neither an insurance policy, extended warranty, nor service contract.

SECTION I: MEMBERSHIP TERM

A. Annual Membership. An annual membership term commences at 12:01 a.m. local time on the effective date shown on the face of this agreement and continues indefinitely until written cancellation notice is submitted to Meridian. Refer to Section III.A for cancellation procedures and terms.

B. Monthly Membership. A monthly membership term commences at 12:01 a.m. local time on the effective date shown on the face of this agreement and continues indefinitely until written cancellation notice is submitted to Meridian. Refer to Section III.B for cancellation procedures and terms.

SECTION II: MEMBERSHIP SERVICES AND BENEFITS

A. Inspections. During each 12-month membership period, Meridian will conduct two performance inspections on each HVAC system covered under this membership. These inspections will include, to the extent applicable and insofar as system components are accessible, all services specified in the membership description selected at enrollment.

B. Scheduling Responsibilities. The Member must contact Meridian to arrange each performance inspection. Inspections will be scheduled only during the membership term and only during Meridian's regular business hours. Meridian will make three attempted contacts to remind the Member to schedule due inspections. However, the Member bears responsibility for scheduling inspections. Unused inspections expire three months following the scheduled inspection date or upon membership termination, whichever occurs first. Expired inspections cannot be rescheduled or credited.

C. Repair Service Discounts. Meridian will apply a ten percent (10%) discount, not to exceed $100.00 per service call, to all repair work provided at the membership residence for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems. Members also receive the following fixed pricing on specialized maintenance services:

| Service | Standard Rate | Member Rate | Member Savings |
|---------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Water heater drain and flush (conventional tank only) | $89 | $49 | $30 |
| Drain cleaning via accessible cleanout | $238+ | $0 | $238+ |
| Electrical panel and system safety check | $211 | $29 | $182 |
| Electrical smoke detector inspection | $98 | $0 | $98 |
| Water softener sanitization service | $134 | $39 | $95 |
| Reverse osmosis filter replacement (customer-supplied filter) | $99 | $0 | $99 |
| Water quality inspection | $89 | $0 | $89 |

D. Priority Service Access. For unscheduled repair services, Meridian will use reasonable efforts to provide expedited scheduling as a membership benefit. The Member may request emergency service on a 24-hour, seven-days-per-week basis. Emergency service applies to situations presenting substantial risk of serious bodily harm or extensive property damage.

E. Workmanship Warranty. All work performed by Meridian under this membership is warranted against defective workmanship for one year from the date of completion.

F. Equipment Upgrade Incentives. Upon completion of each full consecutive membership year, the Member receives a $50 credit applicable solely toward the purchase and installation of either a new HVAC system (complete system) or an electrical panel at the membership residence. This credit may be applied to only one system type, may not be combined with other offers, may not be converted to cash, and can only be used when Meridian performs the installation. Any unused credits are forfeited without notice upon membership cancellation or termination. These credits are not transferable to other Members or properties.

SECTION III: MEMBERSHIP CANCELLATION AND TERMINATION

A. Annual Membership Cancellation and Refunds. An annual membership continues perpetually until the Member cancels in writing. The annual membership fee will be charged each anniversary date until written cancellation notice is received. Cancellation requires written notice sent to Meridian's office or by telephone. Meridian may require up to two weeks following the anniversary date to process the cancellation. Refunds are issued only as follows: (1) If cancellation occurs within thirty (30) days of an effective date (either the initial membership date or any annual anniversary date), the Member receives a refund of that year's membership payment less a $20 administrative processing fee; (2) If cancellation occurs after such thirty-day period, the refund is calculated on a pro-rata basis for the number of months remaining in the annual term in which cancellation occurs. No charges or refunds other than those specified herein will be issued regardless of inspections completed or missed.

B. Monthly Membership Cancellation and Refunds. A monthly membership continues perpetually until the Member cancels in writing. Written cancellation notice must be submitted to Meridian's office. Following receipt of cancellation notice, Meridian will continue charging the monthly membership fee for one full additional month, after which all service obligations cease. Meridian is not obligated to complete any remaining scheduled inspections and shall have no further service responsibilities to the Member upon cancellation. Meridian allows up to two weeks for processing the cancellation. No charges other than the continued monthly fee specified herein and no refunds will be issued regardless of inspections completed or missed.

C. Automatic Cancellation Due to Payment Failure. If a scheduled credit card payment is declined and the Member does not provide updated payment information and successfully process payment within thirty (30) days of the decline, this membership will automatically terminate. Upon such automatic termination, Meridian shall have no further obligation to provide services or complete pending inspections.

SECTION IV: EXCLUSIONS AND LIABILITY LIMITATIONS

A. Equipment Failures. Meridian does not cover and assumes no liability for any breakdown, failure, malfunction, or defect in HVAC equipment, whether or not such defects existed prior to or were detectable during any inspection.

B. Repair Services. Meridian does not supply parts or labor for repair services except as expressly provided in Section II.C and Section II.D. Any repair services that Meridian does provide under this membership will be supplied in accordance with those sections.

C. Limitation of Damages. Meridian shall not be liable for any consequential, special, indirect, or incidental damages, nor for bodily injury or property damage, regardless of cause. This limitation applies to all membership claims without exception.

SECTION V: MEMBERSHIP PRICING AND BILLING

A. Annual Membership Pricing. The annual membership rate is $153.89 per year for a single HVAC system. For each additional HVAC system at the membership residence, an additional $87.89 per year applies. Annual charges are billed in the same calendar month each year as the membership effective date.

B. Monthly Membership Pricing. The monthly membership rate is $13.99 per month for the first HVAC system. For each additional HVAC system at the membership residence, an additional $7.99 per month applies.

C. Payment Information. The Member is responsible for maintaining current and valid payment information on file with Meridian. Should a credit card expire, be cancelled, or otherwise become invalid, the Member must promptly contact Meridian to update payment information to avoid automatic membership termination as described in Section III.C.

D. Rate Adjustments. All membership rates are subject to change upon sixty (60) days' advance written notice to the Member. Members with questions regarding rates should contact Meridian.

SECTION VI: MEMBER SUPPORT

To schedule inspections, request service, or for questions regarding this membership, Members should contact Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air.
