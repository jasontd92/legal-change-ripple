## SECTION 1: PROGRAM DESCRIPTION

The Meridian Comfort Club is a preventative maintenance membership program providing members with regular tune-up services and discounted rates on equipment servicing. Membership includes the right to one annual tune-up on enrolled equipment per household or business, along with additional program benefits. Coverage and benefits apply exclusively to equipment enrolled in the Meridian Comfort Club. The program does not include discounted diagnostic fees, parts, or labor for any service or repair work beyond what is expressly stated in these terms.

## SECTION 2: ANNUAL TUNE-UP SERVICE

Members are entitled to one annual tune-up on all equipment under membership coverage. The tune-up may be performed simultaneously or on separate occasions, at the sole discretion of Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air.

## SECTION 3: MAINTENANCE DOCUMENTATION

Each annual tune-up performed under the membership will include a written maintenance checklist provided to the member upon completion of service. The checklist will document: the date of service; a description of all points inspected and/or cleaned; and written notice of any recommendations for repair and/or adjustment. Any repair or improvement recommendations will be communicated to the member prior to the performance of any additional work.

## SECTION 4: MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS AND DISCOUNT TIERS

### 4.1 One-Year Membership Tier

Members enrolled in a one-year Meridian Comfort Club membership receive the following benefits:

- 10% discount off service parts and repair labor
- 10% discount off diagnostic service fees
- Manufacturer warranty compliance support

### 4.2 Three-Year Membership Tier

Members enrolled in a three-year Meridian Comfort Club membership receive the following benefits:

- 15% discount off service parts and repair labor
- 15% discount off air duct and dryer vent cleaning services
- 15% discount off electrical services
- 15% discount off deep cleaning services for heating and cooling systems
- 15% discount off plumbing services
- 15% discount off new equipment installations
- 15% discount off diagnostic service fees
- Automatic tune-up scheduling reminders
- Manufacturer warranty compliance support

### 4.3 Discount Calculation and Restrictions

All discounts are based on standard published retail rates and apply only to services and products provided by Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air. Discounts may not be combined with any other promotions, discounts, or special offers.

## SECTION 5: MEMBERSHIP DURATION AND ANNIVERSARY DATE

The anniversary date is established on the date when the first preventative maintenance service is performed and marks the commencement of Meridian Comfort Club coverage. Members retain all club benefits for one full year following the final anniversary date if they elect not to renew their membership. Upon renewal, a new membership term commences.

## SECTION 6: AIR CONDITIONING EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENT

Any membership that includes air conditioning equipment must have its anniversary date established between May 15 and September 15.

## SECTION 7: SERVICE REMINDERS AND MEMBER SCHEDULING OBLIGATIONS

### 7.1 Reminder Notifications

Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air will send a first reminder notice via email to the address provided by the member one month prior to the anniversary date. Between the date of the first reminder and the anniversary date, Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air will make at least one telephone contact attempt to the member.

### 7.2 Scheduling Deadline

Members are responsible for scheduling their annual tune-up service within 45 days following their anniversary date.

### 7.3 Consequences of Failure to Schedule

If Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air has made at least two contact attempts and receives no response from the member 60 days past the anniversary date, Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air may forgo performance of that year's tune-up service with no refund to the member for such tune-up. If additional tune-up services remain available under the membership term, Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air will repeat the reminder and contact process in the following year. The member's club benefits will remain in effect through the remainder of the membership term.

## SECTION 8: MEMBERSHIP CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

If a member elects to cancel their Meridian Comfort Club membership, the member is entitled to receive a refund for any remaining tune-up services not yet performed under the membership. Upon cancellation, the member immediately forfeits all additional club benefits and discount privileges.

## SECTION 9: RELOCATION AND MEMBERSHIP TRANSFER

In the event a member relocates to a different residence, the member may select one of the following options:

(a) Request a refund for any remaining tune-up services owed under the membership;

(b) Transfer the membership to the new residence, provided that: (i) the new residence is located within Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air's service area; and (ii) the equipment type is included on Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air's approved equipment list; or

(c) Transfer the remaining tune-up services to the new resident occupying the original address under the Meridian Comfort Club membership terms.

## SECTION 10: COVERED EQUIPMENT

Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air provides maintenance services for most major manufacturers and brands of heating and cooling equipment, including furnaces, air handlers, heat pumps, air conditioners, split systems, ductless heat pump systems, tankless water heaters, and approved generator units.
