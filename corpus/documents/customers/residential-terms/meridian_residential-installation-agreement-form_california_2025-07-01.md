RESIDENTIAL INSTALLATION AGREEMENT

This Residential Installation Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of July 1, 2025, by and between Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air ("Company"), and John Martinez and Patricia Martinez, husband and wife ("Customer"), regarding the installation of certain equipment and services at the Customer's residential property located at 4287 Sycamore Street, Sacramento, California 95819 ("Property").

1. ACCEPTANCE AND ENTIRE AGREEMENT

These terms and conditions constitute the entire agreement between the Company and the Customer concerning the sale of materials and equipment ("Products") and the provision of services ("Services") by the Company to the Customer and supersede all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, negotiations, representations, warranties, and communications, both written and oral. Except as otherwise provided herein, all sales are final. The Customer's acceptance of this Agreement is indicated by the Customer's signature below and constitutes the Customer's acknowledgment that the Customer has read, understands, and agrees to be bound by all terms and conditions herein.

2. PROPOSAL AND PRICE

2.1 Scope of Work. The Company proposes to provide the following Services and Products at the Property: (a) removal and replacement of the existing HVAC system with a new Lennox XC25 central air conditioning and heating system with a rated capacity of 5 tons, including ductwork modification and thermostat installation; and (b) removal and replacement of the existing water heater with a new Rheem Professional Classic Series electric water heater with a 50-gallon capacity and Energy Star certification.

2.2 Proposal Price. The total proposal price for all Services and Products described in Section 2.1 is Twenty Thousand Seven Hundred Dollars ($20,700.00), exclusive of applicable California sales tax. This price is based upon the conditions and specifications as described herein and as visually ascertained by the Company's representative during the in-home comfort consultation conducted on June 28, 2025.

2.3 Price Components. The proposal price consists of the following:

(a) HVAC System Replacement: Eighteen Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($18,500.00)
    - Equipment and materials: Twelve Thousand Dollars ($12,000.00)
    - Installation labor and ductwork modification: Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000.00)
    - Thermostat and smart controls: One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($1,500.00)

(b) Water Heater Replacement: Two Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($2,200.00)
    - Equipment and materials: One Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($1,200.00)
    - Installation labor and permits: One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00)

2.4 Payment Terms. Unless a financing agreement has been executed or alternative payment arrangements have been approved in writing by the Company, the Customer shall pay the full proposal price upon completion of Services and installation of Products, prior to operation of the systems. Invoiced amounts are exclusive of, and the Customer agrees to pay, applicable California sales tax and related fees. All orders not paid in full by the due date are subject to a late payment charge of 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance.

2.5 Changes and Additional Charges. Any alteration or deviation from the specifications contained herein involving extra costs will be executed only upon written orders signed by both the Company and the Customer and will incur an extra charge over and above the proposal price. The Company reserves the right to charge the Customer additional fees resulting from: (i) incorrect information furnished by the Customer; (ii) deviations from specifications provided by the Customer; (iii) causes beyond the Company's control, including but not limited to supply chain disruptions, regulatory changes, and third-party failures; and (iv) concealed physical conditions encountered at the Property that differ materially from that which was visually ascertained.

2.6 Concealed Conditions. If conditions and/or circumstances are encountered at the Property which are concealed physical conditions or unknown physical conditions of an unusual nature that differ materially from that which was visually ascertained during the initial consultation, the Customer agrees to pay for additional labor and materials necessary to address such conditions. The Company will provide the Customer with a written estimate of any such additional charges before proceeding with work beyond the original scope.

3. RIGHT TO CANCEL

3.1 Three-Day Cancellation Right. Pursuant to California Civil Code Section 1689, the Customer has the right to cancel this Agreement without penalty or obligation within three (3) business days from the date of this Agreement. The three-day cancellation period is calculated as June 28, 2025 (date of solicitation), July 1, 2025 (date of Agreement), and expires at 5:00 p.m. on July 3, 2025.

3.2 Notice of Right to Cancel. The Customer acknowledges receipt of a separate written notice of the Customer's right to cancel, which has been provided in compliance with California Civil Code Section 1689.1. This notice is printed in at least 10-point type and clearly explains the Customer's cancellation rights.

3.3 Method of Cancellation. To cancel this Agreement, the Customer shall mail, deliver, or transmit by facsimile or email a written notice of cancellation to the Company at the address set forth on Page 1 of this Agreement. The notice of cancellation shall be effective upon receipt by the Company, regardless of the method of transmission.

3.4 Effect of Cancellation. If the Customer cancels this Agreement within the three-day period, the Company shall, within thirty (30) days, refund all payments made by the Customer, including any down payment or deposit. If any Products or Services have been partially performed with the Customer's prior written consent before the cancellation notice is received, the Customer remains obligated to pay only for the reasonable value of work performed up to the time of cancellation.

3.5 Exclusions. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the right to cancel does not apply if: (a) the Customer has agreed in writing that the performance of Services shall commence before the expiration of the three-day cancellation period and acknowledges that the Customer waives any right to cancel once performance has commenced; or (b) the Services or Products consist entirely of labor and the Customer's cost is not reasonably estimable in advance.

4. PERMITS AND INSPECTIONS

4.1 Permit Responsibility. The Company shall obtain all necessary building permits and licenses required by the County of Sacramento and the City of Sacramento to perform the Services and install the Products described in this Agreement, including permits for HVAC system replacement and water heater installation.

4.2 Permit Costs. The cost of all permits, plan review fees, and inspection fees are included within the proposal price specified in Section 2.2. The Customer shall not be charged separately for permit costs unless permit requirements change after the date of this Agreement due to code amendments or other regulatory changes.

4.3 Inspections. The Customer is responsible for scheduling and paying for any final inspections required by the local building department to approve the completed work. The Company will coordinate inspection scheduling, and typical inspection costs range from Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00) to One Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($125.00).

4.4 Compliance with Code. All work shall be performed in compliance with the current California Title 24 Energy Code and all applicable local building codes and regulations. The Company warrants that its installation will meet or exceed all applicable code requirements.

5. WARRANTY

5.1 Manufacturer's Warranty. The Customer shall receive the full benefit of all manufacturer warranties covering the Products supplied hereunder. These warranties include: (a) the Lennox XC25 HVAC system, which carries a ten-year compressor warranty and five-year parts warranty, as issued by Lennox International Inc.; and (b) the Rheem Professional water heater, which carries a six-year limited tank warranty and one-year parts warranty, as issued by Rheem Manufacturing Company. Manufacturer warranties are expressly limited to repair or replacement of defective parts and do not include labor costs for removal, reinstallation, or related services.

5.2 Company Labor Warranty. The Company warrants that all labor performed in connection with the installation of the Products and the performance of Services will be completed in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards and local building codes. The Company warrants all labor performed under this Agreement for a period of one (1) year from the date of system startup and final inspection. If defects in workmanship are discovered within this one-year period, the Company will return to the Property and, at its sole option, either correct the defective workmanship at no additional charge or refund the labor portion of the proposal price.

5.3 Warranty Limitations. The Company's one-year labor warranty does not cover: (a) damage resulting from Customer misuse, abuse, or neglect of the Products; (b) failure to maintain routine maintenance as recommended by the Company; (c) normal wear and tear; (d) damage caused by acts of God, including flooding, lightning, or severe weather; (e) modification or attempted repair of the Products by persons other than the Company or the manufacturer; or (f) failure to operate the system in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and the Company's guidance.

5.4 Maintenance. To maintain all warranty coverage, the Customer shall have the HVAC system professionally serviced at least annually by a qualified HVAC technician, and the water heater shall be drained and flushed annually to remove sediment. Failure to maintain the Products may void the manufacturer's warranty and the Company's labor warranty.

6. COMFORT CLUB MEMBERSHIP

6.1 Membership Offer. As an optional benefit for new installations, the Company offers enrollment in the Meridian Comfort Club, an annual maintenance and service membership program. The initial enrollment year is offered at no charge (the "Complimentary Year"). During the Complimentary Year, which shall extend for twelve (12) months from the date of system startup and final inspection, the Customer will receive priority scheduling, two seasonal maintenance visits per year, and discounted rates on emergency service calls.

6.2 Renewal Terms. Unless the Customer cancels the Comfort Club membership in writing at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the Complimentary Year, membership shall automatically renew for successive one-month periods at the rate of Nineteen Dollars and Ninety-Five Cents ($19.95) per month. Renewal charges will be automatically charged to the payment method provided by the Customer.

6.3 Membership Benefits. During any renewal period, the Customer shall receive: (a) two seasonal maintenance visits per calendar year, scheduled at times convenient to the Customer; (b) fifteen percent (15%) discount on all repair and service calls; (c) priority emergency service availability; (d) system tune-up and efficiency check; and (e) filter replacement and system cleaning.

6.4 Cancellation. The Customer may cancel the Comfort Club membership at any time by providing written notice to the Company at least thirty (30) days prior to the renewal date. Cancellation can be requested by telephone at (916) 447-2100, by mail to the address on Page 1, or by email to service@meridianmechanical.com. Upon cancellation, no further monthly charges will be assessed.

6.5 No Automatic Renewal Without Affirmative Consent. The Company acknowledges that the initial Complimentary Year constitutes a clear and conspicuous disclosure of the material terms of the membership, including the automatic renewal feature and the monthly renewal rate of $19.95. The Customer has affirmatively consented to the automatic renewal terms by signing this Agreement.

Financing is available for qualified customers with approved credit. The proposal price shall remain the same; only the method and timing of payment will be affected by financing.

8. MECHANICS LIEN WARNING

8.1 Mechanics Lien Rights. UNDER CALIFORNIA LAW, MECHANICS HAVE A RIGHT TO FILE A LIEN ON YOUR PROPERTY IF THEY ARE NOT PAID FOR SERVICES AND MATERIALS. A MECHANICS LIEN MAY BE FILED FOR UP TO NINETY (90) DAYS AFTER THE LAST DATE WORK IS PERFORMED OR MATERIALS ARE FURNISHED, AND IF NOT PAID, THE LIEN COULD RESULT IN LOSS OF YOUR PROPERTY THROUGH FORECLOSURE. TO PROTECT YOURSELF AGAINST A MECHANICS LIEN, YOU MAY REQUIRE A SIGNED RELEASE OF LIEN RIGHTS FROM THE CONTRACTOR BEFORE MAKING PAYMENT. THE CONTRACTOR IS REQUIRED TO PROVIDE YOU WITH A NOTICE OF LIEN RIGHTS IF PAYMENT IS NOT MADE.

8.2 Lien Waiver. Upon final payment of the entire proposal price and any additional charges incurred, the Company shall provide the Customer with a signed, unconditional release of mechanics lien rights. This lien waiver shall be provided within five (5) business days following receipt of final payment.

8.3 Subcontractors and Materialmen. The Company may employ subcontractors or purchase materials from third parties in connection with performing Services under this Agreement. The Customer acknowledges that subcontractors and suppliers also have mechanics lien rights and may file liens on the Property if not paid. The Company shall ensure that all subcontractors and suppliers are paid in full from funds provided by the Customer.

9. ARBITRATION

9.1 Disputes Subject to Arbitration. Except as otherwise provided in Section 9.5, any dispute, claim, or controversy between the Customer and the Company arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Products, the Services, or any related matters shall be resolved exclusively by binding arbitration administered by JAMS (Judicial Arbitration and Mediation Services) in Sacramento County, California, rather than in court.

9.2 Arbitration Procedures. The arbitration shall be governed by JAMS Comprehensive Arbitration Rules & Procedures, as modified by this Agreement. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single neutral arbitrator with experience in construction and HVAC/plumbing matters. The arbitrator shall issue a written decision including findings of fact and conclusions of law. The decision shall be final and binding and may be entered as a judgment in any court of competent jurisdiction.

9.3 Costs and Fees. The Company shall pay all filing fees and arbitrator costs. The Customer shall pay the Customer's own attorneys' fees and costs unless the arbitrator awards such fees and costs to the Customer as provided by law. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if the Customer demonstrates financial hardship, the Company shall pay the Customer's filing fees.

9.4 Opt-Out Right. The Customer may opt out of this arbitration agreement by providing written notice of opt-out to the Company within thirty (30) days from the date of this Agreement. Notice of opt-out must be sent by certified mail to the Company's address listed on Page 1. If the Customer timely opts out, all other terms of this Agreement remain in effect, but any dispute shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Sacramento County, California.

9.5 Exceptions to Arbitration. Notwithstanding Section 9.1, the following matters shall not be subject to arbitration: (a) any claim for workers' compensation or unemployment insurance benefits; (b) claims by the Company to collect unpaid invoices or payment obligations; (c) claims arising out of the Company's collection activities; or (d) requests for injunctive relief or other equitable remedies to prevent irreparable harm.
