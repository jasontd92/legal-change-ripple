3. MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS

The Meridian Comfort Club annual membership includes the following benefits during the membership year (January 1 through December 31):

3.1 HEATING SYSTEM TUNE-UP. One precision heating system tune-up per membership year. The tune-up includes inspection of the furnace, heat exchanger, thermostat, blower motor, electrical connections, and gas lines; lubrication of moving parts; replacement of furnace filter; safety testing; and a detailed written report of system condition. The member must schedule this service and have it completed between September 1 and November 30 of each membership year.

3.2 COOLING SYSTEM TUNE-UP. One precision air conditioning system tune-up per membership year. The tune-up includes inspection of the condenser coil, evaporator coil, refrigerant lines, thermostat, blower motor, electrical connections, and condensate drain; cleaning of accessible coils; replacement of air filter; refrigerant charge verification; and a detailed written report of system condition. The member must schedule this service and have it completed between April 1 and June 30 of each membership year.

3.3 ANNUAL PLUMBING INSPECTION. One comprehensive plumbing system inspection per membership year. The inspection includes examination of visible water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, water heater condition, and water pressure; testing of sump pump (if applicable); and identification of any apparent leaks, corrosion, or maintenance concerns. A written report of findings will be provided to the member.

3.4 PRIORITY SCHEDULING. Members receive priority scheduling for service appointments, with preference for available appointments within 24 business hours of the requested date, subject to current demand and service availability.

3.5 REPAIR DISCOUNT. Members receive a 15% discount on the labor portion of any repair service performed by Meridian outside the scope of included membership benefits. The discount does not apply to material costs, service calls for no-fault diagnoses, or services under warranty.

3.6 NO AFTER-HOURS OVERTIME SURCHARGE. Members are not charged overtime premiums or after-hours service fees when emergency repairs are performed on nights, weekends, or holidays, provided the call is made to Meridian's main dispatch line at 1-800-MERIDIAN (1-800-637-4342). Normal repair charges apply.

3.7 TWO-YEAR PARTS AND LABOR WARRANTY. Meridian warrants all repair work performed under the membership benefit discount against defects in workmanship for two years from the date of service. Parts used in repairs carry the manufacturer's warranty or Meridian's two-year warranty, whichever is longer. This warranty covers only defects caused by Meridian's repair work and does not cover subsequent damage, improper use, or damage from external causes.

3.8 UNUSED BENEFITS. Tune-up appointments and inspections must be scheduled and completed during the membership year in which the benefit is available. Unused benefits do not carry over to the following year and are forfeited if not used by December 31 of the membership year.
