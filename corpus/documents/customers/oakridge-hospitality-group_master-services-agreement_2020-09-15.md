MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of September 15, 2020 (the "Effective Date"), by and between OAKRIDGE HOSPITALITY GROUP, LLC, a Washington limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1201 Pacific Avenue, Suite 900, Tacoma, Washington 98402 ("Customer"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF WASHINGTON, LLC, a Washington limited liability company with its principal place of business at 3310 South Pine Street, Tacoma, Washington 98409 ("Contractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Customer owns and operates six hotel properties in the State of Washington and requires ongoing, on-site and on-call maintenance and repair services for HVAC systems, boilers, kitchen plumbing, and domestic water heater equipment at such properties; and

WHEREAS, Contractor is engaged in the business of providing HVAC, mechanical, and plumbing services to commercial hospitality customers in the State of Washington; and

WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Contractor to provide such services on a master services basis, and Contractor desires to provide such services, all upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Customer Properties" means all real property currently owned or operated by Customer in the State of Washington, including but not limited to: the Tacoma Downtown Hotel located at 1200 Pacific Avenue, Tacoma, Washington 98402; the Tacoma Waterfront Hotel located at 2101 Ruston Way, Tacoma, Washington 98402; the Olympia Capitol Hotel located at 415 11th Avenue SW, Olympia, Washington 98501; the Seattle Eastside Hotel located at 750 Bellevue Way NE, Suite 100, Bellevue, Washington 98004; the Spokane Convention Hotel located at 10 West Third Avenue, Spokane, Washington 99201; and the Vancouver Riverside Hotel located at 1200 Esplanade Avenue, Vancouver, Washington 98660.

1.2 "Services" means all labor, materials, equipment, and services described in Section 2 below, to be performed by Contractor at the Customer Properties.

1.3 "Work Order" means a written or electronic instruction issued by Customer to Contractor requesting performance of specific Services, in accordance with Section 3 below.

1.4 "Dedicated Technician" means the full-time on-site employee of Contractor assigned to the Tacoma Downtown Hotel as further described in Section 5 below.

1.5 "Equipment" means all HVAC systems, boiler systems, kitchen plumbing systems, domestic water heater equipment, and related mechanical systems located at the Customer Properties for which Contractor is responsible under this Agreement.

1.6 "Emergency Service" means any requested service that requires immediate response outside normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time) to prevent imminent threat of property damage, health and safety hazard, or loss of essential building systems.

1.7 "Business Day" means any Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays and local holidays observed by Customer.

2. SERVICES

2.1 SCOPE OF SERVICES. Contractor shall provide the following services to Customer:

2.1.1 On-site HVAC maintenance and repair services, including but not limited to inspection, diagnosis, repair, replacement, and routine maintenance of all heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems, equipment, controls, and components at the Customer Properties;

2.1.2 On-site boiler system maintenance and repair services, including but not limited to inspection, diagnosis, repair, replacement, routine maintenance, and seasonal startup and shutdown procedures;

2.1.3 On-site kitchen plumbing services, including but not limited to inspection, diagnosis, repair, and replacement of kitchen fixtures, drain lines, supply lines, grease traps, and related plumbing equipment;

2.1.4 On-site domestic water heater services, including but not limited to inspection, diagnosis, maintenance, repair, replacement, and seasonal flushing of all domestic hot water heaters and associated equipment;

2.1.5 Twenty-four hour, seven days per week on-call emergency response services for all Equipment at the Customer Properties, with response times as specified in Section 2.2 below;

2.1.6 Provision of a Dedicated Technician at the Tacoma Downtown Hotel, as described in Section 5 below; and

2.1.7 All additional services as may be reasonably requested by Customer in writing, which services shall be performed on a time and materials basis as set forth in Section 6 below.

2.2 RESPONSE TIMES. Contractor shall respond to and commence Services as follows:

2.2.1 For Emergency Services, Contractor shall respond within two hours of receipt of notification and shall dispatch a qualified technician to the affected Customer Property.

2.2.2 For routine maintenance and repair requests submitted during Business Days, Contractor shall schedule Services to commence within one Business Day of receipt of the Work Order, or at such other time as mutually agreed upon by the parties.

2.2.3 For Services requested on weekends or holidays, Contractor shall schedule performance within one Business Day of receipt of the Work Order, or as otherwise agreed by the parties.

2.3 PERFORMANCE STANDARDS. Contractor shall perform all Services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with all applicable codes, standards, and regulations applicable in Washington State. All Services shall be performed by qualified, trained, and licensed technicians, consistent with industry standards and best practices for commercial hospitality maintenance.

2.4 MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT. Contractor shall supply all tools, equipment, materials, and supplies reasonably necessary to perform the Services, except as otherwise specified in individual Work Orders. All materials shall be new, of high quality, and suitable for the intended purpose.

2.5 SITE ACCESS AND COORDINATION. Customer shall provide Contractor and its agents, employees, and subcontractors with reasonable access to the Customer Properties during normal Business Hours and at other times as reasonably necessary to perform Services. Contractor shall coordinate with Customer's on-site management to minimize disruption to hotel operations and guest activities.

3. WORK ORDERS

3.1 ISSUANCE OF WORK ORDERS. Customer may request Services by issuing written Work Orders to Contractor. Work Orders may be submitted by email, phone, facsimile, or through other mutually agreed communication methods. Work Orders shall include: (a) the Customer Property or properties affected; (b) a description of the work to be performed; (c) any specific access requirements or timing preferences; (d) the name and contact information of the Customer contact person; and (e) any safety, security, or operational requirements specific to the requested Services.

3.2 URGENT AND EMERGENCY REQUESTS. In the case of Emergency Services, Customer may notify Contractor by telephone to the Contractor's emergency dispatch line at (253) 555-0147. Contractor shall maintain this emergency dispatch line twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week. Emergency verbal requests shall be confirmed by Customer with a follow-up written Work Order within one Business Day, unless otherwise waived in writing by Contractor.

3.3 ACCEPTANCE AND SCHEDULING. Upon receipt of a Work Order, Contractor shall acknowledge receipt of the Work Order and confirm the proposed date and time for performance, subject to Contractor's schedule and availability. Contractor shall not unreasonably withhold scheduling of requested Services. In the event of conflicting requests, Contractor shall prioritize Emergency Services.

3.4 MODIFICATIONS TO WORK ORDERS. Any modifications to the scope, timing, or specifications set forth in a Work Order shall require written amendment signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Contractor shall perform only the Services specified in the applicable Work Order, or as subsequently amended in writing.

4. ON-SITE REQUIREMENTS

4.1 SAFETY COMPLIANCE. While performing Services at the Customer Properties, Contractor, its agents, employees, and subcontractors shall comply with all federal, state, and local safety laws, regulations, and codes, including without limitation the Occupational Safety and Health Act and all Washington State Department of Labor and Industries standards and regulations applicable to the Services. Contractor shall be solely responsible for the safety of its employees and shall ensure that all employees maintain current certifications and training as required by law.

4.2 HOTEL OPERATIONS PROTOCOL. Contractor, its agents, employees, and subcontractors shall comply, in all respects, with Customer's operational requirements, guest service protocols, physical access procedures, security requirements, and other policies applicable to the Customer Properties, copies of which shall be provided by Customer upon request. Contractor shall at all times conduct itself in a professional manner and shall not interfere with Customer's hotel operations, guest activities, or other service providers.

4.3 PROPERTY PROTECTION. Contractor shall exercise reasonable care to protect the Customer Properties and the personal property of Customer and its guests during performance of Services. Contractor shall use protective coverings and shall take all reasonable precautions to prevent damage to floors, furnishings, walls, and other building components. Any damage caused by Contractor, its agents, employees, or subcontractors during performance of Services shall be the sole responsibility of Contractor.

4.4 CLEAN-UP. Upon completion of Services at each Customer Property, Contractor, at its cost and expense, shall promptly remove all excess materials, equipment, packaging, and debris within the scope of its performance and shall leave the work area in a clean and orderly condition. Should Contractor fail to perform such clean-up within one Business Day of completion of Services, Customer shall have the right to engage a third party to perform such clean-up and set off the reasonable costs of such clean-up against amounts otherwise owed by Customer to Contractor.

4.5 CREDENTIALS AND IDENTIFICATION. The Dedicated Technician and all other Contractor personnel performing Services at the Customer Properties shall carry valid identification and shall provide such identification upon request. All Contractor personnel shall present themselves in professional attire consistent with Customer's standards for on-site service personnel.

4.6 BACKGROUND CHECKS. Contractor's employees who will have regular or frequent access to the Customer Properties shall successfully complete a criminal background check and drug screening, at Contractor's expense, prior to commencing performance of Services and annually thereafter. Contractor shall notify Customer immediately of any arrests, convictions, or other legal issues involving such employees.

5. DEDICATED TECHNICIAN

5.1 ASSIGNMENT. Contractor shall assign a full-time, on-site employee (the "Dedicated Technician") to the Tacoma Downtown Hotel to provide on-site maintenance, repair, response, and operational support services. The Dedicated Technician shall report to the Hotel Manager at the Tacoma Downtown Hotel for all on-site operational and access matters.

5.2 QUALIFICATIONS. The Dedicated Technician shall possess or obtain, at Contractor's expense, current EPA Section 608 Certification for all applicable refrigerant categories, Washington State HVAC Contractor license, Washington State plumber license or journeyman plumber permit, and such other certifications and credentials as may be required or reasonably requested by Customer. The Dedicated Technician shall be experienced in commercial hospitality HVAC and plumbing systems and shall receive training specific to the Customer Properties at Contractor's expense.

5.3 AVAILABILITY. The Dedicated Technician shall be available at the Tacoma Downtown Hotel during all normal Business Hours, Monday through Friday, 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific Time, and shall be on-call for emergency response outside such hours. Contractor shall provide backup coverage by other qualified technicians during the Dedicated Technician's scheduled days off, vacation, illness, or other absences.

5.4 PERFORMANCE DUTIES. The Dedicated Technician shall: (a) perform routine maintenance on all Equipment at the Tacoma Downtown Hotel in accordance with manufacturer specifications and industry standards; (b) respond immediately to urgent and emergency requests from hotel staff; (c) maintain accurate maintenance logs and equipment records; (d) coordinate with other service providers at the hotel; (e) report to hotel management any equipment deficiencies, safety issues, or recommended improvements; and (f) perform minor repairs and adjustments as appropriate to maintain Equipment in good working condition.

5.5 REPLACEMENT. Should the Dedicated Technician become unavailable due to termination, resignation, illness, or other cause, Contractor shall assign a replacement Dedicated Technician within five Business Days or as soon as reasonably practicable. Any replacement Dedicated Technician shall meet the qualifications set forth in Section 5.2 above.

5.6 TERMINATION. Customer may require Contractor to replace the Dedicated Technician at any time for performance deficiencies, unprofessional conduct, safety violations, or other cause, and Contractor shall comply with such request within five Business Days.

6. PRICING AND MATERIALS

6.1 PRICING STRUCTURE. Contractor's pricing for Services shall be as set forth in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated by reference, which pricing shall be in effect during the initial Term of this Agreement as specified in Section 8.1 below. Pricing is subject to annual adjustment upon renewal of this Agreement in accordance with Section 8.2 below.

6.2 DEDICATED TECHNICIAN FEE. Customer shall pay Contractor a monthly fee of Fourteen Thousand Eight Hundred Fifty Dollars ($14,850.00) for the provision of the Dedicated Technician at the Tacoma Downtown Hotel, which fee shall include all compensation, benefits, payroll taxes, workers' compensation insurance, and other employment-related costs. This monthly fee shall be invoiced on the first Business Day of each calendar month during the Term and shall be due within thirty days of invoice.

6.3 LABOR RATES FOR ADDITIONAL SERVICES. For Services rendered at Customer Properties other than the Tacoma Downtown Hotel, and for services beyond the scope of routine maintenance duties by the Dedicated Technician, Contractor's labor rates shall be One Hundred Twenty-Six Dollars ($126.00) per hour for regular-time labor and One Hundred Sixty-Two Dollars ($162.00) per hour for Emergency Services, overtime labor, and services performed outside normal Business Hours. Labor shall be billed in quarter-hour increments.

6.4 MATERIALS PRICING. Contractor shall charge Customer for all materials, parts, and supplies used in performance of Services at cost, plus a mark-up of twenty percent (20%). "Cost" shall mean Contractor's actual and documented cost of purchase from suppliers, including shipping and handling charges. Contractor shall provide itemized material invoices supporting all material charges, and Customer may request and inspect original supplier invoices.

6.5 TRAVEL AND TRANSPORTATION. Reasonable travel time between Customer Properties shall be billed at fifty percent (50%) of the applicable labor rate. Travel time from Contractor's facility to the first Customer Property of each day shall not be charged. Contractor's personnel shall utilize fuel-efficient vehicles, and fuel and vehicle expenses shall not be charged separately but shall be included in the monthly Dedicated Technician fee or the applicable labor rates.

6.6 PARTS AND EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT. Customer shall be charged for replacement parts and equipment at cost plus twenty percent (20%), as stated in Section 6.4 above. For major equipment replacement (repair costs exceeding One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00)), Contractor shall provide Customer with a written estimate prior to commencing work, and Customer shall have the right to approve or reject such repair prior to proceeding. Contractor shall not proceed with any major repair without written approval from Customer.

6.7 PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE. Contractor shall include routine preventive maintenance visits as part of the Dedicated Technician's regular duties and shall not charge separately for such maintenance. Preventive maintenance services shall include quarterly HVAC system inspections, annual boiler inspections, and such other routine maintenance as is consistent with industry standards and manufacturer recommendations.

7. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 MONTHLY INVOICES. Contractor shall submit itemized invoices to Customer on a monthly basis. Invoices shall clearly state: (a) the Dedicated Technician monthly fee; (b) all labor charges, including date, time, technician name, Property, description of Services, and hours worked; (c) all materials and parts, with descriptions and costs; (d) any other charges; and (e) the total amount due. Invoices shall be submitted to Customer's attention at the address set forth in Section 20 below or to such other address as Customer may designate in writing.

7.2 INVOICE PAYMENT TERMS. Invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of invoice date. Customer shall remit payment to Contractor at the address specified on the invoice or to such other address as Contractor may designate. Payment shall be made by check, electronic funds transfer, or other mutually agreed method.

7.3 ACCEPTANCE OF INVOICES. Customer shall pay invoices within the specified payment terms without further acceptance or approval. Customer may dispute any invoice within fifteen (15) days of receipt by providing written notice specifying the nature of the dispute. The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any invoice disputes within thirty (30) days of notice.

7.4 LATE PAYMENT. If Customer fails to pay any invoice by the due date, Contractor may charge interest on the unpaid amount at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by Washington law, whichever is less. In addition, Contractor may suspend performance of Services, including Emergency Services, until all past due invoices are paid in full.

7.5 CREDIT VERIFICATION. Contractor reserves the right to verify Customer's creditworthiness prior to commencing Services and may require Customer to provide references or credit information. Contractor also reserves the right to require payment in advance for services if Customer's credit becomes questionable or if an invoice remains unpaid for more than fifteen (15) days beyond the due date.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 INITIAL TERM. This Agreement shall commence on September 15, 2020 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue through September 14, 2023 (the "Initial Term"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with this Section 8.

8.2 RENEWAL. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods (each a "Renewal Term"), unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other party at least ninety (90) days prior to expiration of the then-current Term. Pricing shall be subject to adjustment upon each renewal, with any proposed increase not to exceed the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index (Seattle-Tacoma metropolitan area) for the prior twelve-month period, plus three percent (3%).

8.3 TERMINATION WITHOUT CAUSE. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon ninety (90) days' written notice to the other party, provided that: (a) such notice is given during the Initial Term or any Renewal Term; (b) all outstanding invoices are paid in full as of the termination date; and (c) Contractor is afforded the opportunity to recover or dispose of its tools, equipment, and materials as permitted by Customer.

8.4 TERMINATION FOR MATERIAL BREACH. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any material term or condition of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail.

8.5 TRANSITION SERVICES. Upon termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall: (a) discontinue performance of Services as of the termination date; (b) remove its equipment, tools, and materials from the Customer Properties; (c) return to Customer any Customer property in Contractor's possession; (d) provide Customer with all maintenance records, equipment logs, and other documentation related to Services performed under this Agreement; and (e) cooperate with any successor contractor in transitioning the Services.

8.6 SURVIVAL OF OBLIGATIONS. Expiration or termination of this Agreement shall not relieve either party of its obligations that by their nature are intended to survive termination, including but not limited to obligations under Sections 9 (Warranty), 10 (Insurance), 11 (Indemnification), 12 (Limitation of Liability), and 21 (Governing Law).

EXHIBIT A

PRICING SCHEDULE

1. DEDICATED TECHNICIAN

Monthly Fee: $14,850.00 per month
Includes: Full-time on-site technician at Tacoma Downtown Hotel, all compensation and employment-related costs, routine preventive maintenance, daily on-site response to maintenance and repair requests during Business Hours (6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday through Friday), and on-call emergency response services.

The monthly fee shall be invoiced on the first Business Day of each calendar month and shall be due within thirty (30) days of invoice date.


2. LABOR RATES FOR ADDITIONAL SERVICES

Regular Time Labor (Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.): $126.00 per hour
Emergency Services Labor (all hours outside regular time, including weekends, holidays, and overnight): $162.00 per hour

Labor shall be billed in quarter-hour increments. Travel time between Customer Properties shall be billed at $63.00 per hour (50% of regular rate). Travel from Contractor's facility to the first Customer Property of each day shall not be charged.


3. MATERIALS AND PARTS

All materials, parts, equipment, and supplies used in performance of Services shall be charged at Contractor's actual documented cost, plus twenty percent (20%) mark-up.

Cost shall include Contractor's purchase price from suppliers, plus reasonable and documented freight, shipping, and delivery charges.

Contractor shall provide itemized invoices documenting all material charges, with supporting supplier invoices available upon Customer request.


4. PRICING ADJUSTMENTS

Pricing shall remain fixed through September 14, 2023.

Upon renewal of this Agreement for any Renewal Term beyond the Initial Term, Contractor may propose pricing adjustments not to exceed the annual percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index (Seattle-Tacoma metropolitan area) plus three percent (3%). Any proposed increase shall be provided to Customer at least sixty (60) days prior to renewal.


5. TRAVEL AND MILEAGE

Fuel and vehicle expenses are included in labor rates and monthly dedicated technician fee. Reasonable multi-property trips on a single day shall be combined; travel time between properties shall be calculated from the first property to the last property visited and divided equally among all properties visited on that day.

For services at properties other than Tacoma Downtown Hotel, travel time shall be charged at fifty percent (50%) of the applicable labor rate for any travel time in excess of thirty (30) minutes one-way from Contractor's facility.


6. ESTIMATE REQUIREMENTS

For any individual service event (emergency or routine) estimated to exceed $500.00 in labor and materials, Contractor shall provide Customer with a written estimate prior to commencing work.

For any major repair or equipment replacement estimated to exceed $1,000.00, Contractor shall provide a written estimate and shall not proceed without written approval from Customer's authorized representative.

Emergency estimates may be provided verbally, with written confirmation to follow within one Business Day.


7. PAYMENT TERMS

All invoices are due and payable within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

Late payment shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month (or the maximum rate permitted by Washington law) on any unpaid balance after the due date.

Contractor may suspend performance of Services (including Emergency Services) if any invoice remains unpaid fifteen (15) days beyond the due date, following written notice to Customer.

END OF EXHIBIT A
