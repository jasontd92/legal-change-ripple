WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Contractor to provide preventive maintenance, repair, and replacement services for plumbing and HVAC systems at the Properties; and

WHEREAS, Contractor represents that it is qualified, licensed, and willing to provide such services on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

2. SERVICES

2.1 SCOPE OF SERVICES. During the Term of this Agreement, Contractor shall provide the following Services to Customer:

(a) Quarterly Preventive Maintenance. Contractor shall perform quarterly preventive maintenance inspections and servicing of plumbing and HVAC systems at each of the Properties in accordance with the PM Services schedule and specifications set forth in Exhibit A, Schedule 1.

(b) Emergency Repair Services. Contractor shall provide twenty-four (24) hour emergency repair services for plumbing and HVAC system failures and emergency conditions at the Properties. Customer may request emergency services by telephone, email, or through Contractor's online portal, and Contractor shall respond to such requests with reasonable promptness. Emergency services shall be performed at the After-Hours Billing Rate set forth in Exhibit A.

(c) Maintenance and Repair Services. Contractor shall provide maintenance, repair, and replacement services as requested by Customer via Work Order. All non-emergency work shall be performed during regular business hours unless otherwise agreed in writing.

(d) System Inspections. Contractor shall perform inspections of plumbing and HVAC systems as requested by Customer and shall provide written reports of findings within five (5) business days of completion.

2.2 INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT. Customer acknowledges that Contractor shall make an independent assessment of the work required at each Property. Contractor shall provide written quotes or estimates for work beyond routine maintenance and repair, and such quotes shall be valid for thirty (30) calendar days unless otherwise specified.

2.3 CONTRACTOR PERFORMANCE STANDARDS. Contractor shall perform all Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in compliance with all applicable laws, codes, and regulations in California and the jurisdictions where the Properties are located. Contractor shall comply with all environmental, safety, and OSHA regulations applicable to its work.

2.4 CONTRACTOR PERSONNEL. Contractor shall provide all labor and personnel necessary to perform the Services. All personnel shall be properly trained, licensed, and qualified for the work performed. Contractor shall be responsible for background checks, training, and ongoing compliance of its personnel with applicable regulations.

2.5 SCHEDULING. Contractor shall coordinate scheduling of Services with Customer's facilities managers or designated representatives. For non-emergency work, Contractor shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice unless Customer requests otherwise.

2.6 EXCLUDED SERVICES. This Agreement does not include: (a) construction of new plumbing or HVAC systems; (b) systems at properties outside the fourteen (14) Properties listed in Section 1.1; (c) services required due to Customer's negligence or failure to maintain systems; (d) services for systems installed after the Effective Date unless separately agreed in writing; or (e) any services not specifically authorized by written Work Order or this Agreement.

3. WORK ORDERS

3.1 WORK ORDER REQUIREMENTS. Except for Services under the PM Services schedule and emergency services within the scope of Section 2.1(b), Customer shall request Services by issuing a written Work Order in the form substantially similar to Exhibit B-1. Work Orders shall include: (a) property address and location; (b) description of work requested; (c) date(s) and time(s) requested; (d) customer contact person and phone number; and (e) any special instructions or access requirements.

3.2 WORK ORDER AUTHORIZATION. Each Work Order shall be signed by a representative of Customer authorized to bind Customer. Contractor shall not be obligated to perform work without a valid Work Order unless performing PM Services or responding to a documented emergency.

3.3 WORK ORDER ACCEPTANCE. Contractor shall respond to each Work Order within one (1) business day, indicating whether it can perform the requested Services and, if applicable, providing an estimate of costs and time required. Work Orders shall not be deemed accepted by Contractor unless Contractor provides written confirmation.

3.4 CHANGES TO WORK ORDERS. Any changes to the scope of work described in a Work Order, or any additional work not contemplated by the Work Order, shall require a written change order signed by both parties, specifying the additional work, revised timeline, and adjusted pricing before Contractor is obligated to perform such additional work.

3.5 WORK ORDER TRACKING. Contractor shall maintain a system for tracking all Work Orders and shall provide Customer with monthly status reports, including Work Orders received, in progress, and completed, upon request.

4. PRICING

4.1 LABOR RATES. Contractor's hourly labor rates for the Services are set forth in Exhibit A, Schedule 2, according to the trade classifications listed therein. The rates are in effect as of the Effective Date. Labor rates shall be billed based on actual time spent performing Services, rounded to the nearest quarter (0.25) hour.

4.2 AFTER-HOURS MULTIPLIER. All work performed during After-Hours shall be billed at one and one-half times (1.5x) the applicable Billing Rate for the trade classification performing the work.

4.3 MATERIALS COST. All Materials required to perform the Services shall be charged to Customer at Contractor's cost plus twenty percent (20%) markup. Contractor shall submit invoices with documentation of the cost of Materials, such as supplier invoices, receipts, or quotations, upon Customer's request.

Material Cost Adjustments. Contractor may adjust the pricing for Materials upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Customer to reflect documented increases in Contractor's cost of Materials, including increases resulting from tariffs, duties, or other governmental charges imposed after the Effective Date. Any adjustment shall be limited to Contractor's actual documented cost increase, without additional markup, and shall apply only to Work Orders issued after the effective date of the notice.

4.4 QUARTERLY PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE FEES. In addition to labor and Materials, Contractor shall charge quarterly PM fees for each Property as set forth in Exhibit A, Schedule 1. These fees shall be charged once per calendar quarter for the scheduled PM Services. The quarterly fees shall include all labor, travel time, and routine materials such as filters, belts, and gaskets normally included in a PM visit.

4.5 TRAVEL TIME. Contractor may charge travel time between Properties at a rate of fifty percent (50%) of the applicable Billing Rate for the trade providing services, provided that travel time to the first Property visited on any day and return travel from the last Property shall not be charged. For Properties more than twenty (20) miles from Contractor's principal place of business, Contractor may charge mileage at the current Internal Revenue Service standard mileage rate.

4.6 MINIMUM SERVICE CHARGES. Contractor reserves the right to establish a minimum service charge of one-half (0.5) hour of labor for any service call to a Property, including emergency and non-emergency visits, except for emergency calls that do not require any work to be performed.

4.7 EQUIPMENT AND TOOLS. The cost of specialized equipment rental, if required to perform the Services, shall be charged to Customer at Contractor's cost plus twenty percent (20%) markup. Small hand tools and standard equipment maintained by Contractor shall be included in the labor rates and shall not be separately charged.

5. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 INVOICES. Contractor shall issue invoices for Services performed and Materials supplied under this Agreement. Each invoice shall include: (a) invoice number and date; (b) Property address(es); (c) dates services were performed; (d) description of work completed; (e) labor hours by trade classification and applicable rate; (f) itemized Materials and Equipment costs with documentation; (g) total amount due; and (h) invoice due date and payment instructions.

5.2 PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) calendar days of the invoice date ("Net 30"). Payment shall be made to Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., at the address designated in writing by Contractor, or by electronic transfer to an account designated by Contractor.

5.3 LATE PAYMENT. If any invoice remains unpaid more than five (5) days after the due date, Contractor may charge interest on the unpaid balance at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by California law, whichever is less. Contractor may also suspend performance of non-emergency Services under this Agreement until all outstanding invoices are paid in full.

5.4 DISPUTED INVOICES. If Customer disputes any charge on an invoice, Customer shall notify Contractor in writing within ten (10) calendar days of receipt of the invoice, specifying the disputed items and the reason for the dispute. Contractor and Customer shall meet and attempt to resolve the dispute within ten (10) calendar days. The undisputed portion of each invoice shall remain due and payable in accordance with Section 5.2.

5.5 ACCEPTED QUOTATIONS. If Contractor provides a written quotation for work beyond routine maintenance, and Customer verbally authorizes Contractor to proceed, Contractor shall not be obligated to begin work until it receives written authorization or a signed Work Order. Customers authorizing work verbally shall still be responsible for paying the full quoted price plus Materials, unless the Work Order specifies otherwise.

5.6 QUARTERLY BILLING. Quarterly PM fees shall be invoiced monthly in advance of the month in which PM Services will be performed. If PM Services are not performed or must be rescheduled due to building access or other issues beyond Contractor's control, the PM fees shall still be due unless the parties agree in writing to reschedule the PM visit within the same quarter.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 INITIAL TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date of March 1, 2022 and shall continue through February 28, 2025 (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated by either party pursuant to the provisions of this Section 6.

6.2 RENEWAL TERMS. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year renewal periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other party at least ninety (90) calendar days prior to the expiration date of the then-current term.

6.3 TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon written notice to the other party, provided that the terminating party provides at least ninety (90) calendar days' advance written notice of its intention to terminate. If Customer terminates for convenience, Customer shall remain obligated to pay all invoices for Services performed through the effective date of termination, including all accrued quarterly PM fees.

6.4 TERMINATION FOR CAUSE. Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving written notice of the breach; or (b) becomes insolvent, bankrupt, or is assigned for the benefit of creditors. Contractor may also terminate this Agreement for cause if Customer fails to pay any invoice within thirty (30) calendar days of the due date.

6.5 EFFECT OF TERMINATION. Upon termination of this Agreement: (a) Contractor shall complete any work in progress to a safe stopping point and shall invoice Customer for such work; (b) Customer shall pay all outstanding invoices within ten (10) calendar days; (c) all confidential information shall be returned or destroyed as requested; and (d) the provisions of Sections 1 (Definitions), 7 (Warranty), 8 (Insurance), 9 (Indemnification), 10 (Limitation of Liability), 12 (Independent Contractor), 13 (Governing Law), and 14 (General Provisions) shall survive termination.

6.6 NO OBLIGATION TO PERFORM BEYOND TERM. Contractor shall have no obligation to perform Services after the expiration or earlier termination of this Agreement, except as necessary to safely shut down systems and complete work in progress.
