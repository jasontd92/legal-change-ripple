COMPREHENSIVE HVAC AND PLUMBING MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

THIS COMPREHENSIVE HVAC AND PLUMBING MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of January 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between VALLEY MEDICAL SERVICES GROUP, a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, with principal offices located at 1250 East Herndon Avenue, Fresno, California 93720 ("Customer"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation doing business as Meridian Plumbing, Heating & Air, with principal offices located at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, California 95833 ("Contractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Customer operates nine (9) outpatient medical clinics located throughout the Fresno and Sacramento metropolitan areas; and

WHEREAS, Customer requires comprehensive preventive maintenance, repair, and replacement services for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and plumbing systems at all nine clinics; and

WHEREAS, Contractor represents that it is a licensed, bonded, and insured mechanical contractor qualified to provide all such services under California law; and

WHEREAS, Customer and Contractor desire to enter into this comprehensive maintenance agreement whereby Contractor shall provide all preventive maintenance, emergency repairs, parts, and equipment replacement services on a fully inclusive basis for the duration of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that, directly or indirectly, is controlled by, controls, or is under common control with a party, where control is defined as the power to direct the establishment or management and policies of an entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

1.2 "Claim" means any third-party claim, suit, action, or demand, including claims for bodily injury, property damage, professional negligence, or economic loss.

1.3 "Equipment" means all HVAC and plumbing systems, fixtures, components, and appurtenances located at any of the Clinic Locations that are owned, operated, or used by Customer in its business operations, whether installed before or after the Effective Date.

1.4 "Emergency Service Call" means any service request received by Contractor for repairs to Equipment that result in (i) complete system failure, (ii) imminent threat to patient safety or clinic operations, or (iii) water damage or hazardous conditions. Emergency Service Calls shall be documented by the requestor with the time and nature of the emergency.

1.5 "Maintenance Year" means each calendar year during the Term of this Agreement, with the first Maintenance Year commencing January 1, 2024 and ending December 31, 2024.

1.6 "Scheduled Maintenance Visit" means a planned visit by Contractor personnel to perform preventive maintenance services as outlined in the Preventive Maintenance Schedule set forth in Exhibit A.

1.7 "Service Credits" means monetary credits issued to Customer in accordance with Section 5 of this Agreement for Contractor's failure to meet specified Service Level commitments.

1.8 "System Down Time" means any period during which Equipment is non-functional or unable to operate at intended capacity due to a failure or defect, measured from the time Customer reports the failure to Contractor until the time Contractor restores the Equipment to full operational status.

2. SCOPE OF WORK AND COVERED SERVICES

2.1 Preventive Maintenance Services. Contractor shall provide comprehensive preventive maintenance services at each of the nine (9) Clinic Locations identified in Exhibit A, including but not limited to: (a) inspection and testing of all HVAC equipment, including air handling units, rooftop units, split systems, and zone controls; (b) cleaning and replacement of air filters and furnace filters; (c) inspection, testing, and repair of all thermostats and building automation systems; (d) lubrication of bearings and mechanical components; (e) inspection of all ductwork and duct sealing as required; (f) inspection and cleaning of drain lines and condensate systems; (g) inspection and repair of all plumbing fixtures, including sinks, toilets, and faucets; (h) inspection and maintenance of hot water heaters and circulation systems; (i) inspection of all drainage and waste systems; (j) grease trap maintenance and inspection; (k) testing and inspection of backflow prevention devices; and (l) such other services as are reasonably necessary to maintain the Equipment in proper working condition.

2.2 Frequency of Maintenance Visits. Contractor shall conduct Scheduled Maintenance Visits at each Clinic Location on a quarterly basis (four times per calendar year). Each Scheduled Maintenance Visit shall be scheduled in advance with Customer's facilities manager and shall occur during normal business hours or at times mutually agreed by the parties.

2.3 Repair Services. Contractor shall respond to and perform all repairs to the Equipment, whether or not such repairs arise during Scheduled Maintenance Visits. All repairs shall be performed by licensed technicians employed by or contracted by Contractor and shall be completed in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards and all applicable laws and regulations.

2.4 Emergency Repair Services. For any Emergency Service Call, Contractor shall dispatch a qualified service technician within four (4) hours of receiving notification from Customer during Contractor's normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time). For Emergency Service Calls received outside normal business hours, Contractor shall have a technician on-call and shall respond as soon as reasonably possible, but in no event later than the start of Contractor's next business day.

2.5 Equipment Replacement and Upgrades. Contractor shall have authority to recommend Equipment replacement or significant upgrades when repair is no longer economical or when Equipment has reached the end of its useful life. No Equipment replacement or upgrade shall be performed without prior written approval from Customer, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

2.6 Excluded Services. The following services are excluded from this Agreement and shall not be the responsibility of Contractor: (a) modifications to the structure or utility infrastructure of any Clinic Location; (b) emergency code compliance upgrades required by changes in law enacted after the Effective Date; (c) services arising from Customer's negligence or misuse of Equipment; (d) equipment damage caused by third parties not employed by Contractor; (e) services provided by other contractors or vendors; and (f) cosmetic repairs unrelated to Equipment function or safety.

2.7 Workmanship Standards. All work performed by Contractor shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner, in full compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, codes, and ordinances. Contractor warrants that all work shall be performed by licensed, trained, and competent personnel and that all materials used shall be new, clean, and fit for their intended purpose.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2024 and shall expire at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on December 31, 2026, unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement (the "Initial Term").

3.2 Automatic Renewal Terms. This Agreement shall automatically renew for two consecutive additional one-year terms (each, a "Renewal Term"), unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other party at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Each Renewal Term shall commence on January 1 of the calendar year following expiration of the preceding term and shall expire at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on December 31 of that calendar year.

3.3 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time with one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice to Contractor, provided that Customer shall remain obligated to pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Contractor may terminate this Agreement only for material breach by Customer that remains uncured for thirty (30) days following written notice from Contractor.

3.4 Effect of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall: (a) cease performance of services hereunder; (b) deliver to Customer all documentation, records, and materials related to maintenance and repair of the Equipment; and (c) provide Customer with a final invoice for any unpaid fees through the date of termination.

4. ANNUAL MAINTENANCE FEE AND PAYMENT TERMS

4.1 Annual Maintenance Fee. In consideration for all services provided by Contractor under this Agreement, Customer shall pay Contractor an annual maintenance fee of Four Hundred Eighty-Six Thousand Dollars ($486,000.00) per Maintenance Year (the "Annual Maintenance Fee").

Fixed Pricing. The Annual Maintenance Fee and all rates set forth in Exhibit A are firm and fixed for the Initial Term and each Renewal Term and shall not be adjusted for any reason, including changes in the cost of labor, materials, equipment, fuel, freight, taxes, tariffs, duties or other governmental charges. Contractor assumes all risk of such cost changes.

4.2 Monthly Billing and Payment. The Annual Maintenance Fee shall be payable in twelve (12) equal monthly installments of Forty Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($40,500.00) each. The first monthly payment shall be due on January 1, 2024, and each subsequent payment shall be due on the first calendar day of each month thereafter during the Term.

4.3 Included Costs. The Annual Maintenance Fee is all-inclusive and covers the cost of all preventive maintenance services, emergency repair services, standard repair parts, all labor, service calls, diagnostic fees, and the annual Equipment replacement allowance described in Section 6.1. There shall be no additional charges or fees for services rendered unless expressly excluded in Section 2.6 or approved in writing in advance by an authorized representative of Customer.

4.4 Invoice and Payment Terms. Contractor shall submit an invoice for each monthly payment due hereunder on or before the last business day of each month. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. All payments shall be made via electronic funds transfer to an account designated in writing by Contractor.

4.5 Late Payment. If Customer fails to pay any invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt, such payment shall be considered past due. Contractor may assess a late charge of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month on the unpaid balance. If payment is not received within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date, Contractor may, at its sole discretion, suspend services until payment is received, provided that Contractor shall give Customer five (5) business days' written notice before suspending Emergency Services.

4.6 Adjustments for Term Extensions. If this Agreement is renewed for any Renewal Term, the Annual Maintenance Fee shall remain at Four Hundred Eighty-Six Thousand Dollars ($486,000.00) per calendar year unless the parties mutually agree in writing to adjust the fee. Any fee adjustment shall be documented in a written amendment executed by both parties at least thirty (30) days prior to the commencement of the Renewal Term.

5. SERVICE LEVELS AND PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

5.1 Response Time Commitment. Contractor shall maintain the following response time commitments for all service requests:

(a) For Emergency Service Calls, Contractor shall dispatch a technician within four (4) hours of Customer's notification during Contractor's normal business hours of Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time.

(b) For non-emergency repair requests submitted during normal business hours, Contractor shall schedule a service appointment within two (2) business days.

(c) For non-emergency repair requests submitted outside normal business hours, such requests shall be treated as received at 8:00 a.m. on the next business day.

5.2 Repair Completion Timeline. Contractor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete all repairs within one (1) business day of the initial service call. Repairs that cannot be completed within one business day shall be documented by Contractor with a detailed explanation of the cause of delay and an estimated completion date.

5.3 Service Level Credits. If Contractor fails to meet the response time commitments set forth in Section 5.1, Customer shall be entitled to receive Service Level Credits as follows:

(a) For each instance of failure to respond to an Emergency Service Call within four (4) hours during normal business hours, Customer shall receive a Service Credit equal to five hundred dollars ($500.00).

(b) For each instance of failure to schedule a non-emergency repair appointment within two (2) business days, Customer shall receive a Service Credit equal to two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00).

5.4 Service Credit Procedures. To claim a Service Credit, Customer must provide written notice to Contractor within ten (10) business days of the alleged failure, specifying the date, time, nature of the service request, and the failure for which credit is claimed. Contractor shall verify the claim and, if valid, issue the Service Credit by reduction of the next monthly invoice. Service Credits may not exceed ten percent (10%) of the annual maintenance fee in any single Maintenance Year.

5.5 Preventive Maintenance Compliance. Contractor shall document completion of each Scheduled Maintenance Visit and shall provide Customer with a written report within five (5) business days of each visit. The report shall identify all services performed, any issues identified, any repairs recommended, and any equipment monitored for ongoing condition assessment.

5.6 Performance Monitoring. Customer shall have the right to monitor Contractor's performance against the service level standards set forth herein. Contractor shall provide Customer with monthly performance reports and shall be available for quarterly performance review meetings.

6. PARTS AND EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT

6.1 Equipment Replacement Allowance. Included in the Annual Maintenance Fee is an annual Equipment replacement allowance of Sixty Thousand Dollars ($60,000.00) per Maintenance Year. This allowance shall be available for the purchase and installation of replacement parts and equipment that become necessary due to normal wear and tear, obsolescence, or failure of existing Equipment. The allowance shall be cumulative within each Maintenance Year and shall not carry over to the following Maintenance Year.

6.2 Equipment Replacement Process. When Equipment replacement is recommended, Contractor shall submit to Customer a written estimate identifying the equipment to be replaced, the reason for replacement, the cost of the replacement equipment and installation labor, and the expected useful life of the replacement. Customer shall have the right to approve or reject the recommendation and to obtain competitive bids from other contractors if desired.

6.3 Excess Equipment Costs. Any Equipment replacement costs that exceed the annual allowance of Sixty Thousand Dollars ($60,000.00) shall be paid by Customer at Contractor's standard labor rates and equipment pricing. Customer shall have the right to approve or deny any Equipment replacement costs in excess of the annual allowance prior to Contractor's incurrence of such costs.

6.4 Parts Quality Standards. All replacement parts used by Contractor shall be either original manufacturer parts or equivalent quality aftermarket parts that meet industry standards and original equipment manufacturer specifications. Contractor shall not use reconditioned or remanufactured parts unless Customer expressly authorizes in writing.

6.5 Warranty on Replacement Parts and Labor. Contractor warrants that all replacement parts shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of one (1) year from the date of installation. All repair work shall be warranted for a period of ninety (90) days from the date of completion.
