CUSTOMER MASTER AGREEMENT

This Customer Master Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of the 1st day of July, 2021 ("Effective Date"), by and between SUNPOINT PUBLIC SCHOOLS COOPERATIVE, an Illinois intergovernmental cooperative of eleven school districts with principal offices at 800 South Plum Grove Road, Palatine, Illinois 60067 ("Cooperative"), and MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF ILLINOIS, LLC, an Illinois limited liability company with principal offices at 1455 Busse Road, Elk Grove Village, Illinois 60007 ("Contractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Cooperative issued Request for Proposal SPSC-2021-07 ("RFP") seeking qualified contractors to provide HVAC, boiler, and plumbing maintenance and repair services at the facilities of its member school districts; and

WHEREAS, Contractor submitted a proposal in response to the RFP and was selected for award following a comprehensive evaluation process conducted by the Cooperative's Board of Education;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 General Description. Contractor shall provide preventive maintenance, emergency repairs, and routine maintenance services for mechanical systems at the facilities of the Cooperative's member school districts, including but not limited to heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems; boiler and steam systems; domestic hot water systems; plumbing systems; and all associated mechanical equipment and controls. Contractor shall provide these services in a professional, timely, and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards and all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

1.2 Service Territory. Services shall be provided at all facilities operated by member school districts of the Cooperative, located throughout Cook, DuPage, and Lake Counties in Illinois. As of the Effective Date, the Cooperative comprises eleven member school districts, including but not limited to Community Consolidated School District 23 (Antioch), Community Consolidated School District 50 (Elk Grove), Community Consolidated School District 54 (Palatine), Community Unit School District 200 (Wheaton), and others as designated by the Cooperative from time to time.

1.3 Categories of Work. The scope of services encompasses:

   (a) Preventive Maintenance: Scheduled maintenance visits and inspections designed to maximize equipment life, operational efficiency, and reliability; filter replacement; system calibration and adjustment; coil cleaning; lubrication of moving parts; thermostat testing and calibration; refrigerant charge verification; boiler water quality testing and treatment; pressure relief valve inspection; low-water cutoff device inspection; drain pipe cleaning and treatment; backflow prevention device testing; and all other preventive services reasonably necessary to maintain mechanical systems in good working order.

   (b) Emergency Repairs: Twenty-four hour emergency response capability for critical mechanical system failures occurring during nights, weekends, holidays, and other times when normal business services are not available, including system failures that threaten building comfort, safety, or operational continuity.

   (c) Routine Repairs: Non-emergency corrective maintenance addressing identified deficiencies, malfunctions, or component failures; component repair and replacement; system adjustment and optimization; and all work necessary to return mechanical systems to proper operating condition.

   (d) System Modifications: Component and system upgrades, replacements, retrofits, and modifications as specifically authorized by written Task Order from the Cooperative.

1.4 Standards and Compliance. All work shall be performed in compliance with the National Mechanical Code, Illinois Plumbing Code, Illinois HVAC Code, current editions and amendments, manufacturer specifications and installation instructions, all applicable building codes and standards, OSHA requirements, Illinois Occupational Safety and Health Act standards, and all other applicable statutes, regulations, and industry best practices. Contractor warrants that all work will be performed by appropriately licensed, trained, certified, and insured personnel.

1.5 Excluded Services. This Agreement excludes: (a) major capital improvements or new construction; (b) architectural or engineering design services; (c) system performance testing beyond routine operational verification; (d) cleaning or janitorial services not integral to mechanical system maintenance; and (e) any work not specifically authorized through written Task Order.

2. TASK ORDERS AND WORK AUTHORIZATION

2.1 Task Order Requirement. All work shall be authorized exclusively through written Task Orders issued by or on behalf of the Cooperative. No work shall be performed by Contractor and no compensation shall be due unless a Task Order has been issued in advance, except as provided in Section 2.3 for genuine emergency situations.

2.2 Task Order Contents and Issuance. Each Task Order shall include: (a) unique Task Order number and reference to this Master Agreement; (b) detailed description of the work to be performed; (c) specific location or locations where work will be performed; (d) estimated scope of labor hours and material requirements; (e) estimated cost or firm not-to-exceed cost authorization; (f) requested completion date or timeline; (g) any special conditions, constraints, or requirements; (h) name and contact information of the Cooperative's authorized representative; and (i) authorized signature of the Cooperative's Director of Facilities or other authorized designee.

2.3 Emergency Work Authorization. In the event of a genuine emergency mechanical system failure that creates an immediate threat to building safety, health, or core operational needs during times when normal Task Order authorization procedures cannot be met due to time constraints or unavailability of authorized personnel, Contractor may proceed with necessary emergency repairs upon oral telephonic or electronic communication with the Cooperative's designated emergency contact, currently Tom Richardson, Director of Facilities, or an authorized alternate. Contractor shall document the time, date, nature of emergency, and name of person authorizing emergency work. A written Task Order shall be issued by the Cooperative within one business day of completion of emergency work, and the work shall be invoiced in accordance with Section 3.

2.4 Task Order Pricing and Terms. Pricing for all work authorized under Task Orders shall be calculated exclusively using the compensation methodology and rates established in Section 3 of this Agreement. All Task Orders shall incorporate and reference this Master Agreement and shall be deemed subject to all terms and conditions contained herein.

2.5 Changes to Authorized Work. Any change to the scope of work, timeline, budget, or other material aspect of a Task Order must be authorized in writing by the Cooperative through an amended, supplemental, or new Task Order before Contractor proceeds with changed work. Contractor shall not perform work beyond the scope described in the applicable Task Order without written authorization.

3. COMPENSATION AND PRICING

3.1 Labor Compensation. Contractor shall bill for labor services based on the prevailing wage rate established and maintained by the Illinois Department of Labor for the applicable county, trade, and job classification, plus an additional markup of 42 percent. Prevailing wage rates shall be applied in accordance with all updates, amendments, and changes promulgated by the Illinois Department of Labor. Contractor shall provide current prevailing wage rate documentation to the Cooperative upon request and shall update rates automatically without requiring amendment to this Agreement.

3.2 Material and Supply Costs. Contractor shall bill for all materials, supplies, fixtures, and equipment at actual documented cost plus a markup of 15 percent. Actual cost shall be evidenced by manufacturer invoices, supplier receipts, purchase orders, or similar documentation provided with each invoice. Bulk materials purchased for projects shall be invoiced at actual unit cost plus the 15 percent markup applied to each item.

3.3 Invoicing Procedures. Contractor shall submit detailed invoices to the Cooperative within ten business days of completion of work under each Task Order. Each invoice shall include: (a) Task Order number and reference to this Master Agreement; (b) detailed narrative description of work performed; (c) dates and times work was performed; (d) number of labor hours broken down by employee, trade classification, and prevailing wage rate; (e) gross labor charges with markup calculation; (f) itemized list of all materials with individual cost and 15 percent markup; (g) supporting receipts or documentation for all materials; (h) calculation of all applicable charges and markups; and (i) total amount due.

3.4 Payment Terms. The Cooperative shall remit payment to Contractor within thirty days of receipt of a complete, accurate, and properly documented invoice. Payment shall be made via check or electronic funds transfer to the address or account designated by Contractor. Invoices shall be submitted to: Sunpoint Public Schools Cooperative, Attn: Director of Facilities, 800 South Plum Grove Road, Palatine, Illinois 60067.

3.5 Change Order Process. Any work requested that exceeds the scope, timeline, or budget of an existing Task Order, or any change to previously authorized work, shall require a formal Change Order issued by the Cooperative. Contractor shall not proceed with changed work without written Change Order authorization. Costs for changed work shall be calculated using the same labor rate and material markup methodology established in Sections 3.1 and 3.2.

3.6 Equipment and Tools. Contractor shall provide and maintain all equipment, hand tools, power tools, specialized equipment, vehicles, and supplies required to perform work under this Agreement. Contractor shall not bill the Cooperative for general equipment, tools, or vehicles. Specialized equipment rental for specific projects, such as lifts, compressors, or diagnostic equipment, may be invoiced if the rental cost is reasonable and commercially competitive and has been approved in advance through the Task Order authorization.

3.7 Travel and Mileage. Reasonable mileage from Contractor's principal office at 1455 Busse Road, Elk Grove Village, Illinois to job sites within the Cooperative's service territory shall be included within Contractor's labor cost calculations and shall not be separately billed. Contractor shall not charge separate travel fees, mobilization fees, service call fees, or emergency response fees; all work shall be compensated using the standard labor rates and material cost formulas established in this Section 3.

Most Favored Pricing. The Cooperative is entitled to pricing at least as favorable as the most favorable pricing (including any discount, rebate, credit, or waiver or reduction of any surcharge) that Contractor extends to any other public entity or commercial customer in the State of Illinois for comparable Services. Contractor shall certify its compliance with this Section annually, and any breach shall entitle the Cooperative to a refund of the difference for the affected period.

4. PREVAILING WAGE COMPLIANCE

4.1 Statutory Requirement. Contractor acknowledges that the Illinois Prevailing Wage Act, 820 ILCS 130/1 et seq. ("Illinois PWA"), applies to all work performed under this Agreement. This Agreement is subject to and bound by all provisions of the Illinois PWA. Contractor shall comply fully and completely with all requirements of the Illinois PWA, including but not limited to payment of applicable prevailing wage rates to all employees, maintenance of payroll records, and certification of compliance.

4.2 Prevailing Wage Rates and Classifications. Contractor shall pay all employees performing work under this Agreement at least the prevailing wage rates established and updated from time to time by the Illinois Department of Labor for Cook County, Illinois and the other applicable counties within the Cooperative's service territory, for each trade and job classification applicable to each employee's work. Current prevailing wage rates as of the Effective Date are attached as Exhibit B and incorporated herein by reference. As the Illinois Department of Labor establishes updated prevailing wage rates and publishes wage determinations, such updated rates shall apply automatically to all work performed after the effective date of the updated determination without requirement of amendment to this Agreement.

4.3 Fringe Benefits. Contractor and its employees shall participate in fringe benefit programs as required by the Illinois Department of Labor prevailing wage determinations. Fringe benefits include but are not limited to: employer-provided health insurance; pension or retirement contributions; apprenticeship program contributions; training funds; and other benefits specified in the applicable wage determinations. Contractor shall ensure that fringe benefits are properly calculated, withheld, or contributed on behalf of all covered employees.

4.4 Payroll Records and Documentation. Contractor shall maintain detailed and accurate payroll records documenting: (a) full name, address, and Social Security number of each employee; (b) job classification and trade designation; (c) hourly wage rate paid to each employee; (d) hours worked by each employee each week; (e) gross wages paid; (f) all deductions and withholdings; (g) fringe benefit contributions; and (h) dates of work performed. Contractor shall maintain payroll records in accordance with all requirements of the Illinois PWA and shall retain records for the period required by law.

4.5 Quarterly Compliance Certification. Contractor shall certify to the Cooperative quarterly, coinciding with each invoicing period, that all employees have been paid in full compliance with prevailing wage rate requirements and that all fringe benefits have been properly calculated and applied. Certification shall be executed by Contractor's authorized representative and shall be submitted with each invoice. Contractor shall provide copies of relevant payroll records to the Cooperative upon reasonable request.

4.6 Audit and Verification. The Cooperative and the State of Illinois shall have the right to audit Contractor's payroll records and prevailing wage compliance at any time. Contractor shall cooperate fully with all audits and shall provide complete access to payroll records, time records, and related documentation. Contractor shall bear all costs associated with audit preparation and correction of any deficiencies.

4.7 Statutory Mandates and Non-Waiver. Contractor acknowledges that prevailing wage requirements are statutory mandates that cannot be waived, modified, reduced, or circumvented. Any violation of prevailing wage requirements, regardless of whether the violation results from negligence or intentional action, shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement. Material breach shall entitle the Cooperative to immediate termination of this Agreement and pursuit of all available legal remedies, including recovery of penalties and interest as provided by law.

5. TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall be effective on July 1, 2021 and shall continue through June 30, 2024, unless sooner terminated for cause or convenience as provided herein.

5.2 Renewal Options. The Cooperative shall have the option to renew this Agreement for two successive one-year periods, each commencing on July 1 and ending on June 30. The Cooperative may exercise each renewal option by providing written notice to Contractor at least sixty days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Upon timely exercise of a renewal option, this Agreement shall be renewed on the same terms and conditions.

5.3 Termination by Passage of Time. If the Cooperative does not timely exercise an available renewal option, this Agreement shall terminate automatically on the expiration date of the then-current term. Upon such termination, Contractor shall complete all work remaining under existing Task Orders and shall not initiate new work after the scheduled termination date. The Cooperative shall compensate Contractor for all work properly completed through the termination date in accordance with the compensation provisions of Section 3.

5.4 Termination for Convenience. The Cooperative may terminate this Agreement for any reason, with or without cause, by providing written notice to Contractor. If termination for convenience is provided during the initial term or first renewal period, the Cooperative shall provide thirty days' written notice. Upon termination for convenience, Contractor shall cease work and the Cooperative shall pay Contractor for all work properly performed through the termination date.

5.5 Termination for Cause. The Cooperative may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if any of the following occur: (a) Contractor fails to perform material obligations under this Agreement and does not cure such failure within ten business days of receiving written notice specifying the default; (b) Contractor fails to comply with prevailing wage requirements or commits any prevailing wage violation; (c) Contractor fails to maintain insurance and bond coverage as required; (d) Contractor becomes insolvent, dissolves, files for bankruptcy protection, or ceases operations; (e) Contractor engages in illegal conduct or conduct that materially breaches this Agreement; or (f) Contractor's license or certifications required to perform work are suspended or revoked.

5.6 Continuation of Obligations. Termination of this Agreement, whether by expiration, for cause, or for convenience, shall not relieve Contractor of the obligation to complete and properly close out all ongoing work and Task Orders. Contractor shall remain bound by all terms and conditions of this Agreement with respect to work performed prior to termination.

6. INSURANCE AND BONDS

6.1 General Liability Insurance. Contractor shall obtain, maintain, and keep in force comprehensive general liability insurance covering bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury arising out of Contractor's operations and performance of work under this Agreement. Insurance shall have minimum limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 general aggregate. Insurance shall include contractual liability coverage for indemnity obligations under Section 7 of this Agreement.

6.2 Professional Liability Insurance. Contractor shall maintain professional liability insurance (also known as errors and omissions insurance) with minimum limits of not less than $1,000,000 per claim and $2,000,000 aggregate, covering professional errors, omissions, and negligent performance in mechanical system design, installation, maintenance, and repair services.

6.3 Workers' Compensation Insurance. Contractor shall maintain workers' compensation insurance in accordance with all statutory requirements of the State of Illinois for all employees performing work under this Agreement. Contractor shall maintain minimum statutory limits and shall ensure that coverage is maintained continuously throughout the term of this Agreement. Contractor waives any right to recover or obtain reimbursement from the Cooperative for workers' compensation claims and shall maintain such waiver in all insurance policies or by separate endorsement.

6.4 Automobile Liability Insurance. Contractor shall maintain automobile liability insurance covering all vehicles operated by Contractor or Contractor's employees in performing work under this Agreement, with minimum combined single limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence for bodily injury and property damage.

6.5 Insurance Certificates and Endorsements. Prior to commencing any work under this Agreement and no later than July 1, 2021, Contractor shall provide the Cooperative with certificates of insurance evidencing all required coverage. Certificates shall identify the Cooperative as additional insured on all liability policies. All certificates shall include provisions for thirty days' written notice to the Cooperative of cancellation, material modification, or non-renewal of coverage. Contractor shall provide updated certificates annually or upon renewal of policies.

6.6 Insurance Requirements Non-Exclusive. Contractor acknowledges that insurance requirements are minimum requirements and do not limit Contractor's indemnification obligations under Section 7 or Contractor's liability for damages. Contractor shall be responsible for all insurance deductibles and shall not seek reimbursement from the Cooperative.

6.7 Performance Bond. Prior to commencing work, Contractor shall furnish a performance bond issued by a corporate surety authorized to do business in Illinois and approved by the Cooperative. The performance bond shall guarantee faithful, complete, and timely performance of all work under this Agreement and shall be in an amount equal to 10 percent of the estimated annual contract value. The current estimated annual contract value is $450,000 based on historical service patterns and anticipated work volume; the required performance bond amount shall be adjusted annually if service volumes change materially.

6.8 Payment Bond. Contractor shall furnish a payment bond in an amount equal to the performance bond amount, guaranteeing prompt payment of all employees, suppliers, subcontractors, and materialmen.

6.9 Bond Renewal and Maintenance. Contractor shall ensure that all bonds remain in full force and effect throughout the entire term of this Agreement and any renewal periods. Bonds shall be renewed before expiration and shall be in continuous effect without lapse. Contractor shall provide certified copies of renewed bonds to the Cooperative at least ten days before expiration of existing bonds.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

7.1 Contractor Indemnity. Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Cooperative, all member school districts of the Cooperative, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents (collectively "Indemnitees") from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs, arising out of or resulting from: (a) Contractor's performance or failure to perform work under this Agreement; (b) injury to or death of any person or damage to or destruction of any property caused by or arising from Contractor's work, operations, or omissions; (c) any breach, violation, or failure to perform any term, condition, or obligation of this Agreement by Contractor; (d) any violation of applicable federal, state, or local law by Contractor; (e) intellectual property infringement claims, patent claims, or trademark claims related to materials, equipment, or methods used by Contractor; (f) any lien, claim, or encumbrance filed or threatened by employees, suppliers, or subcontractors; (g) Contractor's negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct; or (h) any other cause arising from Contractor's activities under this Agreement.

7.2 Scope and Exceptions. Contractor's indemnity obligation shall apply broadly to all claims and damages arising from any cause, including without limitation ordinary negligence, breach of contract, violation of law, or statutory liability, except to the extent any claim arises solely from the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Indemnitees that is the sole and independent cause of the claim.

7.3 Defense Obligations. Contractor shall be responsible for the costs of investigating, defending, settling, and resolving any claims subject to indemnification, including retention and payment of counsel. The Cooperative shall have the right to participate in defense of any claim at Contractor's sole expense using counsel of the Cooperative's choosing.

7.4 Relationship to Insurance. Contractor's indemnity obligations are independent of and in addition to insurance requirements. The existence of insurance, the limits of insurance, or any waiver of subrogation shall not limit, reduce, or eliminate Contractor's indemnification obligations, which shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement with respect to claims arising from work performed during the term.

10. EXHIBIT A – SERVICE STANDARDS AND PERFORMANCE SPECIFICATIONS

10.1 Response Time Standards. Contractor shall meet the following response time standards for all work:

Emergency Calls (System Inoperative or Critical Failure): Contractor shall respond with qualified on-site personnel within two hours of receiving notice during normal business hours (7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday) and within three hours during evening, after-hours, weekend, and holiday periods. Contractor shall maintain emergency call-in capability 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, 365 days per year.

Non-Emergency Work (Minor Issues or Maintenance): Contractor shall schedule and respond with on-site personnel within one business day of receiving a Task Order authorization for non-emergency work.

Preventive Maintenance Visits: Contractor shall schedule and complete preventive maintenance visits in accordance with mutually agreed-upon facility-specific maintenance calendars and schedules, typically on quarterly, semi-annual, or annual cycles as specified.

10.2 Preventive Maintenance Service Components. Contractor shall provide comprehensive preventive maintenance services including:

HVAC Systems: Monthly or quarterly filter replacement based on facility usage; thermostat inspection, testing, and recalibration; blower motor visual inspection and lubrication; condenser and evaporator coil cleaning; ductwork inspection; refrigerant charge verification; electrical connection inspection and tightening; seasonal start-up procedures; seasonal shut-down procedures; and performance testing.

Boiler Systems: Water quality testing and chemical treatment; pressure relief valve inspection and testing; low-water cutoff device inspection and testing; energy efficiency testing; combustion air analysis; annual hydrostatic pressure test; internal tube and surface cleaning; flue gas temperature verification; and documentation of all services.

Plumbing Systems: Visual inspection for leaks and water intrusion; drain line cleaning and maintenance; water pressure regulation verification; water temperature regulation verification; backflow prevention device testing; shut-off valve inspection and operation; water heater flushing and maintenance; cross-connection testing; and compliance certification.

10.3 Service Documentation. Contractor shall provide detailed written reports for all maintenance visits, repairs, and emergency calls, documenting work performed, parts replaced or installed, operating conditions observed, test results, recommended follow-up maintenance, dates and times of service, and name of technician. Reports shall be provided to the Cooperative within five business days of service completion.

10.4 Work Warranty. Contractor warrants all workmanship and materials for a period of twelve months from the date of completion of each work item. Contractor shall correct any defective or non-conforming workmanship or materials at no additional cost to the Cooperative during the warranty period upon notification.

10.5 Safety Compliance. All work shall be performed in strict compliance with applicable OSHA standards, Illinois Occupational Safety and Health Administration standards, Illinois Public Act 88-0018, and all other applicable safety requirements. All Contractor personnel shall be properly trained, certified, and equipped to perform work safely. Contractor shall maintain all required safety certifications and shall carry all required safety equipment on job sites.
