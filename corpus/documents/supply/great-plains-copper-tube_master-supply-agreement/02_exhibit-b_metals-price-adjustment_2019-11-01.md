Metals Price Adjustment

Adjustment Principles

The Metals Adjustment shall be calculated as follows:

(a) Each Product shall be subject to a per-unit price adjustment (a "Metals Adjustment") for copper.

(b) The Metals Adjustment shall be calculated quarterly based on the variance between the average COMEX HG front-month settlement price for copper during the applicable adjustment period and the Base Metal Price of $2.60 per pound.

Determination of Metals Adjustment

(a) Base Metal Price. The Base Metal Price for copper is $2.60 per pound, established as of the Effective Date of the Master Supply Agreement.

(b) Adjustment Period. The Metals Adjustment shall be calculated on a quarterly basis. For each calendar quarter, the adjustment period shall consist of the three calendar months immediately preceding such calendar quarter. The Metals Adjustment determined for each adjustment period shall apply to all Products shipped during the following calendar quarter.

(c) Current Copper Price. For each adjustment period, Seller shall determine the average of the COMEX HG front-month settlement closing prices for copper. Such average shall be calculated by adding the daily settlement prices for all trading days during the applicable adjustment period and dividing by the number of trading days.

(d) Metals Adjustment Calculation. The Metals Adjustment per pound of copper shall be calculated as follows:

Metals Adjustment per Pound = Current Copper Price – Base Metal Price

Application to Products

(a) Copper Weight Specification. The Metals Adjustment shall be applied to each Product based on the copper content of such Product. The copper weight for each Product shall be expressed in pounds per 100 feet for copper water tube and per unit for wrought copper fittings, as established in Seller's current product specifications.

(b) Per-Unit Adjustment. The per-unit Metals Adjustment for each Product shall be calculated as follows:

Per-Unit Metals Adjustment = (Current Copper Price – Base Metal Price) × Copper Weight per Unit

(c) Price Application. The per-unit Metals Adjustment shall be added to (in the case of a positive adjustment) or subtracted from (in the case of a negative adjustment) the base Contract Price for each Product.

Administration and Notice

(a) Notice of Adjustment. Seller shall provide Buyer written notice of each quarterly Metals Adjustment, which notice shall include: (i) the average COMEX HG front-month settlement price for copper used in the calculation; (ii) the Base Metal Price; (iii) the resulting Metals Adjustment per pound of copper; (iv) the per-unit adjustment for each affected Product; and (v) the effective date of such adjustment.

(b) Invoice Procedures. The Metals Adjustment shall be reflected on Seller's invoices and shall be separately identified thereon for each Product.

(c) Effective Date. Adjustments shall apply to all Products shipped on or after the effective date of the adjustment, regardless of the order date or date of quotation.
