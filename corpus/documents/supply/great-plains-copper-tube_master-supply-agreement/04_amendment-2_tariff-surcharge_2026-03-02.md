AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 2 to Master Supply Agreement (the "Amendment No. 2") is effective as of March 2, 2026 (the "Amendment Date") between Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc., an Oklahoma corporation having a place of business at 4400 Foundry Road, Tulsa, OK 74107 ("Great Plains" or "Seller") and Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation having a place of business at 2150 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Sacramento, CA 95833 ("Meridian" or "Buyer"). Seller and Buyer may be referred to herein as "Party" or "Parties."

WHEREAS, Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. and Buyer are parties to a Master Supply Agreement for copper tubing, copper fittings, and related HVAC components, having an Effective Date of March 2, 2019, and amended on December 15, 2024 (such Master Supply Agreement and amendments thereto, collectively, the "Agreement"); and

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to further amend the Agreement to establish a tariff surcharge mechanism to address supplemental duties imposed under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 and related U.S. trade laws.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants hereinafter set forth, the Parties hereto agree to amend the Agreement as follows:

1. Amendments to the Agreement.

Section 7 of the Agreement is hereby amended to add new subsection (g) as follows:

"(g) Tariff Surcharge. "Tariff Charges" means the supplemental ad valorem duty imposed under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. § 2132), pursuant to Presidential Proclamation 11012 (91 Fed. Reg. 9339 (Feb. 25, 2026)), and any successor, replacement, and/or additional duties imposed on goods imported into the United States under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, or other applicable U.S. trade laws that increase or replace such duty. The rate of the Tariff Charges (the "Tariff Rate") as of March 2, 2026 is 10% (calculated as ad valorem).

Notwithstanding anything set forth in this Agreement, if during the remainder of the Term the Tariff Rate imposed by the United States government on Products containing imported Copper Cathode or Fittings Components increases to above 5%, Seller will invoice Buyer for an amount equal to:

the aggregate value of Products containing imported Copper Cathode or Fittings Components ordered in the applicable billing period × (the then-effective Tariff Rate – 5%) × 1.

Seller will provide Buyer with reasonably prompt written notice (email will suffice) specifying the date Seller begins paying such additional Tariff Charges and the specific Product(s) that will be impacted by the additional Tariff Charges. This incremental Tariff Charges described above shall begin thirty days after Seller begins paying such Tariff Charges at the rate over 5% for the Products (or components of the Products) under this Agreement, on a non-retroactive basis. Buyer will pay such invoices in accordance with Section 8.

For illustrative purposes only with hypothetical Tariff Rate and then-current Product price, if the then-current Tariff Rate in connection with Products at the point of import into the United States rises to 15% and if the then-current price for a Standard Copper Fitting Kit (Model SCP-500) ordered pursuant to this Agreement is $38.00, then thirty (30) days after Seller begins paying the additional Tariff Charges for such Copper Fitting Kit, Seller would invoice Buyer for and Buyer would pay a 5% surcharge assessed against $38.00, resulting in $1.90 surcharge per Copper Fitting Kit.

If after such incremental Tariff Charges are collected Seller later receives a rebate, refund or any other form of remuneration back to Seller for such payment(s), Seller will promptly notify Buyer, and Buyer will be entitled to their pro-rata share of whatever Tariff Charges Seller passed on to Buyer as set forth hereinabove during the period covered by the rebate, refund or other remuneration.

Seller will not pass through Tariff Charges to Buyer other than in accordance with the terms set forth hereinabove. If the Tariff Charges increase to a level that materially impacts Seller, the Parties agree to revisit these terms."

2. Entire Agreement. Except as expressly stated herein, this Amendment No. 2 does not alter any term or condition of the Agreement. This Amendment No. 2 represents the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, communications, agreements, and understandings of any kind and nature between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof. All capitalized terms not defined in this Amendment No. 2 shall have the respective definition set forth in the Agreement.

3. Reference to Agreement. On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement", "hereunder", or words of like import referring to the Agreement shall mean and be a reference to the Agreement as modified by this Amendment No. 2.

4. Governing Law. This Amendment No. 2 and performance by the Parties hereunder shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, U.S.A., without regard to provisions on the conflicts of laws.

5. Counterparts. This Amendment No. 2 may be executed in one or more counterparts, and each of which shall be deemed to be an original, and all of which shall constitute one and the same instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereto acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions of this Amendment No. 2 and have caused this Amendment No. 2 to be executed by their respective duly authorized representatives to be effective as of the Amendment Date.
