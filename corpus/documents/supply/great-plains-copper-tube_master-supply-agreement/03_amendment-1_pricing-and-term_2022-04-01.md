Amendment No. 1 to Master Supply Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 is made pursuant to and amends the Master Supply Agreement dated January 1, 2020, as subsequently amended, ("Agreement") between Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. ("Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc.") and Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. ("Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc.") (collectively, the "Parties"). For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree to amend the Agreement as follows:

1. Term. This Amendment No. 1 shall take effect as of April 1, 2022 ("Effective Date"), and terminate, along with the Agreement, on October 31, 2027 ("Amendment No. 1 Term"). Any pricing adjustments resulting from the execution of this Agreement shall be applied retroactively to the Effective Date.

2. Agreement Amendment. The following provisions of the Agreement are deleted in their entirety and have no effect:

• The first sentence of Paragraph 5(a) (pricing of copper tube and fitting products);

• Paragraph 11 (quarterly metals adjustment);

• The second sentence of Paragraph 16(a) (auto renewal);

• The prior pricing column in Exhibit A (prior base pricing); and

• Exhibit F (supplier incentive schedule).

3. Price. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Agreement, any Amendment, or the Annual Binding Order, the Price for copper tube and fitting products delivered during the Amendment No. 1 Term shall be the firm, fixed prices set forth in the attached Exhibit A-1, provided that $0.08 per pound materials adjustment shall be added to the Price for any copper tube product ordered for delivery during the Amendment No. 1 Term based on monthly market conditions. Except as set forth in Paragraph 4 below, no further adjustments shall be made to the Price, including without limitation a Metals Price Adjustment or supplier incentive. Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. shall pay the Annual Volume Commitment on or before December 31, 2022 and the last day of December of each subsequent year of this Amendment No. 1 Term, provided that the Annual Volume Commitment shall be reduced or refunded on a pro rata basis for every pound of copper tube ordered in excess of 1,150,000 lbs annually.

4. Additional Orders. The Metals Price Adjustment set forth in Exhibits E-1 and E-2, revised as set forth below, shall apply to the Price for any Additional Orders placed by Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. for delivery during the Amendment No. 1 Term. The following revisions to the Metals Price Adjustment shall apply to any Additional Orders, calculated on a monthly basis effective the first business day of each calendar month:

• No adjustment shall be made for the price of secondary metals or alloys.

• The base copper price shall be $4.75 per pound.

• The market price shall be measured using the following per pound market price as of close of business on the last business day of the preceding calendar month:

• Copper – Copper High Grade (Globex) (COMEX) Month-End Settlement Price

5. Annual Binding Order. Notwithstanding anything in the Agreement to the contrary other than Paragraph 2(b), specifically including without limitation Paragraph 7(d), Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. may revise the entire delivery schedule in the Annual Binding Order (including without limitation delivery quantities for each month April 2022 through October 2027) so long as Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. does not decrease its total annual quantity ordered during any calendar year below 1,150,000 lbs of copper tube. The revised delivery schedule is attached as Exhibit H.

6. Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment. In addition to all other obligations under the Agreement, Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. commits to purchase a minimum of 1,150,000 lbs of copper tube and fittings annually during each year of the Amendment No. 1 Term. If Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. fails to achieve this minimum purchase commitment in any calendar year, Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. shall pay Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. a shortfall fee of $0.18 per pound for each pound of copper tube not purchased below the 1,150,000 lbs minimum. Such shortfall fees shall be calculated and invoiced by December 31 of the following calendar year and shall be due within thirty (30) days of invoice.

7. Limited Exclusivity Relief—Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. Notwithstanding anything in the Agreement to the contrary, on a noninterference basis with Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc.'s orders, Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. may sell copper tube and fitting products to other customers during the Amendment No. 1 Term. For the avoidance of doubt, better terms shall be inclusive of all other terms directly and indirectly affecting price (e.g., shipping terms, rebates, discounts, credit terms).

8. Limited Exclusivity Relief—Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc. The limitations on Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc.'s sourcing of alternative copper tube and fitting products, as contained in Paragraphs 4 and 10(a) of the Agreement, are eliminated. For the avoidance of doubt, the limitations on Meridian Mechanical Group, Inc.'s sourcing of specialty products remain unchanged.

9. Other Agreements. This Amendment No. 1 is contingent upon, and not effective unless and until, both Parties agree upon and execute a copper tube supply agreement covering the periods November 1, 2027 through October 31, 2032 ("Follow-on Agreement"). If the Follow-On Agreement is not agreed upon and executed by both Parties by August 31, 2027, this Amendment No. 1 shall have no effect. Unless specifically changed by this Amendment No. 1, all other terms and conditions of the Agreement (including without limitation any open Binding Orders and contract orders) remain unchanged. This Amendment No. 1, the Agreement, the Annual Binding Order as amended, the Follow-on Agreement, and any nondisclosure agreements executed in connection herewith constitute the entire understanding and agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof, and supersede all prior or contemporaneous discussions, agreements and understandings, both written and oral, among the Parties with respect thereto.

10. Release. For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which is hereby acknowledged, both Parties hereby release, acquit and forever discharge and hold harmless the other, including the indemnified Party's past and present parents, affiliates and subsidiaries (and the officers, directors and employees of each), from and against all existing claims (other than for product warranty and defects, and requirements pursuant to separate supply arrangements), known and unknown, as of the Effective Date arising out of the sale by Great Plains Copper Tube, Inc. of copper tube and fitting products supplied pursuant to prior amendments to the Agreement ("Release").

11. Amendment. Any amendment or modification of this Amendment No. 1 shall be void unless set forth in a writing signed by each of the Parties.
