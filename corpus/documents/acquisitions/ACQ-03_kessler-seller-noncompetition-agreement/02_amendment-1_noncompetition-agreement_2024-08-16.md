NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION AGREEMENT
AMENDMENT NO. 1

This NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION AGREEMENT AMENDMENT NO. 1 (this "Amendment") is entered into as of August 16, 2024, by and among MERIDIAN MECHANICAL OF ILLINOIS, LLC, an Illinois limited liability company (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "Meridian Mechanical of Illinois, LLC"), KESSLER COMFORT SERVICES, INC., an Illinois corporation (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "Kessler Comfort Services, Inc."), KESSLER REFRIGERATION, LLC, an Illinois limited liability company (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "Kessler Refrigeration, LLC"), and KARL KESSLER, an individual (together with his successors and permitted assigns, "Karl Kessler"). Each of Meridian Mechanical of Illinois, LLC, Kessler Comfort Services, Inc., Kessler Refrigeration, LLC, and Karl Kessler may also be referred to herein individually as a "Party", and collectively as the "Parties".

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Meridian Mechanical of Illinois, LLC, Kessler Comfort Services, Inc., Kessler Refrigeration, LLC, and Karl Kessler are affiliated business entities and individual with common ownership;

WHEREAS, the Parties had entered into a Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation Agreement dated October 3, 2022, by and among the Parties (collectively, the "Non-Competition Agreement") to set out certain restrictive covenants of each Party;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to amend the existing obligations under the Non-Competition Agreement by narrowing the geographic Territory and permitting certain competitive activities;

WHEREAS, the Parties also desire to confirm the continuation of non-solicitation obligations under the Non-Competition Agreement.

AGREEMENT

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, agreements and conditions set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions. Any capitalized term used but not defined in this Amendment will have the meaning set forth for that term in the Non-Competition Agreement.

2. Non-Competition. Section 2 of the Non-Competition Agreement is hereby amended to limit the geographic scope of the Territory to Cook County and DuPage County, Illinois only. Notwithstanding any other provision in Section 2, Karl Kessler is hereby expressly permitted to perform residential boiler service work for customers of Kessler Refrigeration, LLC within the Territory. All other restrictions contained in Section 2 shall continue in full force and effect, mutatis mutandis.

3. Non-Solicitation. Section 4 of the Non-Competition Agreement shall continue in full force and effect as originally written, except that nothing herein shall prohibit Karl Kessler from servicing customers of Kessler Refrigeration, LLC in connection with the performance of residential boiler service within the Territory.

4. Continuation. This Amendment will apply and be effective only with respect to the provisions of the Non-Competition Agreement specifically referred to herein. Except as otherwise set forth in this Amendment, the Non-Competition Agreement will continue in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.

5. Miscellaneous. This Amendment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation Agreement Amendment No. 1 as of the date first written above.
