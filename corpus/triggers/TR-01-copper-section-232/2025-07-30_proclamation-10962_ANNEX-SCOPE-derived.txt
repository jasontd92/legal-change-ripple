Plain-language scope for plumbing/HVAC purchasing:
- COVERED: refined copper tubes and pipes (7411.10: e.g., Type K/L/M water tube, ACR tube); copper-alloy (brass/bronze) tubes (7411.21-7411.29);
  copper and copper-alloy tube or pipe fittings (7412.10 refined copper, e.g., wrot elbows, tees, couplings; 7412.20 copper alloy, e.g., brass press fittings);
  copper household articles and sanitary ware (7418.10, 7418.20); copper wire, plate, sheet, strip, foil, bars and rods; certain insulated copper conductors (8544.42, 8544.49).
- NOT LISTED: refined copper cathode and unwrought copper (7403); copper ores, concentrates, mattes, cement copper; copper scrap (7404);
  valves and taps (8481, e.g., brass ball valves); water heaters (8419); air conditioners (8415); heat pumps and refrigerating equipment (8418).
- The Proclamation directs a Commerce update by June 30, 2026 on whether to impose a phased duty on refined copper (15% from January 1, 2027; 30% from January 1, 2028).
  As of 2026-09-29 no proclamation imposing that duty has been published in the Federal Register.
