NEW SECTION.   Sec. 1.  (1) The legislature finds that 7
noncompetition covenants hinder innovation and entrepreneurship, 8
suppress wages, reduce job mobility, and ultimately harm consumers 9
and the economy. In 2019 the legislature took a critical step forward 10
by banning the use of noncompetition covenants for lower-wage 11
earners. This did not go far enough. Research shows that 12
noncompetition covenants restrict workers' mobility, impede efforts 13
to correct inequities, and significantly suppress workers' wages 14
across all sectors, including for those not subject to covenants, or 15
subject to covenants that are unenforceable under state law. Even 16
among supposed high-wage earners, the suggestion that these covenants 17
are bargained is largely a legal fiction. Noncompetition covenants 18
are often unilaterally imposed by employers and businesses without 19
meaningful negotiation. Businesses have more specific and effective 20
